Chapter 24 – Bran

Bran, bitter and housebound, hears Old Nan's tale of the Others, is brought to the hall where a clash with Tyrion Lannister is eased by a saddle design for riding, and then hears from Night's Watch men that Benjen is missing.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – POV; 8 (name day passed), crippled and paralyzed from the waist down; angry, grieving, and resentful of Old Nan's stories, then drawn into her tale of the Others. Remembers nothing of his fall or climb (Maester Luwin tells Tyrion this); never believes he fell. Misses his parents; thinks everyone left him. Hopes for a horse; recalls the crow in his dream that "lied" about flying. Dreams of climbing an ancient tower and being stalked by red-eyed stone gargoyles whispering, "I didn't hear." Direwolf Summer – silver-and-smoke, gold eyes, smaller and warier than Grey Wind, the smartest of the litter; obeys Bran's call.
  • Robb Stark – 14, "Robb the Lord", acting lord of Winterfell while his parents are away; wears ringmail and a real sword, never smiles, drills the guard, studies accounts with Maester Luwin, rides out to holdfasts with Hallis Mollen; closer to Theon and Hallis than to his brothers lately. Greeted Tyrion with a bare sword and harsh words ("I am not your boy"), then softened and offered hospitality (refused). Refuses to believe Benjen is dead; weeps in the dark and promises Bran a horse and a ride to the Wall. Direwolf Grey Wind.
  • Rickon Stark – small ("baby"), runs with the wolves, cries when Robb is gone, can't read. Direwolf Shaggydog – black, green eyes, fierce.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Hand of the King, in King's Landing with Sansa and Arya. Distrusts the Lannisters: Lysa's letter says Jon Arryn was murdered by them; Catelyn brought him the Valyrian-steel dagger of Bran's would-be killer, which Littlefinger says belonged to Tyrion. Killed Sansa's direwolf Lady himself rather than let Ilyn Payne do it. Has told Catelyn to go home and have Moat Cailin and White Harbor fortified, and is quietly watching Theon Greyjoy.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; sailed secretly to King's Landing (two fingers permanently stiff from the assassin's blade), met Ned in a brothel and was told to return to Winterfell. Robb says she will be home soon. Suspects Bran was thrown from the tower (she noted Jaime skipped the hunt); told only Robb, Luwin, Rodrik and Theon.
  • Sansa Stark – 11, betrothed to Joffrey; falsely claimed not to remember the fight with Joffrey; lost her direwolf Lady (killed on the queen's demand); blames Arya. Arya Stark – 9, wilful; her wolf Nymeria bit Joffrey and was driven off into the wild (Arya threw rocks at her to make her go); her friend Mycah was killed by the Hound; carries the sword Needle (Jon's gift, Ned let her keep it) and is learning "water dancing" from Syrio Forel.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14 (now 15), at Castle Black as a recruit; nicknamed "Lord Snow" by Ser Alliser Thorne; beats other recruits in drills, was rebuked by Donal Noye for bullying, now helps Grenn and Pyp; befriended Tyrion; says that he and Ghost will look for Benjen if he does not return. He asked Tyrion to help Bran.
  • Benjen Stark – First Ranger of the Night's Watch; missing on a ranging while searching for Ser Waymar Royce. Brandon (the first Brandon Stark was Old Nan's baby charge; also Ned's late brother killed by the Mad King), Brandon the Builder (ancient legend), Rickard, Lyanna (dead). Wolves: Ghost (Jon's), Lady dead, Nymeria gone.
Winterfell household
  • Old Nan – oldest person in Winterfell, near blind, wrinkled; Hodor's great-grandmother; wet nurse to an old Brandon Stark; tells stories; lost sons in Robert's war and a grandson at Pyke in Balon's rebellion; mixes up all the Brandons.
  • Hodor (real name Walder) – huge, nearly seven feet, simple stableboy who only says "Hodor"; gently carries Bran.
  • Maester Luwin – advisor; sent ravens to Ned, Catelyn and Jon; took Tyrion's saddle drawing and thinks it will work.
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward, stays at Robb's side; laughs at Bran and Tyrion; says the wolves don't like Tyrion's smell. Hallis Mollen – guard captain with Robb.
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel (master-at-arms; shaved his whiskers; went with Catelyn to King's Landing), Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Vayon Poole (Ned's steward), Hullen, Harwin, Fat Tom (gone south with Ned), Septa Mordane and Jeyne Poole (with the girls), Syrio Forel (Braavosi sword master, ex-first sword to the Sealord), Mikken (smith), Septon Chayle (library). Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, at the Eyrie; her coded letter says the Lannisters (the queen) murdered her husband Jon Arryn (dead). Robert Arryn – her sickly young son. Edmure (Catelyn's brother), Brynden (Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate), Hoster Tully (her father).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, one black and one green eye; arrived at Winterfell with the Night's Watch men; brought word from Jon; designed a special saddle for a crippled rider to be used with a trained yearling, gave the drawing to Maester Luwin, says he has "a tender spot for cripples and bastards and broken things". Asked Bran how he fell and found his amnesia "curious". Nearly attacked by the direwolves (they shredded a sleeve); refused Winterfell's hospitality and stays at the winter-town inn; leaves south at daybreak with his men and Yoren.
  • King Robert Baratheon – fat, hard-drinking; in King's Landing; ordered a great tourney in honour of Ned as Hand (the Crown is six million gold dragons in debt); allowed Lady's killing but disliked it. Queen Cersei – demanded the direwolf's death after Joffrey was mauled; Lysa's letter accuses her of Jon Arryn's murder. Prince Joffrey – 12, lied about the fight; bitten by Nymeria. Tommen, Myrcella – the younger royal children.
  • Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin, Kingsguard, killed the Mad King; Robert has promised him the Wardenship of the East (Ned objected). Sandor "the Hound" Clegane – Joffrey's burned bodyguard; killed Mycah. Ser Ilyn Payne – tongueless king's justice (headsman). Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard. Ser Boros, Ser Meryn – Kingsguard.
  • Small council: Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – master of coin, small, clever, a Tully ward who once loved Catelyn; owns a brothel where Ned and Catelyn met; claims the assassin's dagger was his, won by Tyrion in a bet; has offered Ned help. Lord Varys – eunuch master of whisperers, perfumed, spies through "little birds". Grand Maester Pycelle. Renly Baratheon – the king's youngest brother, handsome, lighthearted. Stannis Baratheon – the king's other brother, gone to Dragonstone.
  • Tywin Lannister (Tyrion's father, Warden of the West), Balon Greyjoy (his rebellion; Pyke), Mad King Aerys II.
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren – black brother, senior of the four who came with Tyrion; stooped, sour-smelling, blunt; recruits for the Watch; calls Jon "Ser Alliser's bane"; tells Robb and Bran that Benjen Stark has not returned from searching for Ser Waymar Royce and is "most like dead". The youngest brother says Benjen knows the forest best and will return.
  • Jeor Mormont – the "Old Bear", Lord Commander, with a talking raven; says the Watch is below a thousand men and begged Tyrion to tell the king. Maester Aemon (ancient, blind). Ser Alliser Thorne – bitter master-at-arms, hostile to Jon. Bowen Marsh (Lord Steward), Ser Jaremy Rykker, Donal Noye (one-armed armorer). Recruits Grenn, Pyp, Todder ("Toad"), Halder, Jeren. Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others in the prologue; Castle Black thinks him missing), Gared (executed).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers) – in Old Nan's tale, cold dead things that hate iron, fire and sunlight and hunt the living riding dead horses with hosts of the slain and giant spiders; came in a generation-long winter thousands of years ago. A last hero sought the children of the forest (long gone; only the faces in the trees remain, per Maester Luwin). Mance Rayder, wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo; riding with the khalasar toward Vaes Dothrak; owns three dragon eggs), Viserys (her brother, humiliated by Dany and made to walk), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile, now Dany's adviser), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, riders Jhogo, Quaro, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.
