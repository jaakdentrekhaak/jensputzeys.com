Chapter 49 – Eddard

After King Robert dies, Ned convenes the small council, is summoned to the throne room where Joffrey claims the crown, and when he names Stannis the true heir and tries to arrest Cersei, the City Watch turns on his men and Littlefinger holds Ned's own dagger at his throat.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; leg still in plaster. Robert's letter named him Protector of the Realm, but Cersei tore it up. Told the court Joffrey has no claim and that Stannis is Robert's true heir, then ordered the queen and her children confined. Betrayed in the throne room: his guards are cut down and Littlefinger now has Ned's dagger under his chin. Had planned to hide the real issue until his daughters were safe and Stannis arrived.
  • Sansa Stark – elder daughter, tearful because Ned will not let her say farewell to Joffrey; stormed out of breakfast. Arya Stark – younger daughter, packed, off to a last lesson with Syrio; both due to sail at evening on the Wind Witch (Ned wanted them ready by midday). Septa Mordane – their septa, tells Sansa to obey.
  • Tomard "Fat Tom" – killed by a gold cloak's spear in the throne room (Ned's loyal household-guard commander). Varly – guardsman, killed (throat cut by Janos Slynt). Cayn – guardsman, killed by the Hound after fighting hard. Desmond, Porther, Alyn (rode out with Lord Beric), Harwin – other Stark men; some guardsmen stood with Ned in the throne room. Dead earlier: Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl. Vayon Poole – Winterfell's steward, arranged the ship.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon, Jon Snow (now a sworn brother of the Watch); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; dead Lyanna, Brandon. Ser Rodrik Cassel. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead of the boar wound, mourned by Ned as a friend; his will named Ned regent.
  • Joffrey (styled "of the Houses Baratheon and Lannister") – about twelve, golden-haired; sits the Iron Throne proclaiming himself king, wants to be crowned within a fortnight, called Ned a liar and screamed for his enemies' deaths. Ned knows he is Jaime's son, not Robert's. Queen Cersei Lannister – ripped up the will and demanded Ned kneel to Joffrey; ordered Barristan to arrest him; calls him a traitor. Myrcella – her young daughter, confused by Ned's claim; Tommen – her younger son (present with the queen).
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; bought the City Watch for Ned as promised, then took Ned's Valyrian dagger and pinned him ("I did warn you not to trust me"). Janos Slynt – Commander of the City Watch (gold cloaks), in black-and-gold plate; his men killed Ned's guards (he cut Varly down himself). Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers, perfumed and powdered; reported Renly's flight. Grand Maester Pycelle – told Ned of Robert's death. Ser Barristan Selmy – white-haired Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, shocked when Cersei tore up the king's words, hesitated to seize Ned and was surrounded by Stark guards.
  • Kingsguard: five white-armored knights ring the throne (all but Jaime and Barristan), including Ser Boros Blount and Ser Meryn Trant, who stand behind the queen; other Kingsguard named earlier are Ser Preston Greenfield and Ser Arys Oakheart. Ser Balon Swann – knight from the hunt party. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's sworn shield, killed Cayn. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin lover, fled the city for his father. Lord Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock. Lancel – Robert's elder squire. Lord Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother; left the city before dawn through a postern gate with Ser Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers) and about fifty men, heading south. Lord Stannis Baratheon – elder brother at Dragonstone, whom Ned has written to; now named by Ned as Robert's heir. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – Sandor's huge brother, sentenced for massacres. Lord Beric Dondarrion, Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar – sent against him. Ser Ilyn Payne – mute King's Justice; Ser Robar Royce (found the wounded king) and father Bronze Yohn Royce; Barra, Mya, Gendry (Robert's bastards); Jon Arryn (dead Hand); Yoren (Watch recruiter).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, defending Riverrun; Hoster Tully – their father; Ser Raymun Darry, Ser Karyl Vance, Ser Marq Piper – riverlands knights; Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; Robert Arryn – her sickly boy lord; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the hill clan Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, newly sworn brother, steward to the Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly – fat, bookish friend, now Maester Aemon's assistant; Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Maester Aemon (blind, aged) and his aide Clydas; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Bowen Marsh – Lord Steward; Othell Yarwyck – First Builder; Ser Jaremy Rykker – commands the rangers; Septon Celladar; Dywen – old forester; new brothers Grenn, Pyp, Toad, Matthar (rangers), Haider, Albett (builders), Dareon (singer, steward, sent to Eastwatch); Chett (steward moved to the kennels), Cotter Pyke (Eastwatch commander); Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing. A severed hand was found beyond the Wall.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak, carrying Rhaego; Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo; Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord, with bloodriders Qotho, Haggo, Cohollo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising Dany; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses; Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo; Khals Jommo and Ogo.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master teaching Arya. Captain Qos – master of the Wind Witch. Grand Maester Malleon, Lady Ashara (mentioned), Rhaegar's children (killed long ago).

Places

Places
  • The throne room (Red Keep) – great hall with the Iron Throne, oak doors twenty feet high, tapestries of hunts and battles; where Joffrey was proclaimed king and Ned was betrayed. The Iron Throne – barbed seat Ned once forced Jaime off. The Tower of the Hand – Ned's quarters. The council chambers, Maegor's Holdfast (royal apartments), the Red Keep / King's Landing, the godswood, the kingswood, the Blackwater.
  • Storm's End (Baratheon seat) and Highgarden (Tyrell seat) – where Renly is thought to be headed. Dragonstone – Stannis's seat. Winterfell, Braavos, Casterly Rock, the Eyrie, Riverrun, Castle Black / the Wall, Vaes Dothrak.
