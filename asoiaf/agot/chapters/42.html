Chapter 42 – Tyrion

Freed Tyrion and Bronn camp in the mountains of the Vale, Tyrion reveals his bitter past and the sellsword's price, and when hill clansmen surround them he offers the Stone Crows "the Vale of Arryn."

Characters

House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" / "halfman" – dwarf, Tywin's youngest son; freed from the Eyrie after Bronn won the trial by combat and sent onto the high road toward the Trident with weapons, horses and supplies. Quick-witted, bitterly funny, gambles on people ("tossed the dice"); "a Lannister always pays his debts" (gave Mord the gaoler a purse of gold, promising more at Casterly Rock). Denies owning the dagger of the assassination plot and means to find out the truth at Casterly Rock or King's Landing. At thirteen he married a girl he took for a crofter's daughter; it was a setup by his brother Jaime, a hired whore, and Tywin made the guards use her and Tyrion go last, paying her in gold. Now bargains with the Stone Crows.
  • Bronn – sellsword, lean, dark, with a gap in his smile where Ser Vardis cracked a tooth; killed his wounded partner Chiggen and lied to Catelyn about it. Now Tyrion's hired sword: "I fought for you, but I do not love you"; no man's friend; wants gold, land, women.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tyrion's golden twin brother, Kingsguard knight; fled King's Landing and is massing a host at Casterly Rock. Tywin Lannister – their cold, harsh father, lord of Casterly Rock. Queen Cersei Lannister – their sister, Robert's queen. Morrec – Tyrion's servant who made his fires (mentioned).
The mountain clans (Stone Crows)
  • Gunthor son of Gurn 🆕 new this chapter – thin chief of the Stone Crows, a hill clan; horned helm, long knife; says the mothers go hungry and steel fills more mouths than gold.
  • Shagga son of Dolf 🆕 new this chapter – huge, slow, loud clansman in skins with club and axe; laughs readily; threatens to feed Tyrion's manhood to the goats.
  • Conn 🆕 new this chapter, Torrek 🆕 new this chapter, Jaggot 🆕 new this chapter (fire-hardened spear) – other Stone Crows; ten or more, poorly armed (pitchforks, scythes, wooden spears), who surround the camp and mean to take horses, gear and lives.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell and Hand of the King, bedridden at the capital with a broken leg after the fight with Jaime's men. Tyrion calls him proud, honorable and honest. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, Tyrion's captor at the Eyrie, planning to return to Winterfell; "worse" than Ned in stern honor. Robb (fifteen, ruling Winterfell), Bran (eight, crippled), Rickon, Sansa, Arya (silent since the attack), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (missing). Ser Rodrik Cassel, Maester Luwin, Theon Greyjoy, Osha (captive wildling) – Winterfell household. Alyn (acting captain of Ned's guard), Vayon Poole (steward), Jory Cassel (dead).
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, tired king; wants peace with the Lannisters. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin. Joffrey, Ser Meryn Trant, Grand Maester Pycelle, Varys, Renly, Stannis, Ser Barristan Selmy, Loras Tyrell, Sandor and Gregor Clegane, Barra (Robert's baby bastard).
House Arryn / Tully / the Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; freed Tyrion reluctantly, hoping the road would kill him. Robert Arryn – sickly boy lord. Ser Lyn Corbray – Lysa's suitor, stone-faced, who ushered Tyrion out of the Bloody Gate. Mord – cruel gaoler, bribed with gold. Ser Vardis Egen – dead champion. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle), Hoster Tully (Riverrun), Edmure Tully (defending Riverrun); Jon Arryn (dead Hand).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly (fat, bookish recruit Jon asked Aemon to help), Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder (recruits passed to the Lord Commander), Ser Alliser Thorne (harsh master-at-arms), Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (blind old maester), Chett, Clydas, Yoren, Benjen (missing).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo, owns a dragon egg. Viserys – her cruel brother, "the dragon". Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; plus bloodriders, handmaids (Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah).

Places

Places
  • The mountains of the Vale 🆕 new this chapter – wild highlands along the high road below the Eyrie, hunting ground of hill clans like the Stone Crows; shadowcats roam. The high road – dangerous route toward the Trident and riverlands. The Bloody Gate – the Vale's gate, where Tyrion was released.
  • The Eyrie, the Vale of Arryn, the Trident, Riverrun, Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Lannisport (town beneath it), King's Landing / Red Keep, Winterfell, Castle Black / the Wall.
