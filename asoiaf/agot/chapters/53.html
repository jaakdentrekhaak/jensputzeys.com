Chapter 53 – Bran

As the northern lords gather at Winterfell, Robb overawes his bannermen and marches south with his army, while Bran, left behind as lord of the castle, prays in the godswood and hears a wildling woman's warnings.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – Ned and Catelyn's crippled second son; carried about in a wicker seat on Hodor's back; dreams, prays at the heart tree and "talks with the gods"; hurt by being called broken, wants to learn magic ("the crow promised I would fly"). Now left as lord in Winterfell in Robb's stead, scared and feeling like "Bran the Broken". Has a direwolf, Summer; his pony/horse is Dancer.
  • Robb Stark – Ned's eldest son, Lord of Winterfell in his father's absence, not yet sixteen; has turned cool and commanding, though shaken in private. Quelled the Greatjon by having Grey Wind bite off his fingers. Has marched south with about twelve thousand men to hold the Neck and aid the Tullys against the Lannisters, vowing to bring Ned home. Direwolf Grey Wind.
  • Rickon Stark – youngest Stark, near four, wild with grief at Robb's departure; refused to say farewell; hid in the crypts with a rusty sword and his direwolf Shaggydog, now chained in the kennels.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; rumors say he is a prisoner, fugitive or dead; Sansa's letter says he conspired treason with the king's brothers. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, away in the south (Robb expects her home soon). Sansa Stark – wrote the raven letter (on Ned's seal) urging fealty to Joffrey and saying she will plead for Ned; Robb is angry she says nothing of Arya. Arya Stark – younger daughter, unaccounted for. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall. Dead: Lady (Sansa's direwolf, her bones returned and buried in the old lichyard under the First Keep), old Lord Rickard Stark (their grandfather) and Brandon, Ned's brother, who went south with two hundred men and never returned.
  • Winterfell household: Maester Luwin – counselor and tutor, taught Bran the banners and built the wicker seat; Hodor – huge, simple stableboy who says only "Hodor", carries Bran; Hallis Mollen (also "Hal") – guard captain, carries Robb's banner; Ser Rodrik Cassel – went south with Catelyn and has not returned; Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward, rides with Robb and teases him; Old Nan – storyteller; Mikken – smith; Gage – cook, who lets Osha pray at the godswood; Farlen – kennelmaster; Quent – one of Robb's men; Vayon Poole (steward, in King's Landing). Jory Cassel, Hullen and Fat Tom went south with Ned (Bran does not know their fate).
  • Osha – wildling woman captured in the wolfswood, now shackled at the ankles and working in the kitchens; born beyond the Wall of the Free Folk. Warns that cold winds are rising, men vanish and return as wights with blue eyes and black hands, that the old gods have no power in the south, and that Robb should march north, not south.
Northern bannermen
  • Lord Rickard Karstark 🆕 new this chapter of Karhold – led three hundred horse and about two thousand foot, last to arrive; banner a white sunburst on black; sons Harrion 🆕 new this chapter (eldest), Eddard 🆕 new this chapter and Torrhen 🆕 new this chapter, the latter two scornful of Bran.
  • The Greatjon (Lord Umber) 🆕 new this chapter – giant, loud lord whose sigil is a roaring giant in shattered chains; defied Robb, lost two fingers to Grey Wind, then became Robb's loudest champion.
  • Roose Bolton (lord of the Dreadfort, flayed-man sigil) – silent, unsettling, demanded battle command; Robb fears the tale that Boltons hang their enemies' skins. Robett Glover (silver mailed fist on red). Maege Mormont – stout grey-haired lady (black bear sigil) in mail, offers her granddaughter in marriage. Lord Cerwyn 🆕 new this chapter (battle-axe) – brought a plain daughter of thirty to cook for Robb. Lord Hornwood 🆕 new this chapter (bull moose) – offers gifts and wants favors. Tallharts (three sentinel trees). Lords Manderly and Flint 🆕 new this chapter, barrow knights and crannogmen – waiting along the kingsroad. A Bolton man knifed a Cerwyn man at the Smoking Log.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead; some rumors say Ned killed him. Joffrey – to be crowned; summons Catelyn and Robb to swear fealty. Queen Cersei Lannister; Prince Tommen (Bran once knocked him down with a wooden sword). Grand Maester Pycelle, Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger", Varys, Janos Slynt, Ser Barristan Selmy, Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (rumored to have killed Arya and Sansa), Ser Jaime Lannister, Lord Tywin Lannister (rumored to march on the Eyrie), Lord Renly Baratheon, Lord Stannis Baratheon.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, with her sickly son Robert Arryn at the Eyrie; Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father, lord of Riverrun; Ser Edmure Tully – her brother (rumor says Catelyn hung Tyrion from Riverrun's walls); Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – her uncle. Fighting has begun in the riverlands.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from Catelyn, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn among the hill clan Stone Crows (Bran does not know).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander, at the Wall (Bran does not know he was confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a walking corpse); Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly; Maester Aemon; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Ser Jaremy Rykker – First Ranger in Benjen's absence; Bowen Marsh; Donal Noye – armorer; Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Dywen, Chett; Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, missing First Ranger.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • Mance Rayder – "King-beyond-the-Wall", a former black brother who came down from the Shadow Tower (per Osha), preparing to fight; wildlings (Osha's former companions Stiv and Hali); giants – Osha says they exist beyond the Wall (her brother killed one), Maester Luwin says they are dead; the Others/white walkers, children of the forest.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak; Khal Drogo – her Dothraki husband; Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. (A drunken tale says Rhaegar Targaryen returned; Rhaegar is long dead.)
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master; Jeyne Poole; Septa Mordane.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat, full to bursting with armies and a crowded winter town; two walls with a moat and drawbridge, armory, kennels, the godswood (heart tree with carved red eyes, hot-spring pools beside the Guest House), the Great Hall, the crypts, the old lichyard under the First Keep, the Smoking Log (a tavern in the winter town).
  • Karhold 🆕 new this chapter – Karstark castle. The Dreadfort 🆕 new this chapter – Bolton seat, rumored to hold a room of flayed skins. The Neck, Riverrun, the riverlands – where Robb marches. The wolfswood, the Shadow Tower, the Wall, Castle Black, the Citadel at Oldtown (where maesters study).
  • King's Landing / the Red Keep, Dragonstone, the Eyrie, the Vale, Vaes Dothrak, the kingsroad.
