Chapter 1 – Bran

Bran goes with his father and brothers to watch Lord Stark behead a Night's Watch deserter, then on the way home they find a dead direwolf and her six pups.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark 🆕 new this chapter – Lord of Winterfell and Warden of the North, 35, grey-eyed, beard going white. Swings the sword himself (greatsword Ice, Valyrian steel) by First Men custom. Father of five trueborn children; let them keep the direwolf pups.
  • Bran (Brandon) Stark 🆕 new this chapter – POV; seven years old, curious, softhearted, rides a pony. Took a grey-black male pup (not yet named).
  • Robb Stark 🆕 new this chapter – eldest trueborn son, about 14, big and broad with the red-brown hair and blue eyes of his mother's family, the Tullys of Riverrun. Brash and competitive; found the pups and insisted on keeping them.
  • Jon Snow 🆕 new this chapter – Ned's bastard son, 14, slender, dark, grey-eyed, perceptive. Argued that the five pups were meant for Ned's five trueborn children (leaving himself out), then claimed the white sixth pup.
  • Rickon Stark 🆕 new this chapter – the baby of the family (mentioned).
  • Stark mother and two daughters 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Ned has three trueborn sons and two daughters; his wife is a Tully. Not yet seen.
  • Direwolf pups 🆕 new this chapter – a huge dead mother direwolf, killed by an antler lodged in her throat, left five grey pups (three male, two female) plus a white, red-eyed sixth pup, found apart, that opened its eyes early (Jon's). The children must feed and train them themselves. No direwolf had been seen south of the Wall in two hundred years; some men call it an omen.
Winterfell household
  • Theon Greyjoy 🆕 new this chapter – Ned's ward, 19, lean, dark, amused by everything; kicked the executed man's head and wanted to kill the pups. Jon dislikes him.
  • Jory Cassel 🆕 new this chapter – captain of Ned's guard.
  • Hullen 🆕 new this chapter – master of horse; wanted the pups killed. His son Harwin 🆕 new this chapter agreed.
  • Desmond 🆕 new this chapter – guardsman.
  • Ser Rodrik 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – has a red bitch with a new litter, suggested as a nurse for a pup.
  • Old Nan 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – tells the children tales of cruel wildlings and the Others.
The Night's Watch
  • The deserter 🆕 new this chapter – old man in ragged black, missing both ears and a finger to frostbite; caught near a holdfast and beheaded by Ned for oathbreaking.
  • Will – ranger, four years on the Wall; last seen seized by the reanimated Royce in the prologue.
  • Ser Waymar Royce – 18-year-old knight of the Watch; killed by the Others in the prologue.
  • Gared – old man-at-arms, left guarding horses in the prologue; fate unknown.
  • Mormont (commander) and Maester Aemon – mentioned in the prologue.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – "Robert of the House Baratheon, the First of his Name", King of the Andals and the Rhoynar and the First Men; Ned acts in his name. Has a royal headsman.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed beings with crystalline swords who killed Royce; in Old Nan's tales they sire half-human children with women in the Long Night.
  • Mance Rayder 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – "King-beyond-the-Wall", whom wildlings are said to serve.
  • Wildlings – raiders north of the Wall; the band Will's party tracked was found dead and then vanished.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell 🆕 new this chapter – seat of House Stark in the north.
  • The Wall / Castle Black – the Watch's barrier and main base; the haunted forest lies beyond.
  • Riverrun 🆕 new this chapter – Tully seat, the home of Robb's mother's family.
  • The Seven Kingdoms 🆕 new this chapter – realm ruled by King Robert; it is the ninth year of summer, with late-summer snows starting.
