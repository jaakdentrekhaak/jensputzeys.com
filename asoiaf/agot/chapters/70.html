Chapter 70 – Jon

Jon Snow rides out of Castle Black by night to join Robb's war, is caught and brought back by his friends, and is then told by Lord Commander Mormont why his place is at the Wall, ending by swearing he will not run again.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Daenerys Stormhorn", "the dragon's daughter" – exiled Targaryen princess, wife of Khal Drogo. Woke from the fever after the birth, torn and aching, her arms around the pale butter-cream and gold dragon's egg, which felt hot (the other two felt hot to her, cold to Jorah). Learned her son Rhaego was stillborn and monstrous (scaled, stub tail, bat wings, long dead, per Mirri); does not weep ("all the grief has been burned out of me") and blames Jorah and Mirri. Swore by the old and new gods that Mago and Jhaqo will "die screaming" for the murder of Eroeh. Bathed Drogo and tried to rouse him under the stars, then, finding him lost, smothered him with a cushion. Her fever dreams: a red door, Rhaegar as "the last dragon" with her own face, Viserys crowned in molten gold, flying on wings.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord; his chest wound healed to a hideous scar, but he is blind, unresponsive and walks only where led; Mirri said he would return only "when the sun rises in the west... when your womb quickens again, and you bear a living child." His khalasar (forty thousand) is gone: Pono named himself Khal Pono and left first; Jhaqo followed with twenty thousand riders and a new khalasar; others slipped away, taking Drogo's herds and slaves. His three bloodriders (Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho) and the warrior Quaro are dead.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont "the Andal", "Iron Lord" – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), Dany's adviser; carried her into the birthing tent, now gaunt, limping and ashamed, believing he fed her son to the darkness. Tells her the Dothraki follow only the strong. Handmaids Jhiqui, Irri (Dothraki) and Doreah (Lysene); khas warriors who stayed: Aggo (archer), Rakharo, Jhogo (thin mustache). Fewer than a hundred people remain: old, sick, women, children, and the sworn few. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
  • Mirri Maz Duur – Lhazareen godswife and healer, Dany's slave; cast the bloodmagic, says Dany knew the price, hates the Dothraki for sacking her town, burning her temple and raping her ("What did you save?"). Dany has her bound hand and foot. Eroeh – the timid Lhazareen girl Dany once saved; raped by Mago (now Jhaqo's bloodrider), given to Jhaqo and his other bloodriders, then killed.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark "the Young Wolf" – fifteen-year-old eldest son, leader of the northern host (wolf Grey Wind, said to have killed four men and a dozen horses); took Jaime prisoner in the Whispering Wood, then broke the siege of Riverrun by night, freeing Edmure; the lords of the Trident now stand behind him. Under the Twins deal he will wed one of Walder Frey's daughters after the war. Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, with Robb at Riverrun.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, former King's Hand; beheaded on Joffrey's order, his head on the Red Keep wall. Tywin calls the killing "madness," since a living Ned could have bought peace. Sansa Stark – his elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey, held in the Red Keep and beaten at Joffrey's orders. Arya Stark – younger daughter, taken away by Yoren (Lord Lefford repeats that the queen holds "the Hand's daughters").
  • Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord of Winterfell, with direwolf Summer; Rickon – youngest, four, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Maester Luwin, Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan), Osha (wildling servant in shackles), Hodor (huge simple stableman), Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen and Theon Greyjoy (with Robb). Dead: Septa Mordane (executed), Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Heward, Wyl, Tomard, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf).
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort, whose foot was beaten by Tywin on the kingsroad; now north of the Lannisters with the remnants, wary and slow to pursue. Lord Hornwood died there; Lord Cerwyn, Ser Wylis Manderly (fat son of Lord Wyman, White Harbor), Harrion Karstark and four Freys were captured.
  • At Riverrun: the Greatjon (Lord Umber) (burned the siege towers; giant-in-chains banner), Maege Mormont, Rickard Karstark (lost two sons), Galbart Glover, Lord Jason Mallister (eagle banner); Ser Helman Tallhart holds the Twins. Robb's guard: Theon Greyjoy, Patrek Mallister, Smalljon Umber, Robin Flint, Ser Wendel Manderly, Dacey Mormont (Maege's eldest, heir to Bear Island), Olyvar Frey. Dead: Torrhen and Eddard Karstark and Daryn Hornwood, killed by Jaime. Howland Reed (mentioned) – Ned's friend at Greywater Watch.
  • Riverrun relief (as reported): Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully led the van cutting down sentries and palisades; riders under Robb swept the west camp; Tytos Blackwood sortied from Riverrun and freed Ser Edmure Tully; a Tyroshi sellsword leader of the Lannister freeriders changed sides.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey, "Lord of the Crossing" – ninety, wizened, gouty, sharp-tongued, with an eighth wife of sixteen and scores of descendants; Hoster Tully's bannerman who holds the only bridge over the Green Fork; resentful of Tullys and Arryns. Sold Robb the crossing and his swords (four hundred kept home). A Frey son is married to Tywin's sister.
  • Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, past sixty; Ser Jared, Ser Hosteen, Ser Danwell (sons), Ronel Rivers (bastard) led the Frey levies; Ser Perwyn (youngest envoy); Olyvar Frey – Robb's new squire; Elmar Frey – youngest son, pledged to Arya; two boys both named Walder (eight and seven), wards for Winterfell. Four Freys were captured fighting for Robb on the kingsroad. Sigil: twin towers, blue on grey.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, vain and cruel; had Ned beheaded; talks of leading the City Watch against Renly himself and of killing Robb. Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – commands Tywin to bring the army to defend the Red Keep "in the name of the king and council." Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Small council: Lord Janos Slynt (frog-faced City Watch commander, made a lord with Harrenhal; bloody-spear sigil), Grand Maester Pycelle, Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin), Lord Varys (eunuch; hears whispers about Stannis).
  • Kingsguard: Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Arys Oakheart, Ser Ilyn Payne (King's Justice), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane; Ser Jaime Lannister (Lord Commander, captive). Ser Barristan Selmy – "Barristan the Bold," dismissed and wanted for treason; Tywin thinks Joffrey a fool to lose him.
House Baratheon / Tyrell (claimants)
  • Renly Baratheon – late King Robert's younger brother; fled King's Landing after Ned's arrest, wed Margaery Tyrell at Highgarden and has now claimed the crown; her father Lord Mace Tyrell and brothers (including Ser Loras, the Knight of Flowers) have sworn him their swords. Stannis Baratheon – elder brother, on Dragonstone; per Varys he is building ships, hiring sellswords and bringing a shadowbinder from Asshai; Tywin thinks him the greatest danger and he has not declared. Storm's End and Highgarden are calling banners.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Lord of Casterly Rock, Hand of the King, grim and controlled (sweats under his stillness); his punishing march south came too late (stragglers die or desert daily). Says "They have my son" twice. Plans to march to Harrenhal and burn the riverlands with Ser Gregor, Vargo Hoat (freerider captain) and Ser Amory Lorch, three hundred horse each, to finish Robb before Renly marches. Criticizes the council's blunders (making Slynt, a butcher's son, a lord; dismissing Barristan Selmy; seating the Hound). Sends Tyrion to King's Landing to "rule" and curb Joffrey and the council, saying only "You are my son," and forbids him to bring Shae. Hanged the inn's keeper on a gibbet.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Tywin's dwarf son, still nursing his injured elbow; wry, drinks, hurled a cup to show peace is broken. Stunned to be sent to rule at court; guesses Tywin thinks Jaime as good as dead. Understands Tywin's remedy for disloyal councillors: spikes, heads, walls. Intends to take Shae (young camp follower lover) anyway and keep his clansmen (Shagga, Timett). Podrick Payne – his squire; Bronn – his sellsword.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tywin's son and Cersei's twin, Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; captured in the Whispering Wood; his besieging army at Riverrun (split into three camps by the rivers) has been slaughtered, captured or scattered. Ser Kevan Lannister – Tywin's brother, steady and frowning, married to a daughter of Ser Harys Swyft; explained Riverrun's layout. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – huge vassal, suggests cutting out a failed scout's eyes. Ser Addam Marbrand – sardonic cavalry captain, argues the war is not lost; will screen the march with outriders in groups of four.
  • Lord Lefford (stores; suggests ransom, mentions the queen holds the Hand's daughters), Ser Harys Swyft (chinless, craven, wails for peace and slanders Jaime), Ser Flement Brax (silver-and-purple; his father Lord Brax drowned crossing a river in armor), Ser Forley Prester (commanded the south camp; retreated in order with two thousand spears and as many bowmen), Lord Serrett, a Crakehall messenger (boar sigil), Vargo Hoat and Ser Amory Lorch (raider captains).
  • Cersei Lannister – queen regent in King's Landing, orders the army to defend the Red Keep; has not told Joffrey about Renly.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, freed from Lannister captivity at Riverrun. Lord Hoster Tully – their father, sick at Riverrun. Lord Tytos Blackwood – led the Riverrun sortie. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate. Marq Piper (raider with about fifty men) and Karyl Vance harassed Lannister supply trains; Beric Dondarrion and Thoros (red priest) still plague foraging parties; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward (and squire) to the Lord Commander; burned hand now unwrapped. Rode south at night on a mare with Ghost to avenge his father and join Robb, leaving Longclaw on his bed and knocking Sam aside; his friends caught him and brought him back. Calls himself "bastard and oathbreaker." Mormont talked him round: Jon swore he is the Old Bear's man and will not run again, though privately he was tempted to try again. Direwolf Ghost gave him away. Has just been told to put his sword back on.
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander at Castle Black; had Jon watched and expected both flight and return (Maester Aemon predicted it). Drinks beer with lemon, eats boiled eggs; his talking raven repeats words. His sister Maege Mormont is probably in Robb's host with her daughters; his son Jorah fled in disgrace. Says the walking dead matter more than who sits the Iron Throne; reports of vast elk and mammoth herds fleeing south, huge footprints near Eastwatch, abandoned villages and great fires in the mountains. Means to lead the Watch in force beyond the Wall to find Benjen, "alive or dead," and face the King-beyond-the-Wall and the Others, with Jon and Ghost.
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, gentle friend of Jon; tried to stop him, then woke the others. Pyp, Grenn "the Aurochs", Toad, Halder, Matthar – Jon's fellow recruits who surrounded him, recited the Night's Watch vow and brought him back. Maester Aemon – old blind maester, born Aemon Targaryen, predicted Jon would go. Hobb ("Three-Finger Hobb") – cook. Donal Noye – armorer; Ser Alliser Thorne – sent south with a wight's hand; Yoren – recruiter now at King's Landing with Arya.
  • Cotter Pyke – commander at Eastwatch; Ser Denys – at the Shadow Tower; Quorin Halfhand – ranger who took a captive swearing Mance Rayder is massing all his people in a new secret stronghold. Benjen Stark – Jon's uncle, First Ranger, missing along with other rangers. Dead: Ser Jaremy Rykker, Othor, Jafer Flowers.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, wights (dead men who walk; fire destroys them), children of the forest, giants, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall, said to be gathering all the wildlings), wildlings.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child (per Varys, killed by the Lannisters); Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa, executed as a traitor (see Stark household). Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places

Places
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress at the Wall; Jon slipped out through the old stables; the King's Tower holds Mormont. The Wall – giant ice barrier. Mole's Town – village along the kingsroad near Castle Black, mostly underground, with a brothel marked by a red lantern. The kingsroad. Eastwatch-by-the-Sea and the Shadow Tower – other Watch castles. Winterfell – Stark seat, held by Bran and Rickon.
  • The crossroads inn – large inn where Tywin's army stops, with a gibbet in the yard. Riverrun – Tully castle on a point where the Tumblestone meets the Red Fork; Harrenhal – ruined castle on the Gods Eye where Tywin will march; the Whispering Wood; Highgarden (Tyrell seat), Dragonstone, Storm's End, Casterly Rock, Lannisport, the Twins, Moat Cailin, the Eyrie, King's Landing (Red Keep, Great Sept of Baelor); the southern trail, Vaes Dothrak, Asshai, the Free Cities, Bear Island.
