Chapter 55 – Catelyn

Catelyn reaches Robb's army at the ruins of Moat Cailin, finds him afraid but resolute, helps him settle his plan to march south, and tells him she is going to Riverrun rather than home.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Khal Drogo's pregnant wife (son to be named Rhaego); survived a poisoning attempt at Vaes Dothrak's Western Market, put her three dragon eggs in a brazier with no visible result, and left with Drogo's khalasar riding south and west. Khal Drogo – her Dothraki warlord husband; vowed to cross the sea and take the Iron Throne for his son; bloodriders Cohollo, Qotho, Haggo. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island, her adviser, who stopped the poisoner; handmaids Doreah, Irri, Jhiqui; guards Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo, Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo. The Lysene wineseller who tried to poison her is chained to her horse; Byan Votyris – Norvoshi merchant captain.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark (Lady Stark, née Tully) – Ned's wife; sailed to White Harbor and reached Robb's army at Moat Cailin. Left the Eyrie on bad terms after Lysa threatened her with the Moon Door when she offered to foster Lord Robert at Winterfell; admitted she no longer holds Tyrion, whom the gods freed "with help from my fool of a sister". Reads Sansa's letter as Cersei's threat. Names Ser Rodrik castellan of Winterfell, advises Robb to stay decisive before his lords, then announces she will go to Riverrun, where her father may be dying and her brother Edmure is surrounded by foes.
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son, who has called the banners and leads about eighteen thousand men from Moat Cailin; grown a stubble beard with reddish hairs; proud but frightened that the Lannisters will kill Ned and Sansa. Plans to leave archers at the Moat, march south, then split his host at the Neck: the foot (under Roose Bolton) down the kingsroad to face Lord Tywin, himself with the horse crossing the Green Fork at the Twins and racing to Riverrun. His wolf is Grey Wind, who ate two of the Greatjon's fingers.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; held as a traitor in King's Landing. Sansa Stark – confined in King's Landing; her letter to Robb, praising the Lannisters' kindness, is read as written for Cersei. Arya Stark – younger daughter, not mentioned in the letter; whereabouts unknown. Bran Stark – crippled, left as lord in Winterfell. Rickon – youngest, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel – now castellan, on his way north from White Harbor; Maester Luwin – counsellor, unskilled in war; Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb's army); Hodor, Osha (captured wildling), Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen. Dead: Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf), Lyanna, Brandon. Direwolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – Lord of the Dreadfort, whose banner is a flayed man; small voice, strange pale colorless eyes, disturbing; wanted Tyrion as hostage. Robb is afraid of him, so Catelyn and Robb agree he should lead the eastern host.
  • The Greatjon (Umber) – huge bass-voiced lord, urges attacking Tywin by surprise; Robb means to honor him with the foot; Catelyn warns that fearless is not the same as cunning. Rickard Karstark (sunburst banner), who with the Glovers favors marching round Tywin to join Edmure. Galbart Glover 🆕 new this chapter and Robett Glover; Ser Helman Tallhart 🆕 new this chapter; Lord Hornwood ("ever obliging", bull-moose banner); Maege Mormont, Cerwyn. The three towers fly the Stark, Karstark and Umber banners.
  • Manderlys of White Harbor 🆕 new this chapter – Lord Wyman Manderly, near sixty, too fat to ride, stayed to hold White Harbor; his sons Ser Wylis (quiet, formal) and Ser Wendel (loud, jovial) lead about 1,500 men to Robb; both bald with walrus mustaches. Sigil: white merman on blue-green.
  • Howland Reed (mentioned) – crannogman, Ned's old friend at Greywater Watch; Robb has asked for help from the crannogmen if Lannisters come up the Neck.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead. Joffrey – boy king, son of Cersei. Queen Cersei Lannister – holds Ned and Sansa. Myrcella, Tommen – her younger children. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin who betrayed Ned; Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; Grand Maester Pycelle; Janos Slynt – City Watch commander; Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart – Kingsguard; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's shield; Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon – Robert's brothers (Stannis at Dragonstone).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister at the Eyrie, will not stir beyond her Bloody Gate; Robert Arryn – her young son, Lord of the Eyrie. Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father at Riverrun, possibly dying; Ser Edmure Tully – her brother, holds Riverrun with his bannermen.
  • Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, left Lysa's service for Catelyn's; rides with her. Lord Vance – killed holding the Golden Tooth pass; Lord Piper – falling back to Riverrun with Jaime on his heels.
  • Lord Frey (Walder Frey, mentioned) – Hoster Tully's bannerman who controls the Twins' bridge over the Green Fork; Hoster has never trusted him. Ser Raymun Darry – riverlands knight, killed at the Mummer's Ford. "Lord Derik/Erik" 🆕 new this chapter – southron lordling Ned put in command of a force sent under the king's banner (including Winterfell guardsmen), which Tywin's men ambushed at the Mummer's Ford; he and a few others may have escaped.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – leads a second, larger Lannister army from the south, closing the kingsroad and marching north toward Harrenhal, burning as he goes; expected to take river castles one by one. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – routed Vance and Piper below the Golden Tooth and presses on toward Riverrun. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – attacked Ned's force from the rear at the Mummer's Ford.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, no longer Catelyn's captive (freed at the Eyrie); last known in the Vale mountains with Bronn, among the Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander; confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a dead man in Mormont's solar. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly; Maester Aemon; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Donal Noye – armorer; Pyp, Grenn, Toad; Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, missing First Ranger. A walking dead man (wight) attacked the Lord Commander's tower; the Others are otherwise known only from legend.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master; Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend; Septa Mordane; Captain Qos.

Places

Places
  • Moat Cailin – ancient First Men stronghold at the south end of the Neck, now ruins: three surviving mossy towers (Gatehouse Tower, where Robb sits; the leaning Drunkard's Tower; Children's Tower, from legend) above bog, quicksand and lizard-lions. Commands the causeway south; Robb's army is camped there, short on supplies.
  • The Neck – boggy land between north and south; Greywater Watch – Howland Reed's home. White Harbor – northern port of the Manderlys. The Golden Tooth – western pass where Jaime beat Vance and Piper. Riverrun – Tully seat; the Eyrie and Bloody Gate, Moon Door; Harrenhal, the Trident, the Red Fork, Mummer's Ford, the Green Fork and the Twins, the ruby ford – river sites. The kingsroad; Casterly Rock.
  • Vaes Dothrak – Dothraki sacred city Dany has just left. King's Landing, Winterfell, Dragonstone, Castle Black / the Wall.
