Chapter 4 – Eddard

King Robert arrives at Winterfell, visits the crypt with Ned, and offers him the post of Hand of the King and a betrothal between their children.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North, POV. Serious, honorable, loves the north; was Robert's close friend and fellow ward at the Eyrie. Has just been offered the Hand of the King; he feels it is the last thing he wants, has asked for time to consult his wife, and has not yet answered. Distrusts Lord Tywin Lannister. Keeps flowers for his dead sister Lyanna.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, of Riverrun; greeted by Robert warmly. Not yet told of the offers.
  • Robb, Jon Snow (bastard), Bran, Arya, Rickon – the Stark children; each has a direwolf pup (Jon's is white).
  • Sansa Stark – eleven; Robert proposes betrothing her to his son Joffrey.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother on the Wall; Robert says he will hear his report later.
  • Rickard Stark 🆕 new this chapter (dead) – Ned's father, former Lord of Winterfell; entombed in the crypt, sword across his lap.
  • Brandon Stark 🆕 new this chapter (dead) – Ned's eldest brother and heir, strangled at twenty on the Mad King Aerys's orders days before he was to wed Catelyn; his father was made to watch.
  • Lyanna Stark 🆕 new this chapter (dead) – Ned's sister, died at sixteen of a fever; she was betrothed to Robert and he loved her deeply. Ned was with her as she died and promised her something ("Promise me") and she asked to be buried at Winterfell. Robert "vowed to kill Rhaegar for what he did to her".
  • Howland Reed 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – a small "crannogman" who was there when Lyanna died.
  • Direwolf pups – six, one per child; being named.
Winterfell household
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward, taken hostage as a boy after Balon Greyjoy's rebellion nine years ago.
  • Jory Cassel, Maester Luwin, Hullen, Harwin, Ser Rodrik, Old Nan – household and retainers.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Jon Arryn – late Lord of the Eyrie, Warden of the East and Hand of the King; Ned's and Robert's foster father; died within a fortnight of a "fire in his gut" after a tourney for the prince's name day.
  • Lysa Arryn – his widow, Catelyn's sister; Robert thinks grief has driven her mad because she left court at night with her son rather than let him be fostered with Tywin Lannister.
  • Robert Arryn 🆕 new this chapter – Jon Arryn's six-year-old, sickly son, now Lord of the Eyrie and the king's namesake. Robert intends to give him the Eyrie but not the Wardenship of the East for now (Ned objected).
  • Brynden – Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate. Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – Lord of Storm's End, hugely tall but now fat, bearded, dark-circled, loud and heavy-drinking; bored and worn by ruling ("sitting a throne is a thousand times harder than winning one"). Won the throne by rebellion fifteen years ago; killed Rhaegar Targaryen at the Trident with his warhammer. Rejects the title "Your Grace" from Ned. Has come north to make Ned his Hand.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – the queen, travels in a huge wheelhouse; protested Robert's going to the crypt first. Twin sister of Jaime. Angry at Lysa's flight.
  • Prince Joffrey 🆕 new this chapter – Robert's son and the crown prince (a tall boy); proposed husband for Sansa. Prince Tommen (7) and other royal children are with the queen.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister 🆕 new this chapter – the queen's twin brother, golden-haired.
  • Tyrion Lannister, "the Imp" 🆕 new this chapter – a small, stunted Lannister, the queen's brother.
  • Sandor Clegane 🆕 new this chapter – terribly burned face; a rider in the royal party.
  • Two Kingsguard knights 🆕 new this chapter – in snow-white cloaks, flanking the king.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Lannister head at Casterly Rock; had agreed to foster Robert Arryn. Ned would not trust a child to him.
  • Maester Pycelle – tended Jon Arryn.
  • Mad King Aerys II Targaryen (dead) – executed Brandon Stark; Ned and Robert rebelled against him.
  • Rhaegar Targaryen (dead) – Targaryen prince, killed by Robert at the Trident in a battle where he wore black armour with a ruby three-headed dragon.
  • Balon Greyjoy 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – self-proclaimed King of the Iron Islands, defeated nine years ago by Robert and Ned.
The Night's Watch
  • Understrength (below a thousand) and losing men on rangings. Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others in the prologue; Will seized by his reanimated body; Gared's fate unknown), Mormont, Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed beings who killed Royce; Ned and Robert treat them as legend (Robert swears "the Others take..."). Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall. Wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles in Pentos)
  • Daenerys Targaryen (Dany) – 13, silver-haired; given to Khal Drogo by her brother. Viserys Targaryen – her brother, calls himself king; cruel and obsessed with retaking the throne. Ser Willem Darry – dead guardian.
Pentos and Dothraki
  • Magister Illyrio Mopatis – fat merchant hosting the Targaryens. Khal Drogo – huge Dothraki warlord with an enormous khalasar, never defeated. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight at Drogo's feast, wanted by the king for selling poachers to slavers.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; its crypt holds the Lords of Winterfell on stone thrones with iron swords across their laps; empty tombs wait at the end.
  • King's Landing – capital and seat of the Iron Throne; Robert wants Ned to live here.
  • Storm's End 🆕 new this chapter – Robert's seat (he is its Lord); Highgarden – southern region with golden roses and fruit, as described by Robert.
  • The Eyrie / the Vale – Arryn seat; Lysa and young Robert Arryn are there. Riverrun – Tully seat. Casterly Rock – Lannister seat.
  • The Trident – river where Rhaegar died.
  • The Neck 🆕 new this chapter – Robert says there is scarcely a decent inn north of it on his journey and that the north is empty and vast.
  • The Wall / Castle Black, the haunted forest – the northern barrier and wilderness.
  • Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone, other Free Cities – across the narrow sea.
  • The Iron Islands 🆕 new this chapter – home of the Greyjoys.
