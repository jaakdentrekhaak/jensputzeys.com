Chapter 14 – Catelyn

Eight days after Ned and the girls leave, a stableman-looking assassin tries to kill Bran during a library fire and is killed by Bran's direwolf; Catelyn, suspecting the Lannisters, resolves to travel secretly to King's Landing with Ser Rodrik.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark (Lady Stark, née Tully) – POV; had been collapsed in grief at Bran's bedside, snapping at Maester Luwin over household business. Fought off the assassin with bare hands (deep cuts to her fingers, scalp torn), slept four days under milk of the poppy, and woke resolute. Tells a trusted few her suspicion that Bran did not fall but was thrown, and that Jaime Lannister (who skipped the hunt the day Bran fell) is behind it, echoing Lysa's belief the Lannisters murdered Jon Arryn. Will ride in secret with Ser Rodrik down the White Knife to White Harbor and take ship to King's Landing, to show the dagger/ask questions and warn Ned.
  • Bran Stark – 7; still unconscious, "unchanged"; target of the assassination attempt. His direwolf (unnamed, golden eyes) killed the attacker by tearing out his throat, licked Catelyn's blood, and now stays on his bed. Guard: one man in the sickroom, one at the door, two at the stair foot; no visitors without Robb's or Catelyn's warrant.
  • Robb Stark – 14; now acting master of Winterfell in Ned's absence; wears real steel and boiled leather; makes appointments (captain of the guard Hallis Mollen, etc.), pleads with his mother to look after Rickon and herself. Reasoned that someone fears what Bran might say or know if he wakes. Angry at the Lannisters; rebuked by Rodrik for waving his sword. Direwolf Grey Wind.
  • Rickon Stark – 3; bewildered, clings to Robb crying. Wolf Shaggydog.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, gone south as Hand with Sansa and Arya eight days ago; Catelyn begged him to stay. Sansa and Arya (with direwolves Lady, Nymeria; Arya secretly has Needle) – with Ned.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, travelling north with Benjen Stark to the Wall. Lyanna, Brandon, Rickard (Ned's dead sister, brother, father); Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin – learned, wary, kindly; tried to get Catelyn to attend to accounts; notes Bran was always sure-footed. Ser Rodrik Cassel – household knight and master-at-arms, white-whiskered; spotted that the killer's dagger is Valyrian steel with a dragonbone hilt, too fine for him; will escort Catelyn. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward; swore secrecy, speaks of his House owing the Starks a debt. Hallis Mollen (Hal) 🆕 new this chapter – muscular guardsman with a square brown beard, new captain of the guard. Old Nan (storyteller; washed Catelyn), Hodor (simple stableboy, said to be acting queerly), Septa Mordane (the girls' septa, south with them), Jory Cassel (former captain of the guard, went south with Ned), Hullen (master of horse, went south with Ned; Harwin his son), Poole 🆕 new this chapter (Winterfell's steward, went south to set up Ned's household at King's Landing), Mikken (smith), Chayle (septon-librarian), Alyn (servant), etc.
  • The assassin 🆕 new this chapter – gaunt, dirty, blond man, smelling of horses, unknown in Winterfell though seen about the castle for weeks; hid in the half-empty stables; kept muttering "You weren't s'posed to be here"; had ninety silver stags buried in the straw. Dead.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister; believes Lannisters murdered her husband Jon Arryn, the late Hand), Robert Arryn (her sickly son), Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully (Catelyn's brother, uncle and father). Catelyn: "a Tully of Riverrun".
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Ser Jaime Lannister, "the Kingslayer" – now suspected by Catelyn (not hunting that day, stayed in the castle); no proof. Queen Cersei (his twin; would not take the accusation kindly), King Robert Baratheon, Prince Joffrey (crown prince), Tommen, Myrcella, Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (Joffrey's burned bodyguard), Tywin Lannister (Lannister head), Maester Pycelle (royal maester), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard Lord Commander), Stannis, Renly (Robert's brothers), Littlefinger, Varys (court figures), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned; self-styled King of the Iron Islands), Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar (dead). Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother, riding north to the Wall with Benjen and Jon.
  • The royal party has left Winterfell heading south by the kingsroad.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother), Yoren (black brother escorting recruits), Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown), Mormont (old commander), Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – under Robb's care; the sickroom tower (Bran), the library tower (set on fire as a diversion, books lost in part), stables, yard, Great Keep (Catelyn's chambers).
  • White Knife 🆕 new this chapter and White Harbor 🆕 new this chapter – the river and port Catelyn plans to take to the sea, then sail to King's Landing to beat Ned and the Lannisters there. She avoids the kingsroad.
  • The Wall / Castle Black (Watch base), Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End, The Trident, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
