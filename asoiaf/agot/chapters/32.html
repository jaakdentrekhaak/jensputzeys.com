Chapter 32 – Arya

While chasing cats for Syrio, Arya runs from the Lannister royal children, gets lost in the Red Keep's cellars, overhears two men plot in a secret tunnel, and tells her father, who dismisses it just before Yoren arrives with urgent news.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Arya Stark – POV; Ned's younger daughter (11), about to be scolded for her scratches; learning "water dancing" from Syrio (calm as still water, quick as a snake, quiet as a shadow); caught the one-eared black tomcat of the Red Keep. Was mistaken for a ragged boy by Princess Myrcella and Prince Tommen and knocked Tommen down fleeing. Overheard (in a tunnel beneath the castle) two unknown men: a stout, scarred man in a steel cap who is a "wizard" of sorts and a very fat man with rings and a forked yellow beard who speaks with a Free Cities accent. She repeated it to Ned, who thought they were mummers.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Hand of the King; was reading a huge old book in his solar; had half his guard hunting Arya; brushes off her story as mummers; is about to hear Yoren in private. Sansa (Arya's elder sister, betrothed to Joffrey), Bran (crippled, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord), Rickon (youngest), Catelyn (their mother, traveling), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall; Arya wants to write him), Benjen (uncle, missing); direwolves Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's), Lady (Sansa's, killed), Nymeria (Arya's, gone; Arya pretends she is beside her in the dark).
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Desmond – Ned's guard, loyal and single-minded; says Ned has fifty guards in King's Landing and "wizards die like other men". Harwin and Fat Tom – guards who bring Arya in. Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Septa Mordane (the girls' septa, praying for Arya's safe return), Syrio Forel (Braavosi dancing master; dabs her wounds with Myrish fire), Jeyne Poole (Sansa's friend), Alyn, Porther, Varly, Jacks, Tomard (Ned's guards). Elsewhere: Vayon Poole (Winterfell steward), Ser Rodrik (with Catelyn), Hodor (simple giant), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Maester Luwin, Old Nan, Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (guard captain); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords

Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Princess Myrcella (Cersei's daughter, seen here) – golden-haired, in blue satin, giggling and haughty; Prince Tommen (her younger brother, seen here) – plump, blond boy with a pearl-sewn stag and toy sword; both with a large septa and two Lannister guards (Godwyn 🆕 new this chapter among them).
  • King Robert (Ned's friend), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father), Jaime (her twin, Kingsguard), Tyrion (their dwarf brother, now Catelyn's captive), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor Clegane (brother knights), Littlefinger (Master of coin), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Pycelle (Grand Maester), Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless executioner), Janos Slynt (gold cloaks, city watch), Thoros of Myr (red priest), Moon Boy (fool), Tobho Mott and Gendry (smith and apprentice), Lancel (Robert's Lannister squire). Gold cloaks at the postern gate are rude to Arya.
  • The one-eared black tomcat – the Red Keep's mean old "king of the castle" once snatched a quail from Tywin's fingers at Robert's feast.
Overheard conspirators 🆕 new this chapter
  • A stout man in mail, steel cap and leather half cape – round scarred face, soundless gait, carries a torch; works in secret; says Stannis and Lysa Arryn have fled beyond his reach and are gathering swords; that Loras and Renly want Robert to wed Loras's fourteen-year-old sister; that Ned "has the bastard, the book and soon the truth"; fears war between Starks and Lannisters after Tyrion's abduction. Wants gold and fifty "birds". Arya thinks he looks familiar.
  • A grossly fat man – forked yellow beard, rings on every finger, Free Cities accent; light on his feet; urges delay ("What good is war now? We are not ready"), says "if one Hand can die, why not a second", and that the princess is with child and the khal will not move until his son is born. Neither is named in Arya's hearing.
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren – gruff, stooped, unwashed black brother, recruiting men for the Wall for Jeor Mormont; has ridden hard to reach Ned ahead of freeriders and sellswords from the inn; says some of them went to Casterly Rock and Lord Tywin will have heard "the word"; says Jon was well when he left. Offers condolence for Benjen's black blood. Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
