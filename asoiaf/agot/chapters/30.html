Chapter 30 – Eddard

On the tourney's last day Ned sees the dead young knight laid out, hears the drunken king's complaints, watches the Hound win the joust after Gregor Clegane's rampage, and is secretly warned by Varys that the queen's side tried to get Robert killed in the melee.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Hand of the King; investigating Jon Arryn's death. Guilt over the dead boy; hopeful after a warm breakfast with Robert; now told by Varys that Jon Arryn was poisoned and the king is in danger. Believes Gendry is Robert's bastard (his own inference). Still holds the dagger used against Bran, which he links to Tyrion and to Jon Arryn's murder.
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey; watched the final jousts at his side, wears Loras's rose, loves the Knight of Flowers. Pleasant to Arya at the feast.
  • Arya Stark – Ned's younger daughter (11); trains with Syrio Forel (blindfold, spins, balancing on one leg; "tomorrow we catch cats"); proud of her bruises; insists on keeping Syrio.
  • Bran (8, crippled by a fall, alive at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord of Winterfell), Rickon (youngest), Catelyn (Ned's wife, traveling north in secret), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing), Old Nan. Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady (Sansa's, killed), Nymeria (Arya's, gone). Lyanna (Ned and Robert's lost sister/love; Robert says she would never have shamed him; Ned says she had iron in her).
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Septa Mordane – the girls' septa and chaperone; ill today. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend, steward Vayon Poole's daughter. Jory Cassel – Ned's captain of guards; keeps Ned informed, is still searching the whorehouses for something (the dead squire's trail). Harwin – guard who announces Varys. Alyn, Porther, Cayn – Ned's guards. Varly, Jacks, Tomard, Desmond, Lothor Brune (freerider who beat Jory). Ser Rodrik (master-at-arms, with Catelyn); at Winterfell: Hodor (simple giant), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Maester Luwin, Mikken (smith), Vayon Poole (steward); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
  • Syrio Forel – Arya's Braavosi dancing master, flamboyant; "every hurt is a lesson".
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Jon Arryn (dead; Ned says he was poisoned per Varys, with the "tears of Lys"; was Hand fourteen years, foster-father to Ned and Robert at the Eyrie), Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister, Jon's widow, silent in the Eyrie), Robert Arryn (her sickly son), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father), Edmure (her brother), Brynden (her uncle). Lord Jason Mallister (of Seagard) and Walder Frey (and kin) – river lords' tourney knights.
  • Ser Hugh of the Vale – the young knight killed in the joust by Gregor; Jon Arryn's squire for four years, knighted by Robert, no family but a mother in the Vale. Varys implies he was the one who poisoned Jon Arryn (a boy who owed him everything, who stayed in King's Landing and prospered).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, boisterous, too fat for his armor; wanted to fight in the melee, threw a breastplate at Selmy when told no one would dare strike him; admits he was never so alive as winning the throne and "so dead" now; resents Cersei ("cold"), despairs of Joffrey, dreams of sailing to the Free Cities as a sellsword; believes Joffrey lied about Lady/Nymeria incident; has bastards (Gendry, a boy at Storm's End, a daughter in the Vale, whose mother he once loved). Did not fight in the end; laughed at Jaime's fall.
  • Queen Cersei – sulked in the castle, absent from the tourney. Varys says she forced Robert into the melee by forbidding it, hoping he would be killed. Joffrey Baratheon – prince; his father says he is not "merely wild". Lancel 🆕 new this chapter – Robert's squire, about Sansa's age, a Lannister cousin (son of Tywin's brother); another unnamed Lannister cousin squire, about fifteen, sandy-haired and green-eyed. Both assigned by the queen.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – lost the joust to the Hound, rolled in the dirt, helmet dented stuck on his head; humiliating for him. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – burned face, hound's-head helm; beat Jaime, saved Loras from Gregor, never struck at his brother's bare face; refused the title "ser" but took the victory, the purse, and the commons' love; now the tourney champion.
  • Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain That Rides" – over seven feet tall; lost to Loras's maneuver (a mare in heat unsettled his stallion), killed his own horse with one blow in a rage, attacked Loras with a sword and was stopped by the Hound and then by the king. Rumored to have killed Prince Aegon and Elia of Dorne at the sack of King's Landing (as Ned recalls); gossip about the deaths of his first two wives, soon marrying a third; his sister and father died strangely; he inherited the family keep while Sandor left.
  • Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" – dazzling in sapphire-forget-me-not armor and cloak; unhorsed Gregor, saved by the Hound, yielded the day to him. Renly Baratheon – the king's youngest brother; bet against Littlefinger on the joust; jests with Littlefinger over the tourney bets. Stannis (at Dragonstone, did not return for the tourney), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father), Tyrion (her dwarf brother, absent; thought to have bought the dagger), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Ser Ilyn Payne (the king's tongueless executioner).
  • Lord Varys, "the Spider" – master of whisperers, eunuch; came to Ned at night in rough disguise by secret ways; warns the Lannisters tried to arrange Robert's death, says the Kingsguard is a "paper shield" (only Selmy is true; Boros and Meryn are the queen's creatures), tells Ned about the poison and Hugh; asks Ned to publicly keep treating him with contempt. Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – bet a hundred dragons on Jaime; wry; hints that Loras planned the mare. Grand Maester Pycelle, Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; old, honorable; stood vigil for Hugh; angered Robert by backing Ned), Ser Meryn, Ser Boros (Kingsguard, queen's creatures per Varys), Janos Slynt (City Watch commander), Balon Greyjoy (defeated rebel lord), Mad King Aerys II, Rhaegar Targaryen (his dead heir).
  • Thoros of Myr – red-robed priest with a flaming sword; won the melee. Anguy 🆕 new this chapter – commoner archer from the Dornish Marches who won archery, refused Ned's offer of a job. Moon Boy (king's fool), Ser Balon Swann, Jalabhar Xho (exiled Summer Isles prince), Lord Beric Dondarrion (young lord of the black lightning banner), Ser Aron Santagar (has a breastplate stretcher), Bryce Caron, Redwynes, Royces, other tourney knights.
  • Tobho Mott (armorer) and Gendry (his surly apprentice; Ned believes he is Robert's bastard).
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit at Castle Black), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit, Jon's friend), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.
On the road (Catelyn's party)
  • Catelyn and Ser Rodrik (traveling north); Tyrion and Yoren at the crossroads inn; Marillion (singer), Masha Heddle (innkeeper). From Ned's side, Catelyn's arrest of Tyrion is not yet known.
