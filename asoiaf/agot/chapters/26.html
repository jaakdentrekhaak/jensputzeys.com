Chapter 26 – Jon

At Castle Black, Jon defends a new, terrified recruit named Samwell Tarly from Ser Alliser's cruel drills, learns his sorrowful history, and rallies the other recruits to protect him.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – POV; Ned Stark's bastard, a Night's Watch recruit at Castle Black with his direwolf Ghost. Skilled swordsman who coaches the others; stood up to Ser Alliser to protect Sam and led the recruits into line (including a night-time threat to Rast). Dreams of an empty Winterfell and a descent into the crypts; worries about his missing uncle Benjen; now calls Sam, Grenn, Pyp, Halder and the rest his true brothers, feeling he never truly belonged with Robb and the others because of Catelyn.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Jon's father (publicly his uncle-figure; Jon is his bastard), Hand of the King in King's Landing, investigating Jon Arryn's death; distrusts Varys and Littlefinger. Arya (Jon's favorite sister, 11, in King's Landing; practicing balance with Syrio Forel); Sansa (elder sister, in King's Landing, betrothed to Joffrey); Bran (crippled by a fall but alive); Robb (acting lord of Winterfell); Rickon (youngest); Catelyn (Ned's wife, Jon's cold stepmother figure); Benjen (uncle, First Ranger, missing: rangers found only his blazes in the trees, which stop in the stony northwest highlands); Old Nan, Maester Luwin (Winterfell). Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady dead, Nymeria gone.
Winterfell household
  • Hodor (simple giant stableboy), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, Robb's friend), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Ser Rodrik (master-at-arms; taught Jon "know your foe"), Jory (captain of Ned's guard), Mikken (smith), Septon Chayle (librarian), Vayon Poole (steward), Alyn (Ned's man in King's Landing), Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn, in the Eyrie with her sickly son Robert Arryn), Jon Arryn (dead former Hand), Maester Colemon (Jon's maester, went with Lysa), Ser Hugh of the Vale (Jon Arryn's squire, now in King's Landing), Edmure (Catelyn's brother), Brynden (her uncle), Hoster Tully (her father).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – evasive; calls Jon Arryn's death natural; warns against Varys. Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – warned Ned of spies and pointed him to Jon Arryn's servants. Varys "the Spider" – eunuch with informers. Ser Barristan Selmy (white-haired Lord Commander of the Kingsguard), King Robert Baratheon (Ned's friend, fat and hard-drinking), Queen Cersei (Lannister), Prince Joffrey (12, heir), Tommen and Myrcella (younger royal children), Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" (Cersei's twin, Kingsguard), Tywin Lannister (their father, Warden of the West), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane (Joffrey's burned bodyguard), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless king's justice), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers).
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf who visited the Wall and Winterfell; Jon recalls his words that most men would rather deny a hard truth than face it, and that admitting cowardice takes a queer courage. Syrio Forel (Braavosi sword master, Arya's teacher), Balon Greyjoy (defeated rebel lord of the Iron Islands), Mad King Aerys II (overthrown by Robert).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Samwell Tarly "Sam" 🆕 new this chapter – hugely fat, gentle, tearful new recruit, about 15; eldest son of Lord Randyll Tarly, bannerman to Mace Tyrell of Highgarden, from Horn Hill (sigil a striding huntsman). Calls himself a coward; loves music, books, cooking and kittens, sickened by blood. His father despised him, favored a younger brother (Dickon), and gave him the choice of taking the black or dying in a "hunting accident" (and forced him to forsake the Tarly inheritance and the Valyrian steel greatsword Heartsbane). Fears cold, heights and fighting; Jon's first real friend.
  • Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; cruel; mocks Jon as "Lord Snow" and "the Bastard", nicknames Sam "Ser Piggy"/"Lord of Ham"; furious that the recruits now won't hurt Sam.
  • Jon's fellow recruits – Pyp (Pypar) – small, big-eared, ex-mummer and gifted mimic, loyal to Jon; Grenn – big, dull, loyal; Halder – 16, quarryman's son, strong and short-tempered, came around to Jon; Dareon – handsome would-be singer sent to the Wall for bedding a lord's daughter (Lord Rowan of Goldengrove); Toad, Jeren, Albett ("Pimple") 🆕 new this chapter – other recruits; Rast 🆕 new this chapter – cruel recruit ("the raper"), the lone holdout, cowed by Jon and Ghost.
  • Donal Noye – one-armed armorer/smith, rebuked Jon earlier. Maester Aemon – Castle Black's maester, praised Dareon's voice. Bowen Marsh (Lord Steward), Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" (sent rangers searching for Benjen), Ser Jaremy Rykker (led two searches), Quorin Halfhand 🆕 new this chapter (ranger from the Shadow Tower, searched too), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of Old Nan's tales; seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, Dany's adviser, son of Jeor), handmaids, bloodriders.
