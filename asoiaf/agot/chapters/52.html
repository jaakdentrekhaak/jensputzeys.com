Chapter 52 – Jon

Rangers find two of Benjen Stark's men dead near the Wall, with strange blue eyes and no blood or rot; news arrives that King Robert is dead and Ned imprisoned for treason, Jon attacks Ser Alliser at supper, and that night a dead man attacks Lord Commander Mormont's solar.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, sworn brother of the Night's Watch and the Lord Commander's steward. Told his father is imprisoned for treason, he refuses to believe it, though privately remembers Ned fathered him and never speaks of his mother; wants to ask Ned about her. Attacked Ser Alliser Thorne at supper after a taunt about "a traitor's bastard" (stopped by Sam, Pyp, Grenn and Toad) and was confined to his cell with Ghost and a guard. Then fought a walking corpse in Mormont's solar, cutting it up and setting it ablaze with a lamp and burning drapes. Dreams of the Winterfell crypts opening and the dead kings walking out.
  • Ghost – Jon's white direwolf, grown tall; led the party to the bodies, sensed the danger in the night, tore the corpse's arm and gut. The only animal unafraid of the corpses.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Jon's father, Lord of Winterfell and the late King's Hand; per a raven letter from Pycelle, imprisoned and charged with plotting with Robert's brothers to deny Joffrey the throne. Mormont plans to ask that he be allowed to take the black.
  • Sansa Stark and Arya Stark – Jon's half-sisters; Jon worries for them (Pycelle's letter does not mention them). Their direwolves: Lady (dead) and Nymeria (lost). Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, whom Jon does not call mother; he blames her for taking Tyrion captive. Robb, Bran, Rickon – Jon's half-brothers; Old Nan – the Winterfell storyteller whose tales of the Others he recalls. Direwolves Summer, Grey Wind. Ser Rodrik Cassel – Winterfell's old knight, mocked by Joffrey in the yard. Benjen Stark ("Ben") – Ned's brother, First Ranger, lost beyond the Wall nearly half a year ago with six men; Jon still clings to hope. Dead: Lyanna, Brandon.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead (word came by raven; Pyp says he was Ned's close friend). Joffrey – boy king, remembered by Jon as contemptuous of Robb and Ser Rodrik in the yard; Mormont expects him to heed his mother. Queen Cersei Lannister – his mother.
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – sent the letter to the Wall (Mormont knew him in his youth). Lord Stannis Baratheon and Ser Barristan Selmy – also known to Mormont; Lord Renly Baratheon, Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer", Tywin Lannister, Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger", Lord Varys, Janos Slynt (City Watch), the Kingsguard and Sandor "the Hound" Clegane are known only by name in the capital.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, a friend to Jon (visited the Wall), held captive by Catelyn; last seen freed in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the Stone Crows clan.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; gruff, shrewd, annoyed at the rangers' failures; praised Jon and Sam; told Jon his duty is now at the Wall and that "the things we love destroy us" (citing his own son, who sold poachers for a vain young wife); has a talking raven ("Corn!", "Burn!"). Ordered the woods searched ten leagues round for Benjen and any intruders; wants the corpses examined, not burned.
  • Samwell Tarly "Sam" – fat, timid brother serving as Maester Aemon's eyes; his father is Lord Randyll (made him watch animals being dressed); noticed that the corpses did not bleed or rot, and was praised by Mormont. Told Jon "you're my brother now, so he's my father too."
  • Ser Jaremy Rykker – First Ranger in Benjen's absence, irritable; thinks wildlings did the killings. Dywen – old forester with wooden teeth; noticed the dead men's blue eyes. Chett – steward in charge of the hounds, sneers at Sam. Bass 🆕 new this chapter – kennelmaster. Hake 🆕 new this chapter, Ketter 🆕 new this chapter – rangers. Othor 🆕 new this chapter and Jafer Flowers 🆕 new this chapter – Benjen's men found dead in the wood, with blue eyes, black hands, no blood or rot; Othor, a big ugly man who sang bawdy songs, rose and attacked in the solar; Jafer's body lies in a storeroom.
  • Maester Aemon – blind maester; to examine the corpses; Clydas – his aide, read the raven letter; Bowen Marsh – red-faced Lord Steward; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms, hostile to Jon, mocked him; Donal Noye – one-armed armorer, wished Jon well; Hobb – cook, gave Jon extra stew; Matthar (had the septon light a candle for Ned), Pyp, Grenn, Toad – Jon's fellow new brothers and friends; Dareon, Othell Yarwyck, Cotter Pyke, Septon Celladar. Jon's guard was found dead with his head twisted around.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others – old tales say they ride south on dead horses with hosts of the slain, hating fire, iron and sun; most men call it a story, but Jon and the rangers privately think of them at the sight of the corpses. Mance Rayder – wildling leader Ser Jaremy suspects; wildlings; children of the forest.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak; Khal Drogo – her Dothraki husband; Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. (Not mentioned this chapter.)

Places

Places
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress; the Lord Commander's Tower (solar with the raven; guarded day and night; Jon's cell is beneath), the common hall, the armory, the stables, a storeroom cut into the ice at the Wall's base where the corpses were taken. The Wall – "weeping" with meltwater in unseasonable warmth; a single horn blast means rangers returning.
  • The haunted forest – woods north of the Wall, a half day's ride from Castle Black, where the bodies were found; horses and dogs refused to go near them. "Spirit summer" 🆕 new this chapter – old men's name for the warm spell before winter; the summer has lasted ten years.
  • Winterfell (and its crypts, from Jon's dream), King's Landing, Vaes Dothrak.
