Chapter 17 – Bran

In a long dream of falling, guided by a three-eyed crow, Bran sees the wider world and finally flies, then wakes in his Winterfell bed with his direwolf at his side.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – POV; 7, Ned and Catelyn's fourth child. After the long coma he wakes in his tower bed, weak and dizzy, unable to move or feel his legs. In his dream a three-eyed crow taught him to fly, showed him the realm and said "winter is coming"; his father's voice told him one is only brave when afraid. A shining golden face in the dream said "The things I do for love"; the crow told him to forget it. Wakes calmly and knows his direwolf's name: Summer 🆕 new this chapter (large now, yellow eyes). Robb rushes in.
  • Robb Stark – 14, heir; acting master of Winterfell in Ned's absence, practises with real steel; reaches Bran's bedside as he wakes.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, Bran's mother; in Bran's dream she is on a galley on the Bite with Ser Rodrik, bound secretly for King's Landing with a bloodstained knife before her; a storm gathers ahead. Rickon – youngest (3), at Winterfell.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; with the royal party in the Riverlands. In the dream he pleads with the king. Killed Sansa's wolf Lady himself with his sword Ice, sending her body to Winterfell; cold toward Robert since.
  • Sansa Stark – 11; told the court "I don't remember" rather than back Arya against Joffrey; grieving for Lady (dead). Arya Stark – 9; her wolf Nymeria escaped and is hunted by the queen; Arya has yet to learn Mycah is dead.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, gone north to the Wall (Bran dreams him sleeping alone in a cold bed). Lyanna, Rickard, Brandon – Ned's dead sister, father, brother; Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin – the maester; in the dream studies the sky through a bronze tube. Hodor – simple, immensely strong stableboy. Mikken – smith. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward. Hallis Mollen – new captain of the guard. Old Nan – storyteller. Ser Rodrik Cassel – household knight, travelling with Catelyn. A black-haired serving woman 🆕 new this chapter (unnamed) was at Bran's bedside and ran shouting "He's awake."
  • With Ned: Jory Cassel (guard captain; found Arya), Vayon Poole (steward), Septa Mordane, Hullen (master of horse). Mycah – the butcher's boy, 13, Arya's friend, found dead, cut almost in half by the Hound.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); her son Robert Arryn is Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – at odds with Ned over the wolves; let the queen have Lady killed. Queen Cersei Lannister – demanded Arya be punished; offers a hundred golden dragons for Nymeria's skin. Prince Joffrey – crown prince, arm bandaged after Nymeria's bite; lied about the fight. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – the queen's twin, Kingsguard. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's burned bodyguard; killed Mycah. Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard. Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother. Ser Ilyn Payne – the mute King's Justice (executioner). Ser Raymun Darry – lord of the castle where the party is staying, a reluctant host who lost brothers fighting for Rhaegar.
  • Princess Myrcella, Tommen (younger royal children), Tywin Lannister, Maester Pycelle, Stannis, Littlefinger, Varys, Balon Greyjoy (mentioned court figures), Mad King Aerys II (dead), Rhaegar (dead), Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother, at the Wall.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother) and Yoren (black brother) – with Jon heading north; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders. The three-eyed crow 🆕 new this chapter – a dream guide who tells Bran to fly, shown to be (in the dream's end) the black-haired serving woman.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • Ser Raymun Darry's castle – small holding half a day's ride south of the Trident where the king's party (king's, Lannister, Darry and Stark men) is crowded in; tense. The audience chamber was taken by the king.
  • Winterfell – Bran's tower room (cold, window open); godswood with the great white weirwood and black pool; Luwin's balcony; Mikken's forge.
  • The Bite 🆕 new this chapter – bay where Catelyn's galley sails south.
  • The Trident – river where Robert killed Rhaegar; Joffrey's sword Lion's Tooth lies at the bottom. Searches of both banks found Arya.
  • The Neck, King's Landing (capital, destination), White Knife / White Harbor (Catelyn's route), The Wall / Castle Black (Watch base), Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone. In the dream Bran also sees the Free Cities, the Dothraki sea, Asshai and the far north beyond the Wall.
