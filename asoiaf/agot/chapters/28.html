Chapter 28 – Catelyn

Traveling north in disguise with Ser Rodrik, Catelyn weighs where to go from the crossroads inn, then is spotted there by Tyrion Lannister and publicly accuses him, calling on the lords' men present to seize him.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – POV; Ned's wife, traveling north on the kingsroad with Ser Rodrik, posing as a common father and daughter to avoid notice. Decided Riverrun (her bedridden father) and the Eyrie (dangerous mountain road; her sister Lysa may hold proof against the Lannisters) must wait; her path is to Winterfell and her sons. At the crossroads inn she is recognized by Tyrion; she accuses him before the room of conspiring at Winterfell to murder her son Bran and calls on the lords' men to seize him for the king's justice. Fears war is coming and wonders whether Tully bannermen would answer.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Catelyn's husband, Hand of the King in King's Landing, whom she has just left. Her children: Bran (crippled by his fall, at Winterfell), Robb (14, acting lord), Rickon (youngest), Arya and Sansa (with Ned in King's Landing); Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing), Lyanna (Ned's dead sister), Old Nan. Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady dead, Nymeria gone.
Winterfell household
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – aging, whiskered master-at-arms and Catelyn's companion; dislikes singers and tourneys; loosened his sword when she confronted Tyrion. Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard, in King's Landing), Alyn, Harwin, Varly, Jacks, Tomard, Desmond, Porther (Ned's guardsmen there); at Winterfell: Hodor (simple giant), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Maester Luwin, Mikken (smith), Septon Chayle (librarian), Vayon Poole (steward); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father, Lord of Riverrun, bedridden two years, still steadfast. Edmure Tully – her brother, dislikes singers. Brynden Tully (her uncle). Lysa Arryn (her sister, widow of Jon Arryn, in the Eyrie with son Robert Arryn), Jon Arryn (dead former Hand), Maester Colemon (his maester, with Lysa), Ser Hugh of the Vale (his former squire).
  • Lord Jason Mallister 🆕 new this chapter – lord of Seagard (silver eagle on indigo), Tully bannerman, riding to the tourney with son Patrek; didn't recognize Catelyn.
  • Tully bannerman houses mentioned: Blackwood and Bracken (ancient enemies; Bracken's sigil a red stallion; Jonos Bracken loyal), Lady Whent (last of her line, lives in Harrenhal; black bat sigil), Lord Walder Frey (very old, outlived seven wives, many descendants, late to the Trident, called "the Late Lord Frey"; plans to wed again on his ninetieth name day; twin towers sigil), Darry, Ryger, Mooton (fought for Rhaegar at the Trident).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, traveling south from Winterfell with two servants and Yoren; bought a room with gold at the crowded inn; greets Catelyn mockingly ("I was sorry to miss you at Winterfell"); now publicly accused by her and facing swords drawn by the room's men.
  • King Robert Baratheon (Ned's friend), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Prince Joffrey (12, heir), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" (Cersei's twin, Kingsguard; favored by gamblers), Tywin Lannister (their father, Warden of the West), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane (Joffrey's bodyguard), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless king's justice). Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" (tourney favorite). Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish (Master of coin; childhood fosterling at Riverrun; Catelyn recalls his mud pies with Lysa), Varys (eunuch master of whisperers), Grand Maester Pycelle, Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers; Stannis at Dragonstone), Janos Slynt (commander of the City Watch), Lord Beric Dondarrion (young lord, tourney entrant), Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard), Syrio Forel (Arya's sword teacher), Balon Greyjoy (defeated rebel lord), Mad King Aerys II, Rhaegar Targaryen (his dead heir, fought at the Trident).
  • Tobho Mott (armorer) and Gendry (his apprentice, bull-helmed bastard of the king) – smith's household in King's Landing.
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren – black brother and recruiter; accompanies Tyrion and sups with him at the inn. Jon Snow and Samwell Tarly (recruits at Castle Black), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Others on the road
  • Marillion 🆕 new this chapter – brash, vain young singer (about 18) with a woodharp, bound for the tourney; boasts of being close to Riverrun's court and mocks the Starks; sat with Catelyn and Rodrik. Masha Heddle 🆕 new this chapter – fat innkeeper with red sourleaf-stained teeth and a dreadful smile; runs the crossroads inn. A freerider sold Tyrion his room.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.
