Chapter 5 – Jon

At the welcoming feast for the king, Jon Snow, seated among the squires, asks his uncle Benjen to take him to the Wall, is rebuffed, and is then given blunt advice by Tyrion Lannister.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, 14 (15 on his next name day), POV. White, red-eyed, silent direwolf pup Ghost (named for that and his colour). Resents his bastardy; Lady Stark had him seated with the squires rather than the family at the feast. Sharp observer, good swordsman and rider, admires the "Young Dragon" Daeren Targaryen. Asked Benjen to take him to the Wall; Benjen would not commit and Jon fled the hall drunk and in tears, declaring he will never father a bastard. Does not know who his mother was.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North. Courteous at the feast but tense and silent; took the king to the crypts that afternoon, which the queen resented. Has been offered the Hand of the King and has not yet answered.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, of Riverrun; escorted by Robert at the feast. Seated Jon away from the royal family.
  • Robb Stark – eldest trueborn son and heir; escorted Princess Myrcella and is smitten. Sansa (11) escorted Joffrey, radiant. Arya escorted Tommen. Bran and Rickon (3) also present; the children sat with the royal children and could not bring their wolves. Each has a direwolf pup (all grey or black except Jon's).
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's younger brother, a man of the Night's Watch (sharp, gaunt, laughing blue-grey eyes), in black velvet. Kind to Jon but warns him that Watch brothers take no wives and father no sons. Said "we could use a man like you on the Wall". Notes direwolves still live beyond the Wall.
  • Rickard, Brandon and Lyanna Stark (all dead) – Ned's father, eldest brother and sister, entombed in the crypt. Howland Reed (mentioned) – "crannogman" present when Lyanna died.
Winterfell household
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward (hostage after Balon's rebellion), 19; ignores Jon.
  • Hullen – master of horse; praised Jon's riding. Harwin – his son. Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard. Ser Rodrik – household knight. Maester Luwin – Winterfell's maester; told Jon bastards grow up faster. Old Nan – storyteller. Desmond – guardsman.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Jon Arryn (dead) – former Hand and Lord of the Eyrie, Ned's and Robert's foster father. Lysa Arryn – his widow, Catelyn's sister, in the Eyrie with her sickly six-year-old son Robert Arryn, now Lord of the Eyrie. Brynden – Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate. Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father, Lord of Riverrun.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – huge, fat, red-faced, black-bearded, drinking and jesting heavily at the feast; to Jon, a disappointment compared with the legend of the "demon of the Trident". Has offered Ned the Hand.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – very beautiful (golden hair, green eyes, emerald tiara), cold and angry at the feast; never looked at Ned.
  • Prince Joffrey Baratheon – crown prince, 12, taller than Jon and Robb; blond curls, green eyes, pouty and disdainful; Jon dislikes him. Princess Myrcella 🆕 new this chapter – nearly 8, golden curls, shy and sweet on Robb. Prince Tommen – plump, white-blond, younger.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister – the queen's twin; tall, golden, green-eyed, with a knife-like smile; "the Lion of Lannister" to his face, "Kingslayer" behind his back. Impressively kingly to Jon.
  • Tyrion Lannister, "the Imp" – youngest of Lord Tywin's children; a dwarf with oversized head, one green and one black eye, near-white blond hair; witty, sardonic, acrobatic. Slipped out of the feast and spoke kindly and frankly to Jon, advising him to wear his bastardy as armor ("all dwarfs are bastards in their father's eyes"). His mother died bearing him, and Lord Tywin has "never been sure" he is truly his son.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – head of House Lannister at Casterly Rock; father of Cersei, Jaime and Tyrion.
  • Sandor Clegane – burned-faced rider in the royal party. Maester Pycelle – royal maester who tended Jon Arryn. Two Kingsguard knights – white-cloaked guards of the king.
  • Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar Targaryen (both dead) – the old king, whom Robert overthrew, and his son, killed by Robert at the Trident. Daeren Targaryen 🆕 new this chapter (dead, mentioned) – the "Young Dragon" / Boy King, conquered Dorne at 14 at great cost and died at 18.
  • Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands, beaten by Robert and Ned nine years ago.
The Night's Watch
  • Understrength; its men swear to take no wives and father no children. Benjen Stark (above). Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (rangers of the prologue; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown). Mormont – the Watch's old commander. Maester Aemon – maester at the Wall.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed beings who killed Royce. Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall. Wildlings – raiders north of the Wall.
House Targaryen (exiles in Pentos)
  • Daenerys Targaryen – 13, silver-haired; given to Khal Drogo by her brother Viserys, who calls himself king and is obsessed with retaking the Iron Throne. Ser Willem Darry – their dead guardian.
Pentos and Dothraki
  • Illyrio Mopatis – fat merchant-magister hosting the Targaryens. Khal Drogo – huge, undefeated Dothraki warlord. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight who sold poachers to slavers.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; its Great Hall hosted the king's welcoming feast. Its crypt holds Stark lords.
  • King's Landing – capital and seat of the Iron Throne.
  • The Wall / Castle Black – northern barrier manned by the Night's Watch, where Benjen serves.
  • Storm's End, The Eyrie / the Vale, Riverrun, Casterly Rock – seats of Baratheon, Arryn, Tully, Lannister.
  • The Trident – river where Robert killed Rhaegar. Dorne 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – southern land, briefly conquered by Daeren Targaryen.
  • Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone – Greyjoy home; Free City; Dothraki city; Targaryen island seat.
