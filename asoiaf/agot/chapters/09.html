Chapter 9 – Tyrion

Four days after Bran's fall, Tyrion slaps Prince Joffrey into showing Ned's family some courtesy, then breakfasts with Jaime, Cersei and her children, discussing Bran's slim chances and his own plan to visit the Wall.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – 7; fell from a tower four days ago and is in a sickroom, unconscious. His back is broken and his legs shattered; the maester thinks he may live (if he were going to die he would have already), and he is kept alive on honey and water. If he wakes he will never walk again. His direwolf howls beneath his window day and night; when the window was once closed Bran weakened, and when reopened his heart beat stronger. Still has no name for the wolf.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark and Catelyn Stark – Bran's grieving parents; the king sat up all night with Ned. Ned is still to go south as Hand; the departure is delayed.
  • Robb (14, heir), Sansa (11), Arya (9), Rickon (3) – Bran's siblings, with their wolves (Grey Wind, Lady, Nymeria, Shaggydog). The queen refuses to bring any direwolves south (Jaime says they follow the girls everywhere).
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, going to the Wall with Benjen; Tyrion calls him "his brother's bastard". White wolf Ghost.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother of the Night's Watch, returning to the Wall with Jon soon.
  • Rickard (Ned's father), Brandon (Ned's elder brother; Jaime notes he was one of the hostages murdered by the Targaryen king), Lyanna (Ned's sister) – all dead; Howland Reed (mentioned) – crannogman present at Lyanna's death.
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin – the maester, attends Bran. Ser Rodrik – household knight and master-at-arms. Jory Cassel – captain of the guard. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Hodor – simple stableboy. Old Nan – storyteller. Hullen – master of horse (Harwin his son). Mikken – smith. Beth Cassel – Rodrik's little daughter. Jeyne Poole – steward's daughter, Sansa's friend. Desmond – guardsman.
  • Chayle 🆕 new this chapter – young septon who looks after Winterfell's library tower; wears the crystal of his order on a silver chain.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); she has accused the Lannisters of murdering him. Her sickly son Robert Arryn is Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle (Knight of the Gate) and father (Lord of Riverrun).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Tyrion Lannister, "the Imp" – POV; dwarf, short twisted legs, clever, sardonic, a voracious reader who spent the night in Winterfell's library; sleeps little. Valued a child's affection only from Jaime. Slapped Joffrey twice for refusing to console the Starks and told him to kneel and apologise. Noticed a charged glance between Jaime and Cersei when he said Bran might wake, and tells Jaime he would like to hear what the boy has to say; Jaime warns him ("whose side you are on"), Tyrion smoothly answers that he loves his family. Plans to travel north with Benjen and Jon to see the Wall rather than go straight south.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister – Kingsguard knight, Cersei's twin, golden, lazy and lightly mocking; says he would kill the injured Bran as a mercy. Kind to Tyrion. Queen Cersei Lannister – cold and snobbish, wants out of Winterfell, finds the direwolves unnatural and "dangerous", dislikes Tyrion's crude jokes. She has called Bran's lingering "no mercy".
  • Prince Joffrey – 12; petulant, cruel, said "the Stark boy is nothing to me" and joked of killing the wolf; humiliated by Tyrion; fled the yard, sent to apologise to the Starks. Prince Tommen (7) – sweet, doesn't want Bran to die. Princess Myrcella – nearly 8, pretty, kind.
  • Sandor Clegane, "the Hound" 🆕 new this chapter – Joffrey's bodyguard, hideously burned, wears a snarling-dog helm; mocks Tyrion's height; offered to "silence" Bran's wolf. Warns that Joffrey will remember Tyrion's slap.
  • King Robert Baratheon – up all night with Ned, grieving for the Starks' sorrow.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – their father, head of House Lannister. Maester Pycelle – royal maester. Kingsguard – Ser Boros and Ser Meryn (the two white knights who came north); Ser Barristan Selmy, Lord Commander, waits at King's Landing. Stannis and Renly (mentioned) – Robert's brothers. Littlefinger (mentioned) – court figure the king might have named Hand.
  • Mad King Aerys II (overthrown), Rhaegar (his son; both dead); Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands, beaten by Robert.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon. Tyrion jokes about the Watch's celibacy and says he wants to see the Wall and "piss off the edge of the world".
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, given to Khal Drogo) and Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king); Illyrio Mopatis (fat Pentoshi magister); Khal Drogo (huge Dothraki warlord); Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight).

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat. Its library tower (steep exterior steps, Valyrian scrolls, maesters' books on seasons and war engines), the yard, the Guest House where the royal family eats, and the sickroom where Bran lies.
  • King's Landing (capital, where the party is bound), The Wall / Castle Black (Watch base, Tyrion's detour), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), The Trident, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
