Chapter 35 – Eddard

After finding a brothel connected to King Robert's bastard, Ned is ambushed in the rainy streets by Jaime Lannister and his men, who kill Ned's guards and leave him with a broken leg.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell; has resigned as Hand of the King ("I am no longer the King's Hand") but is still in King's Landing. Went to Chataya's brothel to see the place where Robert's bastard Barra was born; recalls promising Lyanna on her deathbed (a vow that cost him dearly) and telling a young mother he would carry her message to Robert. Openly told Jaime that Tyrion was seized on his order "to answer for his crimes." Thrown from his horse in the fight: leg badly broken (bone through the calf), carried unconscious back to the Red Keep at dawn; Grand Maester Pycelle gave him milk of the poppy.
  • Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard, wry and loyal; killed defending Ned in Jaime's ambush. Ned cradled his body.
  • Heward and Wyl – young Stark men-at-arms; both killed in the ambush (Heward speared, Wyl dragged off his horse).
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; holding Tyrion prisoner and, as Ned warned Jaime, a threat to Tyrion's life if Ned dies. Sansa and Arya (his daughters, in the Red Keep), Bran (crippled by a fall, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord), Rickon (youngest), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall; Ned sees his face, so like his own, in his thoughts), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves as before. Lyanna – Ned's late sister, once betrothed to Robert; remembered warning Ned that Robert would never keep to one bed (died; Ned made her promises as she lay dying).
Catelyn's party and captives
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – prisoner of Catelyn, last seen at the Eyrie; his brother Jaime is hunting him. Bronn (sellsword), Marillion (singer), Ser Rodrik Cassel (Winterfell's master-at-arms, wounded) as before.
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Lysa Arryn (widow of Jon Arryn, Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister), Robert Arryn (her sickly young son), Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle), Nestor Royce (High Steward of the Vale), Ser Donnel Waynwood (young knight), Mya Stone (mule guide), Maester Colemon (Eyrie maester), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Riverrun), Edmure (her brother); Jon Arryn (dead Hand; Ned believes he was murdered because of what he learned about the king's baseborn children).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin and Tyrion's brother, Kingsguard knight, golden knight in gilded armor; rode into the rain with about twenty Lannister guards seeking his brother Tyrion; drew steel on Ned ("I'll butcher you like Aerys if I must"), but backed off when Ned warned Catelyn would kill Tyrion; told Tregar to see that no harm came to Ned, then ordered Ned's men killed. His gilded sword is the one that killed Aerys, "the last of the Dragonkings".
  • Tregar – Jaime's Lannister guard captain; his helm was split by Ned's sword.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – lord, master of coin, glib, mocking; led Ned to the brothel; offered jokes about buying brothels; fled when Jaime told him to leave, returned with the City Watch (gold cloaks). Says Robert has several bastards: the acknowledged boy at Storm's End (born the night Stannis wed, mother a Florent maid of Lady Selyse; sent by Stannis to Renly) and rumored twin babies by a serving girl at Casterly Rock, killed on Cersei's order with their mother sold to a slaver (rumor only).
  • Barra 🆕 new this chapter – baby girl, King Robert's bastard, with dark hair like his, born at Chataya's brothel; her red-haired young mother (unnamed, freckled, hoping Robert will return) asked Ned to carry word to Robert; Ned promised Barra "shall not go wanting."
  • Chataya 🆕 new this chapter – tall, elegant black-skinned woman who runs the brothel to which Littlefinger led Ned.
  • King Robert (lecherous; Ned thinks he has trained himself to look away from unpleasant things), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father) ("quite vexed" about Tyrion per Jaime), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly (king's youngest brother), Stannis (king's middle brother, wed to Selyse Florent), Grand Maester Pycelle (old maester, tended Ned), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard lord commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) — at court, as before. Rhaegar Targaryen – the dead prince from the rebellion, whom Ned recalls.
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Syrio Forel (Arya's Braavosi dancing master), Vayon Poole (steward), Hodor (simple giant stableboy), Maester Luwin, Old Nan, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Mikken (smith), and others, as before.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, new brother), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.

Places
  • Chataya's brothel 🆕 new this chapter – high-class brothel in King's Landing; Robert's bastard Barra was born there.
  • The streets of King's Landing – rainy night; site of the ambush; the City Watch (gold cloaks) arrive late.
  • The Red Keep – royal castle, pale pink stone; Ned is carried back there.
