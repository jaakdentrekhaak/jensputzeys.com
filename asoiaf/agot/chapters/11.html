Chapter 11 – Daenerys

Daenerys is married to Khal Drogo in a violent Dothraki ceremony outside Pentos, receives gifts including three dragon eggs and a silver filly, and rides out with Drogo for the wedding night, which is gentle and consensual.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, going south as Hand to King Robert. Catelyn Stark – his wife, at the bedside of her comatose son Bran (7, back broken, legs shattered, still unconscious; his direwolf howls under his window). Robb (14, heir), Sansa (11, to marry Prince Joffrey), Arya (9, given the sword Needle by Jon), Rickon (3) – their other children, with direwolves Grey Wind, Lady, Nymeria, Shaggydog.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, riding north to the Wall with his uncle Benjen Stark (Night's Watch); white wolf Ghost. Other family: Rickard, Brandon, Lyanna (Ned's dead father, brother, sister); Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin (maester), Ser Rodrik (household knight), Jory Cassel (guard captain), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Septa Mordane (girls' septa), Mikken (smith), Hodor (simple stableboy), Old Nan (storyteller), Chayle (young septon-librarian), Hullen (master of horse), Harwin (his son).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, who accuses the Lannisters of murdering her husband Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); her sickly son Robert Arryn is Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle (Knight of the Gate) and father (Lord of Riverrun).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – the king, "the Usurper" to the Targaryens. Queen Cersei Lannister – his wife. Ser Jaime Lannister – her twin, Kingsguard. Prince Joffrey – crown prince, cruel, humiliated by Tyrion. Tommen, Myrcella – the younger royal children. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's burned bodyguard. Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's clever dwarf brother, going north to see the Wall.
  • Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – Lannister head at Casterly Rock; Maester Pycelle – royal maester; Kingsguard – Ser Boros and Ser Meryn (with the party), Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander, at King's Landing); Stannis, Renly (mentioned) – Robert's brothers; Littlefinger (mentioned) – court figure; Mad King Aerys II, Rhaegar (dead) – the overthrown king and his son; Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother); Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen (Dany, "Daenerys Stormborn", Khaleesi) – POV; 13, silver-blond, lilac eyes; sold by her brother to Drogo and now his wife. Terrified all day, repeating "I am the blood of the dragon"; saw men die at the feast. Had a nightmare of Viserys beating her and a dragon burning him. Won the khal's first smile by riding her silver filly over a firepit ("he has given me the wind"). On the wedding night Drogo was patient and gentle, and she consented. They share no language (she has no Dothraki; he knows only a little bastard Valyrian and no Common Tongue; "No" and "Yes" suffice).
  • Viserys Targaryen – her older brother, calls himself the rightful king ("the dragon does not beg"); impatient for the crown Drogo promised for her hand; bitter at sitting below her; threatened her to "please" Drogo or "wake the dragon". Slighted at the feast.
  • Khal Drogo – huge, impassive Dothraki warlord, braid hung with silver bells; leader of a khalasar of forty thousand; now Dany's husband, mostly silent but tender toward her. His bloodriders Haggo 🆕 new this chapter, Cohollo 🆕 new this chapter and Qotho 🆕 new this chapter gave her a whip, an arakh and a dragonbone bow (she ritually refused them, so Drogo took them in her stead).
  • Magister Illyrio Mopatis – fat, forked-beard Pentoshi magister hosting the exiles; arranged the match and says the khal will give Viserys a crown after Vaes Dothrak, if omens favour war; gave Dany three petrified dragon eggs (green/bronze, cream/gold, black/scarlet) from beyond Asshai.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight (wanted by the king for selling poachers to slavers), sworn to Viserys, counsels patience; has been to Vaes Dothrak; gave Dany books of Westerosi histories and songs.
  • Irri 🆕 new this chapter and Jhiqui 🆕 new this chapter – Dothraki handmaids (Irri teaches riding, Jhiqui Dothraki). Doreah 🆕 new this chapter – Lysene handmaid, to teach her the arts of love. Viserys gave them to Dany (Illyrio supplied them).

Places

Places
  • Pentos – Free City across the narrow sea; the wedding takes place in a field outside its walls; magisters have doubled the city guard because of the khalasar.
  • Vaes Dothrak – Dothraki city with Drogo's palace; after the wedding Drogo must present Dany to the dosh khaleen there (the dosh khaleen 🆕 new this chapter are the Dothraki crones who sit there).
  • Asshai / the Shadow Lands 🆕 new this chapter – far east, source of the dragon eggs.
  • Winterfell (Stark seat), The Wall / Castle Black (Watch base), King's Landing (capital, Iron Throne), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), The Trident, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Dragonstone (Targaryen island seat, Dany's birthplace).
