Chapter 33 – Eddard

At a council meeting, King Robert demands the killing of the pregnant Daenerys Targaryen, Ned refuses to be part of it and resigns as Hand, and Littlefinger visits him afterward with a mocking offer.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – no longer Hand: laid his silver hand badge on the table and was ordered out by Robert, who threatened his head on a spike. Plans to leave quickly with his daughters and a few guards, perhaps by ship via Dragonstone to question Stannis. Believes Jon Arryn was murdered, though he has only "spoor", not the truth; suspects Stannis shares Jon's secret. Knows (from Yoren, last night) that Catelyn has taken Tyrion. Unsure whether Robert is part of the plot. Holds the dagger.
  • Sansa Stark (elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey) and Arya Stark (younger daughter) – to travel with him. Bran (crippled, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord), Rickon (youngest) (Winterfell), Catelyn (his wife, traveling with Tyrion as prisoner), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (his brother, missing), Lyanna (dead sister, Robert's lost love). Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady (killed), Nymeria (gone).
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Vayon Poole – Winterfell steward; told to arrange departure secretly and find a fast ship. Jory Cassel – guard captain, still searching for a brothel; only he is to be told the plan. Tomard, Desmond, Harwin, Alyn, Porther, Fat Tom – Ned's guardsmen. Syrio Forel (Arya's Braavosi dancing master), Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Jeyne Poole (Sansa's friend), Ser Rodrik (master-at-arms, with Catelyn); at Winterfell: Hodor (simple giant), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Maester Luwin, Old Nan, Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (guard captain); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Jon Arryn (dead former Hand; Ned believes him murdered; Renly says Robert erred by listening to him about the Targaryen children), Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister, in the Eyrie), Robert Arryn (her sickly son), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father), Edmure (her brother), Brynden (her uncle), Walder Frey (old river lord), Roose Bolton 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned: a lord who at the Trident urged cutting the wounded Selmy's throat).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – furious at news that Daenerys is pregnant; wants her, her child and Viserys dead; sees himself threatened by a returning Targaryen; unbends when Ned resists, throws the wine flagon, banishes Ned. Cites the Targaryens as dragonspawn. Earlier admitted he regrets being king.
  • Lord Varys – brought the news (from Ser Jorah Mormont's reports) that the princess is pregnant; publicly agrees she must die ("Sadly, sadly") and suggests poison (the tears of Lys) after saying a blade is death near Vaes Dothrak; will let it be known that whoever kills Daenerys will be made a lord. Lord Renly Baratheon – cold-blooded, says they should have killed Viserys and Dany years ago. Ser Barristan Selmy – stood with Ned: no honor in killing a child in the womb. Grand Maester Pycelle – urges killing "so that tens of thousands might live"; suggested the Faceless Men of Braavos; had sent Ned's letter by raven to Stannis asking him to return to council (no reply). Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – urged "a steel kiss"; complained the Faceless Men are too costly; afterward tells Ned he did Daenerys "more good" by preventing them; is dining with Lady Tanda who wants him to wed her younger daughter; offers to take Ned to the brothel Jory has sought; says Ned rules "like a man dancing on rotten ice".
  • Ser Boros Blount (Kingsguard, at the council door). Queen Cersei (Robert's Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Tywin Lannister (her father), Jaime (her twin, Kingsguard), Tyrion (their dwarf brother, Catelyn's captive), Stannis (Robert's brother, at Dragonstone), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), Sandor and Gregor Clegane (brother knights), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless executioner), Ser Meryn (Kingsguard), Janos Slynt (City Watch commander), Lancel (Robert's squire, Lannister cousin), Tobho Mott and Gendry (armorer and his apprentice, a bastard of the king). Lady Tanda 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned, hosts Littlefinger).
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren (black brother and recruiter; warned Ned last night about the Catelyn and Tyrion business), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen – 14 years old per Ned, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, said to be nearing Vaes Dothrak. Viserys (her exiled brother), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight at her side; sends Varys reports; craves a royal pardon, called a traitor by Ned), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), handmaids, bloodriders. Rhaegar and Aerys (her dead brother and father, the former heir and the Mad King; Robert hates Rhaegar still).
On the road (Catelyn's party)
  • Catelyn and Ser Rodrik with Tyrion (prisoner), sellswords Bronn and Chiggen, Marillion (singer), Ser Willis Wode, Lharys, Morrec (Tyrion's servant), in the mountains.
Overheard conspirators (known only to Arya)
  • A stout scarred man in a steel cap and a fat man with a forked yellow beard (see Arya's chapter; unidentified).
