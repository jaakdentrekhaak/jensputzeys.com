Chapter 50 – Arya

During a lesson in the Red Keep, Arya is sent by Syrio Forel to flee when Ser Meryn Trant and Lannister guardsmen come to take her, then escapes through a castle of dead Stark men and goes back to the dungeon with the dragon skulls to find a secret way out.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Arya Stark – Ned's younger daughter, tomboy with a sword called Needle (Jon's gift). Syrio's lessons ("Fear cuts deeper than swords", "swift as a deer, quiet as a shadow") guide her. Killed a stableboy who tried to seize her for the queen; now wearing a heavy cloak over Needle, carrying a roll of clothes, two stolen prayer candles, heading through the dungeon of the dragon skulls ("the monsters") to find a hidden way out of the castle. Wants to get home to Winterfell.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand. Arya has heard fighting at the Tower of the Hand and been told by the stableboy "father's dead" (unconfirmed to her; at the end of the previous chapter he was held at dagger-point by Littlefinger after the throne-room betrayal, which Arya did not witness); his door was broken in and a Stark guard lies dead on the steps.
  • Sansa Stark – elder daughter, besotted with Joffrey; was to sail with Arya. Septa Mordane – their septa.
  • Stark men: Hullen – Winterfell's master of horse, stabbed to death at the stable, told Arya to warn her father; Desmond – household guard, dead in the stable (had promised to protect Ned); a groom Arya knew, and two other guards also dead. Earlier dead: Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl. Tomard, Varly, Cayn – killed in the throne room. Porther, Alyn, Harwin – other Stark men. Vayon Poole – steward who arranged the ship.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon (youngest, small boy), Jon Snow (sworn brother of the Watch; gave Arya Needle and her first lesson: stick them with the pointy end); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; dead Lyanna, Brandon and the Starks' grandfather (tombs in the Winterfell crypts). Ser Rodrik Cassel. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan (tells tales of spiders and rats), Mikken, Hallis Mollen.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead of the boar wound; his will named Ned regent.
  • Joffrey – golden-haired boy, proclaimed king on the Iron Throne; son of Cersei and (to Ned's knowledge) Jaime, not Robert. Queen Cersei Lannister – tore up Robert's will, branded Ned a traitor; her men now hunt Arya. Myrcella, Tommen – her younger children.
  • Ser Meryn Trant – Kingsguard knight with droopy eyes and rust-colored whiskers, who came to Winterfell with the king; led five Lannister guardsmen to seize Arya "for her father", and in white plate cut Syrio's wooden sword in two (Syrio's fate unseen by Arya, who believes he was killed).
  • Lannister guardsmen (red cloaks, lion-crest helms) – five sent with Trant; Syrio killed or crippled them all. Gold cloaks – City Watch under Janos Slynt, now manning the walls and patrolling; some know Arya by sight.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; held Ned's dagger at his throat. Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers. Grand Maester Pycelle. Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard.
  • Kingsguard: Ser Boros Blount, Ser Preston Greenfield, Ser Arys Oakheart; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's sworn shield, killed Cayn; Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin lover, fled the city for his father; Lord Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock; Lancel (Robert's squire, queen's cousin), Ser Balon Swann. Lord Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother, fled with Ser Loras Tyrell. Lord Stannis Baratheon – elder brother at Dragonstone, named by Ned as true heir. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" (Sandor's huge brother, sentenced for massacres), Lord Beric Dondarrion and Thoros of Myr (sent against him), Ser Ilyn Payne (mute King's Justice), Bronze Yohn Royce, Jon Arryn (dead former Hand), Yoren, Robert's bastards Gendry, Mya, Barra.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister, and her sickly son Robert Arryn; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle; Hoster Tully (lord) and Ser Edmure Tully (his son, defending the border) at Riverrun.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the hill clan Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, newly sworn, steward to the Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly – his fat bookish friend, Maester Aemon's assistant; Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Maester Aemon – blind, aged maester; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Bowen Marsh (Lord Steward), Othell Yarwyck (First Builder), Ser Jaremy Rykker (leads the rangers), Grenn, Pyp, Toad, Dareon, Chett, Cotter Pyke; Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, missing First Ranger. A severed hand was found beyond the Wall.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak; Khal Drogo – her Dothraki warlord husband; Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her; handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master, former First Sword of the Sealord of Braavos, bald, teaches Arya "water dancing" (watch with your eyes, "the first sword of Braavos does not run"); held off the Lannister guards with a wooden sword and stayed to face Ser Meryn; likely dead. Captain Qos – master of the Wind Witch.
  • Stableboy – killed by Arya. Unnamed cooks, a baker's helper and a butcher in the Red Keep kitchens.

Places

Places
  • The Red Keep / King's Landing – royal castle; currently in lockdown, gates closed and walls manned by gold cloaks, doors barred, courtyards deserted. The Small Hall – hall where Syrio gave lessons. The kitchens/buttery, the cellar (dead end with tall ale casks and a high slanting window), the stables, the royal sept (holds prayer candles), the Tower of the Hand – Ned's quarters, door smashed in.
  • The dragon-skull dungeon – dark vaults below the castle where Arya once found huge skulls ("monsters"); she plans to use it to reach another way out. The throne room, the Iron Throne.
  • Winterfell – Arya's home and goal, with the crypts below (statues of the Kings of Winter). The kingsroad, Braavos (Syrio's home), Dragonstone, Casterly Rock, the Eyrie, Riverrun, Castle Black / the Wall.
