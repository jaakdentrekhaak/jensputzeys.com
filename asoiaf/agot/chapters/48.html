Chapter 48 – Jon

Jon's training class is assigned to Night's Watch orders, Jon is made the Lord Commander's steward and sulks before Sam talks him round, and they swear their vows in a weirwood grove beyond the Wall, where Ghost brings back a severed hand.

Characters

The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, Winterfell-raised; best swordsman and rider of the recruits. Furious at being assigned to the stewards (he suspects Ser Alliser's spite) but talked round by Sam; now personal steward to the Lord Commander, sleeping in a cell beneath Mormont's chambers. Took his vow before the old gods in the grove. His white direwolf Ghost (red eyes, like the weirwoods) fetched back a human hand.
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, timid, kind brother from Horn Hill, whose father came to prefer his brother Dickon as heir; passed out of training, now assistant to Maester Aemon in the rookery and library; took Chett's cell near the maester. Argued that serving Mormont is like a lord's heir attending him; follows the old gods now ("the Seven never answered my prayers").
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander, bald and bear-like; gave the recruits' speech about leaving old loyalties behind. Requested Jon as his steward. Maester Aemon – blind, very old maester; calls Jon a Watchman rather than a servant. Clydas – his aide. Ser Alliser Thorne – cold, grim master-at-arms who dislikes Jon. Bowen Marsh – plump Lord Steward who led them beyond the Wall. Othell Yarwyck 🆕 new this chapter – lantern-jawed First Builder. Ser Jaremy Rykker – commands the rangers while Benjen is missing. Septon Celladar 🆕 new this chapter – sept's septon, usually drunk. Dywen 🆕 new this chapter – gnarled old forester who dislikes the smell of the night. Cotter Pyke (mentioned) – commander at Eastwatch; Borcas 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – steward there. Chett – moved to the kennels. Hobb – cook. Benjen Stark – First Ranger, missing; once told Jon the Wall gives you only what you earn. Ser Waymar Royce, Gared (executed).
  • New brothers (sworn this evening): Grenn (strong, slow; to the rangers), Pyp (Pypar) (jokester with big ears; rangers), Toad (Todder) (rangers), Matthar (rangers), Haider (builders), Albett (builders), Dareon (a singer; stewards, posted to Eastwatch; bitter, says a girl once pulled him through a window). Rast – bullying recruit from the earlier training scenes; not among those assigned this chapter.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Jon's father, King's Hand in King's Landing; leg shattered when Jaime's men ambushed him (Jory, Wyl and Heward were killed). Named Protector and regent in the dying Robert's will; has learned that Cersei's children are Jaime's, not Robert's; wrote to Stannis to claim the throne and has asked Littlefinger to win the City Watch. Is sending his daughters home on the Wind Witch. (Jon knows none of this.)
  • Sansa Stark, Arya Stark – his daughters, in King's Landing (Arya overheard two unnamed men in the dungeon tunnels discussing a Hand's death, delay and the "wolf and lion"; Ned dismissed it as mummers). Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; last seen at the Eyrie, planning to leave for Winterfell with Ser Rodrik and Brynden. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon; direwolves Summer, Grey Wind; dead Lyanna, Brandon. Ser Rodrik Cassel. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha (captured wildling woman brought back to Winterfell after Robb and the direwolves killed her band, two of them Watch deserters), Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen. Ned's household in King's Landing: guards Tomard "Fat Tom" (commands them, will escort the girls), Cayn, Desmond, Porther, Varly, Harwin, Alyn (rode with Lord Beric); dead Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl; Septa Mordane, Jeyne Poole, Vayon Poole (steward).
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – mortally gored by a boar, dying, sleeping on the poppy. Queen Cersei Lannister – lover of her twin Jaime; her children are his. Prince Joffrey – heir; Tommen, Myrcella – her other children. Lord Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother, offered Ned swords to seize the children. Lord Stannis Baratheon – elder brother at Dragonstone; heir if Joffrey is a bastard.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; agreed to bribe the City Watch for Ned. Janos Slynt – Commander of the City Watch. Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers. Grand Maester Pycelle. Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard. Other Kingsguard: Ser Boros Blount, Ser Preston Greenfield, Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Arys Oakheart. Knights at court: Ser Balon Swann, Ser Robar Royce (found the wounded king) and his father Bronze Yohn Royce. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled the city. Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock. Lancel – Robert's squire, queen's cousin. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" (huge; his raiders burned Sherrer, Wendish Town and the Mummer's Ford) and Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (Gregor burned his face as a child; won the tourney); Lord Beric Dondarrion, Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar – sent against Gregor. Ser Hugh of the Vale (Jon Arryn's squire, killed by Gregor's lance in the tourney before Ned could question him; Varys implies he poisoned Arryn), Ser Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), Ser Ilyn Payne, Lady Tanda, Redwyne twins, Barra, Mya, Gendry (Robert's bastards), Jon Arryn (dead Hand), Yoren (Watch recruiter).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, holding the riverlands against the Lannisters (Lords Vance and Piper guard the pass below the Golden Tooth); Hoster Tully (bedridden). Ser Raymun Darry, Ser Karyl Vance, Ser Marq Piper – river-lords' knights who asked the throne's leave to answer the raids; Joss (brewer, Sherrer survivor). Bracken men-at-arms and Ser Willis Wode (Lady Whent's knight) helped Catelyn arrest Tyrion.
  • Lysa Arryn – at the Eyrie, ordered Tyrion tried; Robert Arryn (sickly boy, wants to "see him fly"). Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – quit as Knight of the Gate to go to Riverrun, agreeing to travel with Catelyn. Others at the Eyrie: Maester Colemon, Lord Nestor Royce, Ser Lyn Corbray, Lord Hunter, Mya Stone (Robert's bastard, mule guide), Mord (cruel turnkey), Ser Vardis Egen (killed by Bronn in the trial by combat), Marillion (singer).
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – abducted by Catelyn, accused by Lysa of Jon Arryn's murder and Bran's attempted murder; his sellsword champion Bronn won the trial by combat; now freed and riding the high road with Bronn, facing Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, children of the forest, Mance Rayder, wildlings – Osha's band near Winterfell included Watch deserters, and she mentioned fleeing Mance and white walkers; Sam and Jon believe wildlings have never dared come close to the Wall. The owner of the severed hand Ghost brought back is unknown.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – in Vaes Dothrak, pregnant; ate a raw stallion's heart and the dosh khaleen prophesied her son, named Rhaego, as "the stallion who mounts the world". King Robert's council ordered her killed, then he revoked it on his deathbed (Varys says it may be too late). Viserys – killed by Drogo, who poured molten gold over his head after he drew a sword in the sacred city. Khal Drogo, bloodriders Qotho, Haggo, Cohollo; Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, Dany's sworn sword; also the informant who reports on Dany to Varys, which Dany does not know); Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah; Illyrio Mopatis; dosh khaleen; Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo; Khals Jommo and Ogo.
Other
  • Captain Qos – master of the Wind Witch. Syrio Forel – Arya's Braavosi dancing master. Grand Maester Malleon – lineage book author. Lady Ashara (mentioned). Rhaegar's children (mentioned).

Places

Places
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress at the Wall; has a sept with a crystal window (no godswood). The Wall – giant ice barrier, three hundred miles long, melting in the sun; has no gates, only a twisting tunnel with three iron barred gates. The haunted forest – forest beyond the Wall, darker and more ominous than the wolfswood. The weirwood grove 🆕 new this chapter – half a league north, nine weirwoods with carved faces; where the vows were said. Eastwatch – Watch castle on the coast, trading with merchant galleys. The Eyrie – Arryn seat high in the Vale, with the Moon Door and sky cells; the Bloody Gate, Gates of the Moon, Golden Tooth (pass below which the Tully host gathers). Horn Hill (mentioned) – Tarly seat.
