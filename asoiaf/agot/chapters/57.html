Chapter 57 – Sansa

At Joffrey's first court session decrees are read naming Tywin Hand and Janos Slynt a lord, Ser Barristan is humiliatingly dismissed from the Kingsguard, and Sansa kneels to beg mercy for her father, who Joffrey says must first confess.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi" – exiled Targaryen princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo; two days after a poisoning attempt she left Vaes Dothrak with his khalasar, riding south and west; expects a son she calls Rhaego; put three dragon's eggs in a brazier with no visible result.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord; vowed after the attempt to cross the sea and take the Seven Kingdoms for his son. Bloodriders Cohollo (oldest), Qotho (cruel), Haggo (huge, silent).
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island, Dany's adviser, stopped the poisoner. Handmaids Doreah (Lysene), Irri, Jhiqui; young Dothraki guards Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo, Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, still betrothed to Joffrey, who has given her "freedom of the castle" (always with escorts; the gates are watched by gold cloaks). Dressed in black-dyed ivory mourning silk; courtiers shun her. Begged Joffrey on her knees for her father's mercy, blaming his broken leg and milk of the poppy for his denial of Joffrey's right; Joffrey agreed only if Ned confesses and names him king, and she trusts Ned will. Joffrey says she must be good; she still believes he loves her.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, formerly King's Hand; held as a traitor in a dungeon under the Red Keep, replaced as Hand by Tywin. Joffrey wants his confession.
  • Arya Stark – younger daughter, named in the summons list; Sansa hopes this means she escaped by galley and is safe at Winterfell (whereabouts actually unknown). Bran Stark – crippled second son, left as lord in Winterfell. Rickon – youngest son, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall.
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son, has called the banners and leads about eighteen thousand northmen from Moat Cailin; named a traitor in Joffrey's decrees. Wolf Grey Wind. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; went to Riverrun to her father, who may be dying; also named in the decrees.
  • Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan of Winterfell), Maester Luwin (the castle's maester), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb), Hodor (simple, huge stableboy who says only "Hodor"), Osha (captured wildling woman, kept in irons), Old Nan (storyteller), Mikken (blacksmith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard). Dead: Jory Cassel (Ned's guard captain), Hullen (master of horse), Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn (Stark guardsmen); Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Other wolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog (Rickon).
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton (pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort, flayed-man sigil), the Greatjon (Lord Umber) (giant, loud lord), Rickard Karstark (sunburst), Galbart and Robett Glover (red fist), Ser Helman Tallhart (three sentinel trees), Lord Hornwood, Maege Mormont (Lady Mormont, black bear), Lord Cerwyn – northern lords with Robb. Manderlys of White Harbor: Ser Wylis and Ser Wendel (fat sons of Lord Wyman) with about 1,500 men. Howland Reed (mentioned) – little crannogman, Ned's old comrade at Greywater Watch.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, son of Cersei; held his first court session, wearing a crown of rubies and black diamonds; cruel and petulant (called Barristan's defiance "boy" and ordered him seized). Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – now on the small council, in effect rules; calls Sansa "daughter-to-be". Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Decrees (ch57): Tywin Lannister named Hand of the King in place of the "traitor" Ned; Cersei seated in place of "traitor" Stannis; Janos Slynt (frog-faced, balding City Watch commander, a commoner) raised to lord, given Harrenhal, a bloody-spear sigil and a council seat (nobles resent him; two ugly sons). Grand Maester Pycelle reads the decrees; Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin, mocks Barristan.
  • Kingsguard: Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed as Lord Commander by Cersei and Joffrey (for letting Robert die; "too old"), after serving three kings since his twenty-third year; threw down cloak, armor and sword in contempt at Jaime's appointment and left; Joffrey ordered him seized by Slynt's gold cloaks. Ser Jaime Lannister named the new Lord Commander. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – made a Kingsguard in his place (refusing knight's vows). Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart – the remaining Kingsguard in the hall.
  • Lords at court: Jalabhar Xho (black-skinned exile prince from the Summer Isles), Ser Aron Santagar (gloomy), the Redwyne twins Horror and Slobber, sickly Lord Gyles 🆕 new this chapter (feigns coughing), drunken Ser Dontos 🆕 new this chapter (funny; tries to greet Sansa), Ser Balon Swann; only about twenty attend, where a hundred once did, and they shun Sansa. Summoned to swear fealty or be named traitors: Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone, King Robert's brothers), Ser Loras Tyrell, Lord Mace Tyrell, Doran Martell 🆕 new this chapter (Prince of Dorne), the Starks, Lysa Arryn, the Tullys, Mallister, Blackwood, Frey, Vance, Bracken, Whent, Caron, Royce, Beric and Thoros.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Tyrion's and Cersei's father, now Hand of the King; Lord of Casterly Rock and Warden of the West, bald with golden side-whiskers; cold, never smiles. Commands about twenty thousand men at the crossroads inn on the Green Fork; has driven the Tully lords from the field, taken Harrenhal, and plans to march against Robb, then deal with Stannis. Reads Ned's capture as ending the Stark threat.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, freed from the Eyrie's cells (Catelyn's captive until then), now at his father's camp with about three hundred hill clansmen he bought with promises of silk, steel and horses; they will ride with Tywin only if Tyrion goes with them, his life forfeit until they hold their steel.
  • Ser Kevan Lannister – Tywin's brother and chief lieutenant (portly, favors patience). Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tyrion's brother and Cersei's twin; now Lord Commander of the Kingsguard by decree; beat the Tullys under Riverrun, captured Ser Edmure and besieges the castle. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – Lannister knight who burned out the Pipers and Brackens. Ser Addam – Lannister commander told to harass Robb's flanks. Ser Flement Brax – knight who greeted Tyrion. Bannermen in camp: Presters, Crakehall, Marbrand, Lydden.
  • Bronn – sellsword who follows Tyrion. Brorm – rides with Tyrion's party.
  • The hill clans (Mountains of the Moon): Shagga son of Dolf (huge Stone Crow), Conn son of Coratt and Gunthor son of Gurn (Stone Crows), Chella daughter of Cheyk (Black Ears woman, scout), Ulf son of Umar (speaks for the Moon Brothers), Timett son of Timett (one-eyed Burned Man, young war chief); also Torrek, Jaggot.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, captive of the Lannisters. Lord Hoster Tully – their father, at besieged Riverrun, possibly dying. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, with Catelyn. Lord Blackwood – led survivors back to Riverrun; Lady Whent – yielded Harrenhal after Raventree fell. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance – young riverlords raiding Tywin's rear. Jason Mallister – holds Seagard. Walder Frey – gathering levies at the Twins (Tywin says he fights only when victory is certain). Beric Dondarrion – young lord harassing Lannister foragers, with Thoros, a red priest of Myr who sets his sword aflame. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned at the Mummer's Ford.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander; confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a dead man in Mormont's solar. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander. Samwell Tarly – fat, bookish recruit, Jon's friend. Maester Aemon – blind old maester. Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh master-at-arms. Ser Jaremy Rykker – ranger who led sweeps for Benjen. Bowen Marsh – round Lord Steward. Donal Noye – one-armed armorer. Pyp, Grenn, Toad – Jon's fellow recruits. Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers), wights (animated dead), children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings, giants.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

  • Riverrun – Tully seat, under siege; Raventree; Seagard; the Twins; the Golden Tooth; the Red Fork, the Green Fork, the Trident; Moat Cailin – ruined stronghold at the Neck where Robb mustered; White Harbor; Casterly Rock – Lannister seat.
