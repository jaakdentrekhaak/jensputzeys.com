Chapter 19 – Jon

At Castle Black, Jon is trained and bullied, is lectured by the armorer Donal Noye about how his pride has made enemies, and then learns from a letter that Bran has woken but is crippled.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – POV; Ned's bastard, now 15 (name day passed unremarked); at Castle Black as a Night's Watch recruit. Nicknamed "Lord Snow" mockingly by Ser Alliser; cold, lonely, resentful, and proud of beating every recruit in the yard. Brother Benjen refused to take him ranging. In anger Jon pictured him dead on the snow and feels guilty. He misses his siblings, especially Arya, and dreams of a kind, highborn mother whom Ned never speaks of. Lectured by Donal Noye that he acts like a better-than-thou lordling and shames the other boys; ashamed, he later apologizes to Grenn and offers to teach him. Quick-witted with Thorne ("I'd love to see Ghost juggle"), which made an enemy of Ser Alliser. Direwolf Ghost (white), chained in the old stables while he trains; Jon's cell is in Hardin's Tower.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger of the Night's Watch; cold toward Jon at the Wall ("We put aside our old families"). Led a half-dozen men on a ranging into the haunted forest, promised to be back by Jon's name day but is now a fortnight overdue; they were looking for Ser Waymar Royce (whose father is bannerman to Lord Arryn).
  • Bran Stark – 7; Robb's raven letter says he has woken from his coma but is crippled; Jon wept for joy that he will live. Robb Stark – 14, acting master of Winterfell; wrote the letter. Rickon – youngest (3).
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King, with the royal party. Catelyn – Ned's wife, in King's Landing with Ser Rodrik. Arya (Jon's favourite sibling; wolf Nymeria escaped) and Sansa (wolf Lady dead) – with Ned. Rickard, Brandon, Lyanna (dead father, brother, sister of Ned), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel (Jon's old master-at-arms; with Catelyn in King's Landing), Maester Luwin (maester), Hodor (simple stableboy), Mikken (smith), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard), Old Nan (storyteller). With Ned: Jory Cassel (guard captain), Vayon Poole (steward), Septa Mordane, Hullen (master of horse). Mycah (butcher's boy, dead, killed by the Hound).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); Robert Arryn – her sickly son, Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Petyr Baelish, "Littlefinger" – master of coin; grew up with Catelyn; summoned her to the Red Keep and said the dagger was his (won by Tyrion). Lord Varys "the Spider" – eunuch master of whisperers, with many "little birds". Maester Pycelle – royal maester. Stannis Baratheon – Robert's brother, at Dragonstone. Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard Lord Commander) and Renly Baratheon (Robert's youngest brother) – rode north to meet the king. Loras Tyrell (mentioned) – knight who unhorsed Jaime in the tourney. Captain Moreo Turnitis – Tyroshi ship captain who carried Catelyn; Ser Aron Santagar – king's master-at-arms (mentioned).
  • King Robert Baratheon, Queen Cersei, Prince Joffrey, Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer", Sandor Clegane "the Hound", Ser Ilyn Payne (mute King's Justice), Ser Raymun Darry (host of the royal party), Tommen, Myrcella, Tywin Lannister (Lannister head), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned), Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar (dead). The royal party is in the Riverlands, heading south.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother; honored guest at Castle Black: lodged in the King's Tower, dines with Mormont, rides the Wall, dices and drinks with Thorne and the officers. Friend to Jon, advises him to take the mocking name "Lord Snow" as his own; calls the Wall useless; sharp-tongued, stands up to Thorne with a threat of court influence.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jeor Mormont ("the Old Bear") – Lord Commander; gruff, bald, shaggy grey beard; keeps a talking raven ("Corn!", "Live!"); gave Jon Robb's letter. Maester Aemon – the Wall's maester (tends injuries, salves). Bowen Marsh 🆕 new this chapter (high officer, dices with Tyrion). Cotter Pyke 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – tavern wench's bastard, commands Eastwatch by the Sea.
  • Ser Alliser Thorne 🆕 new this chapter – master-at-arms for the recruits; ~50, onyx eyes, sharp-tongued, scornful of all recruits and of Jon in particular; now a declared enemy after Jon's joke.
  • Donal Noye 🆕 new this chapter – armorer; one-armed (left arm lost at the siege of Storm's End; formerly a smith to Stannis, said to have forged Robert's warhammer); blunt, fair; stopped the fight in the armory and gave Jon a hard talking-to.
  • Recruits 🆕 new this chapter: Grenn ("the Aurochs") – 16, big, slow, clumsy; Jon hurt his wrist; Todder ("Toad") – short, pig-eyed, insulted Jon's mother; Jeren (a septon's son, weak), Dareon (quick but afraid of being hit), Pyp (fights with his sword like a dagger), Halder (hits brutally hard but runs into attacks); two "rapers" from the Fingers whom Yoren brought north (names forgotten).
  • Benjen Stark (First Ranger, overdue on a ranging), Yoren (black brother who brought recruits north), Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others and Will seized by his reanimated body, as shown in the prologue; Castle Black only knows Royce is missing). Several rangers have reportedly vanished lately.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • Castle Black 🆕 new this chapter – the Night's Watch stronghold at the end of the kingsroad, half-ruined timber keeps and stone towers below the Wall, no walls of its own; once home to five thousand men, now about five hundred; the common hall (drafty, crows in the rafters), armory, Commander's Keep, King's Tower (Tyrion), Hardin's Tower (Jon, broken battlement). Only three of the Wall's nineteen strongholds are manned: Eastwatch (by the Sea), the Shadow Tower, and Castle Black.
  • The Wall – nearly 700 feet of ice, blue-white in sunlight, wide enough on top for a dozen knights, with catapults and cranes; "the end of the world". Beyond it, the haunted forest where Benjen ranges.
  • King's Landing – capital; the Red Keep where Catelyn met Littlefinger and Varys; Eel Alley inn.
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; Robb's raven came from here. The Trident, Dragonstone (Targaryen island seat, where Stannis is), Storm's End (Baratheon seat; Noye lost his arm in its siege), the Fingers, White Harbor, The Neck, Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak.
