Chapter 3 – Daenerys

Across the narrow sea in Pentos, thirteen-year-old Daenerys Targaryen is dressed up by her exiled brother Viserys and the magister Illyrio and presented at a feast to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles in Pentos)
  • Daenerys Targaryen (Dany) 🆕 new this chapter – POV; 13, silver-pale hair, violet eyes; "Daenerys Stormborn, Princess of Dragonstone". Born on Dragonstone during a great storm; her mother died in childbirth. Timid, bullied by her brother, longs for the "big house with the red door" in Braavos and its lemon tree. Has always expected to wed Viserys; now he is giving her to Drogo.
  • Viserys Targaryen 🆕 new this chapter – her older brother, a gaunt young man with lilac eyes and a feverish manner; styles himself Viserys III, King of the Andals, the Rhoynar and the First Men, and "the last dragon". Obsessed with regaining the Iron Throne ("the Usurper"), cruel to Dany when he "wakes the dragon". Has never used a sword in earnest or killed anyone. He is called "the beggar king" in Pentos. Plans to get an army by marrying Dany to Drogo.
  • Rhaegar 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – their dead elder brother, said to have been killed by the Usurper at the Trident.
  • Their father (the old king) 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – killed by "the Kingslayer" with a golden sword; Viserys's account also says Rhaegar's heir was murdered before Princess Elia of Dorne, and that the Lannisters and Starks sacked King's Landing.
  • Ser Willem Darry 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned, dead) – loyal knight who smuggled the children out of Dragonstone to Braavos and was kind to Dany; died there.
Pentos
  • Magister Illyrio Mopatis 🆕 new this chapter – enormously fat merchant (spices, gems, dragonbone, "less savory things") with a forked yellow beard; has housed and pampered the siblings for about half a year and arranged the meeting with Drogo. Flatters Viserys; Dany distrusts him.
  • Illyrio's slaves 🆕 new this chapter – an old silent woman and a chatty sixteen-year-old blonde girl who dress Dany.
Dothraki and guests
  • Khal Drogo 🆕 new this chapter – Dothraki warlord, under thirty, a head taller than any man, copper skin, a braid longer than his waist (never defeated in battle). Said to command a khalasar of forty thousand to a hundred thousand riders and have a palace in Vaes Dothrak. Lives in a nine-towered manse in Pentos. Dany is afraid of him.
  • Drogo's three bloodriders 🆕 new this chapter, Khal Moro and his son Rhogoro 🆕 new this chapter – guests at the feast.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont 🆕 new this chapter – knight past forty and balding, wearing a black bear on a green tunic; in exile because the Usurper wanted his head for selling poachers to slavers rather than giving them to the Night's Watch.
  • Other guests: a brother of the Archon of Tyrosh, sellswords and bravos, a red priest, men from Ibben and the Summer Isles. Drogo's guards include Unsullied eunuchs.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North; Viserys names "Stark" among the Usurper's dogs.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, of Riverrun.
  • Robb, Jon Snow (bastard), Bran, Sansa, Arya, Rickon (three) – the Stark children; each has a direwolf pup (Jon's is white).
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother on the Wall.
Winterfell household
  • Theon Greyjoy (ward), Jory Cassel (guard captain), Maester Luwin, Hullen, Harwin, Ser Rodrik, Old Nan.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Jon Arryn – Lord of the Eyrie, Ned's foster father; just died. Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, his widow, with her young son, now in the Eyrie. Brynden – Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate. Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father.
Royal court (Seven Kingdoms)
  • King Robert Baratheon – the king who took the throne (Viserys's "Usurper"); riding to Winterfell with Queen Cersei Lannister, her brothers, and royal children including Prince Tommen (7). His rebellion began when the Mad King Aerys demanded the heads of Ned and Robert.
  • The Kingslayer 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – a Lannister who killed Dany's father.
  • Maester Pycelle – tended Jon Arryn.
  • Elia of Dorne 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Rhaegar's wife, said to have been killed with her child.
The Night's Watch
  • Understrength and losing men. Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others in the prologue; Will seized by Royce's reanimated body; Gared's fate unknown), Mormont, Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed beings who killed Royce. Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall. Wildlings.

Places

Places
  • Pentos 🆕 new this chapter – Free City across the narrow sea, brick towers on a bay; red priests worship the Lord of Light; Illyrio's estate and Drogo's manse are here. Slavery is officially forbidden.
  • Braavos, Myr, Tyrosh, Qohor, Volantis, Lys 🆕 new this chapter – other Free Cities where the siblings wandered after fleeing.
  • Dragonstone 🆕 new this chapter – ancient Targaryen island seat where Dany was born.
  • King's Landing 🆕 new this chapter – capital of the Seven Kingdoms, from which the Targaryens fled; the Iron Throne is there.
  • Vaes Dothrak 🆕 new this chapter – Dothraki city, site of Drogo's palace.
  • Westeros 🆕 new this chapter – the land across the narrow sea; the Seven Kingdoms.
  • Winterfell, Riverrun, the Eyrie, Casterly Rock, the Wall / Castle Black – as before.
