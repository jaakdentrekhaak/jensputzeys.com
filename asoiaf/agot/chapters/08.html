Chapter 8 – Bran

On the last day before the royal party leaves for the south, Bran climbs to the First Keep, overhears the queen and a golden-haired man talking about Ned, sees them together, and is pushed from the window.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – 7, POV. Loves climbing Winterfell's roofs and walls barefoot; his father laughed it off ("you're a squirrel") and told him to hide it from his mother. Was excited to go south with Ned (he dreams of being a Kingsguard knight). His unnamed direwolf (yellow-eyed, clever) howled as he climbed. On the First Keep he saw the queen with a man in a secret embrace; she screamed, he pulled Bran up, then said "the things I do for love" and shoved him backward out of the window. Bran fell toward the courtyard; his fate is not yet known.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, about to leave as Hand of the King; the queen and her companion discuss him as a danger. Catelyn Stark – Bran's mother, terrified he'll fall; stays at Winterfell.
  • Robb Stark – 14, heir; his direwolf is Grey Wind 🆕 new this chapter (named for how fast he runs). Out on the boar hunt. Sansa (wolf Lady), Arya (wolf Nymeria), Rickon (3, youngest; wolf Shaggydog 🆕 new this chapter).
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, going to the Wall with Benjen; Bran thinks he is angry at everyone and could not find him this morning. White direwolf Ghost.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother of the Night's Watch; at the hunt.
  • Rickard, Brandon, Lyanna Stark (dead) – Ned's father, brother and sister. The queen sneers that Ned is "still in love" with Lyanna, "the insipid little dead sixteen-year-old".
Winterfell household
  • Hodor 🆕 new this chapter – simple, smiling stableboy who only says "Hodor"; tends Bran's pony. Old Nan – storyteller who warns Bran of falls. Mikken 🆕 new this chapter (smith) and Gage 🆕 new this chapter (cook) – Winterfell staff. Maester Luwin – the maester (once threw a clay boy off a wall to scare Bran). Jory Cassel – captain of the guard, out hunting. Ser Rodrik – household knight, hunting. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward, hunting. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Hullen – master of horse; Harwin – his son.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister; the queen and the man call her a "frightened cow" who "shared Jon Arryn's bed"; they say she fled King's Landing with her son because he was to be fostered at Casterly Rock, and that she might speak up now he is safe at the Eyrie. They say she has no proof, "or does she?". Jon Arryn (dead) – the late Hand; Robert Arryn – her sickly son, Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle (Knight of the Gate) and father (Lord of Riverrun), not present.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – out on the boar hunt with Joffrey; the queen says "he loves me not" and that he loves Ned like a brother. Fond of the late Lyanna.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – seen by Bran, golden-haired. In the conversation she is anxious about Ned becoming Hand ("I should have insisted he name you"), thinks he means to move against her family, and wants Robert's death to come soon so Joffrey takes the throne, while calling her husband restless and still pining for Lyanna. Screamed when Bran was seen.
  • The unnamed man 🆕 new this chapter – with the queen in the First Keep: golden-haired, strong, languid and sardonic, calls her "sweet sister", and looks like her mirror image. He did not want to be Hand; he is relaxed about Ned ("give me honorable enemies rather than ambitious ones"); pushed Bran out the window. The text does not name him; Bran sees that the two look as alike as reflections in a mirror (the queen's twin is Ser Jaime Lannister).
  • Prince Joffrey – crown prince, on the hunt with his father. Tommen, Myrcella – his younger brother and sister.
  • Tyrion Lannister ("the Imp") – the queen's dwarf brother, rode out with the hunt. Ser Jaime Lannister ("Kingslayer") – her golden twin, a Kingsguard knight. Lord Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – their father at Casterly Rock. Sandor Clegane – burned-faced royal retainer. Maester Pycelle – royal maester.
  • Ser Boros 🆕 new this chapter (bald, jowly) and Ser Meryn 🆕 new this chapter (droopy eyes, rust-coloured beard) – the two Kingsguard knights who came north. Ser Barristan Selmy 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – "Barristan the Bold", Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, to be met in King's Landing. Jaime is also Kingsguard and "killed the old mad king" (so Robb says he shouldn't count). The Kingsguard are seven white-armoured sworn knights without wives or children.
  • Stannis 🆕 new this chapter and Renly 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Robert's brothers; the man says Stannis is "enough to give anyone indigestion" and that Robert can barely stomach them. Littlefinger 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – someone the king might have made Hand, "gods help us".
  • Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar (dead) – the overthrown king and his son. The queen says Ned "betrayed one already" (a king). Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands.
  • Legendary Kingsguard from Old Nan's stories, e.g. Ser Arthur Dayne (dead), Aemon the Dragonknight, Serwyn of the Mirror Shield, Gerold Hightower; twin knights Erryk and Arryk who died fighting each other in the Dance of the Dragons 🆕 new this chapter (an old Targaryen war).
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother, to take Jon to the Wall); Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, given to Khal Drogo) and Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king); Illyrio Mopatis (fat Pentoshi magister); Khal Drogo (huge Dothraki warlord); Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight).

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; a maze of towers, tunnels and roofs. Features: the godswood with its heart tree (eyed and hand-like leaves) and tall sentinel trees, the First Keep (oldest part, empty, gargoyles, now used for the secret meeting), the broken tower (burned out by lightning long ago, crows nest there), the armory, glass gardens, kennels, bell tower and rookery.
  • King's Landing – the red castle built by the Dragonlords, where the party goes tomorrow along the kingsroad; Old Nan says it has ghosts and dungeons.
  • The Wall / Castle Black (Watch base), Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), The Trident, Dorne, Starfall, Myr, The Neck (south of it, the queen says, Ned has never taken interest), Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
