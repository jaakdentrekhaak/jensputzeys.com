Chapter 27 – Eddard

Ned handles council business over the crowds flooding King's Landing for the tourney, reviews what Jon Arryn's servants told him, and visits an armorer's shop where he questions an apprentice named Gendry about Jon Arryn's earlier visit.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Hand of the King, in King's Landing; stands in for the absent king at council and dislikes the "Hand's tourney". Investigating Jon Arryn's death: has Malleon's book of great-house lineages (finds nothing yet), learned from the servants that Jon had been reading much, was troubled over his sickly son, quarreled with the king, was visited by Lord Stannis and went with him to commission silver-and-jasper falcon armor and visited a brothel. Suspicious of Stannis leaving for Dragonstone but won't recall him yet. Concluded from Gendry's looks that he is a bastard son of King Robert and wonders why Jon Arryn cared. Misses Catelyn, Robb, Jon and the north.
  • Arya (11) and Sansa (elder, betrothed to Joffrey) – Ned's daughters in King's Landing; Bran (crippled by a fall, alive at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord of Winterfell), Rickon (youngest), Catelyn (Ned's wife, back at Winterfell), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing), Lyanna (Ned's dead sister), Old Nan, Maester Luwin (Winterfell). Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady dead, Nymeria gone.
Winterfell household (Ned's guard in King's Landing)
  • Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard; interviewed the four servants. Alyn – Ned's man, placed in command of the twenty guards lent to the City Watch. Harwin (guard), Varly, Jacks, Tomard, Desmond, Porther – Ned's guardsmen; Jacks and Varly escort him. At Winterfell: Hodor (simple giant who carries Bran), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Ser Rodrik (master-at-arms), Mikken (smith), Septon Chayle (librarian), Vayon Poole (steward); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Jon Arryn (dead) – servants describe him as vigorous, friendly with stable hands, fond of reading, worried about his son (talk of fostering him on Dragonstone), and had commissioned silver armor with Stannis. Lysa Arryn (his widow, Catelyn's sister, in the Eyrie), Robert Arryn (their sickly son), Maester Colemon (Jon's maester, went with Lysa), Ser Hugh of the Vale (Jon's former squire; arrogant new knight who refused to be questioned by Jory), Edmure (Catelyn's brother), Brynden (her uncle), Hoster Tully (her father).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – skips council; delights in the tourney. Ned's friend; known for his lusts. Queen Cersei (Lannister wife of Robert), Prince Joffrey (12, heir), Tommen, Myrcella (younger children), Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" (Cersei's twin, Kingsguard), Tywin Lannister (their father), Tyrion "the Imp" (dwarf brother, went south from Winterfell), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane (Joffrey's bodyguard), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless king's justice).
  • Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – Master of coin; ordered to fund fifty more watchmen; found 40,000 gold dragons for the champion's purse; jokes at council. Varys "the Spider" – master of whispers; Ned suspects his informers shadow him. Grand Maester Pycelle – gave Ned Malleon's book; defends tournaments. Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard).
  • Renly Baratheon – the king's youngest brother, friendly and mocking; wears green armor with golden antlers; showed Ned a locket with a portrait of Margaery Tyrell, asking if she resembled Lyanna (Ned saw no resemblance). Stannis Baratheon – king's other brother; grim and dutiful; left for Dragonstone and hasn't returned; went on rides with Jon Arryn; Renly gibes at his marriage and "ugly daughter"; once held Storm's End through a year's siege. Ned wonders where he stands.
  • Janos Slynt 🆕 new this chapter – stout, bald commander of the City Watch (gold cloaks), complains of crime from the crowds; given fifty hires and twenty of Ned's men. Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" (young tourney knight, mentioned; armor by Tobho Mott), Margaery Tyrell (mentioned). Syrio Forel (Arya's Braavosi sword teacher), Balon Greyjoy (defeated rebel of the Iron Islands), Mad King Aerys II (overthrown by Robert).
  • Lord Beric Dondarrion 🆕 new this chapter – young, dashing lord with red-gold hair, black banner of purple lightning on a night sky; arrived to compete in the tourney.
Smiths of King's Landing
  • Tobho Mott 🆕 new this chapter – wealthy master armorer on the Street of Steel; makes costly armor for Renly and the Knight of Flowers; learned Valyrian steel work in Qohor. Reluctantly admits an unnamed lord paid double to take in Gendry without a fee and keep quiet; won't say who the boy is.
  • Gendry 🆕 new this chapter – apprentice about Robb's age, big, black-haired, blue-eyed, surly; made a bull-horned helm; mother (dead, yellow hair, alehouse worker) is all he knows. Jon Arryn and Stannis visited him; Jon asked about his mother.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit at Castle Black), Samwell Tarly (fat, gentle new recruit, Jon's friend), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.

Places

Places
  • King's Landing / Red Keep – crowded, hot, and crime-ridden with tourney visitors; City Watch (gold cloaks); a woman's head found in the Great Sept. Tower of the Hand (Ned's quarters). The Street of Steel 🆕 new this chapter – hillside street of smiths, up from the market by the Mud (River) Gate; Tobho Mott's shop at the top. Street of the Sisters, the Great Sept, the King's Gate.
  • Dragonstone – Targaryen island fortress conquered by Stannis, where he now stays. Storm's End (Stannis's siege), Qohor (Valyrian steel forging), Myr, Winterfell, Castle Black, the Wall, Horn Hill, the Eyrie, Casterly Rock, Moat Cailin, White Harbor, the Trident, Riverrun, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, the Dothraki sea.
