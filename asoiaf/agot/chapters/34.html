Chapter 34 – Catelyn

Catelyn's battered party reaches the Vale with an Arryn escort, climbs the mountain to the Eyrie, and finds her sister Lysa frightened, hostile to the plan, and fixated on her sickly young son.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; arrived in the Vale with her prisoner Tyrion after the mountain journey cost six brave men their lives. Shaky about whether Tyrion is truly guilty ("could I be wrong?") but resolved; worried about Bronn's growing closeness to Tyrion. Frightened to near paralysis crossing a narrow ridge on the stair; rode up the last stretch in a basket. Carries Ned's orders to ready the north's defenses and plans to send ravens to her father and the north. Warm to Mya Stone but stung by the thought of Jon Snow.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark (Catelyn's husband, Hand in King's Landing as far as she knows), Sansa and Arya (their daughters with him), Bran (crippled by a fall, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord of Winterfell), Rickon (youngest, small), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves and others as before.
Catelyn's party and captives
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – prisoner, but armed (dirk, axe), wearing a shadowskin cloak (won at dice from Marillion) and Chiggen's mail; cheerful and mocking, close with Bronn; remains calm. Jokes about his arrogant brother, avaricious father, power-hungry sister; held in a tower cell at the Gates of the Moon for the night.
  • Bronn – sellsword; fierce fighter, "no kindness, little loyalty"; insisted on coming to the Eyrie. Marillion – singer; looks haggard, wants to see the story's end, tags along. Ser Rodrik Cassel – Winterfell's master-at-arms; feverish and gravely weak from wounds; resting at the Gate of the Vale (the Bloody Gate). Ser Willis Wode – Lady Whent's knight from Harrenhal; stays with Rodrik. (Casualties so far: Jyck, Mohor, Kurleket in the first fight; two more in the second, and Tyrion's servant Morrec died of fever; Chiggen is also dead; Lharys is no longer with them.)
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's younger sister, widow of Jon Arryn, Lady of the Eyrie; heavy, pale, watery-eyed, petulant, with long auburn hair; hugged Catelyn warmly in public then furiously blamed her for bringing Tyrion without warning and dragging them into the Lannister quarrel ("I never meant to fight them!"); says the Eyrie is impregnable; is "only playing at courtship" per Brynden; calls the boy a very strong "seed". Mentions making Tyrion "fly" with her son.
  • Lord Robert Arryn (Robin) (Lysa's son, first seen here) – six years old, small, sickly, trembles ("the shaking sickness"), clutches a cloth doll, still suckles at his mother's breast; "Lord of the Eyrie, Defender of the Vale, True Warden of the East". Wants to see "the bad man fly".
  • Ser Brynden Tully "the Blackfish" – Catelyn's uncle, Hoster's younger brother, the Knight of the Gate; grey, bushy-browed, blunt and kind; feuded with Hoster, left Riverrun for the Eyrie at the sisters' weddings, took the black fish as his own sigil; tells Catelyn the Vale is angry (Jon Arryn loved; Jaime made Warden of the East), that Lysa is afraid, and that Catelyn may find her unhelpful; also thinks Lord Hoster must be told.
  • Lord Nestor Royce 🆕 new this chapter – cousin of Bronze Yohn, High Steward of the Vale for fourteen years and Keeper of the Gates of the Moon; massive, gruff; some want him to rule for the boy; rejected by Lysa as suitor.
  • Ser Donnel Waynwood 🆕 new this chapter – 20, earnest, homely knight who led the escort; says Lysa forbade the Vale's knights to go to the tourney or hunt clansmen. Mya Stone 🆕 new this chapter – 17-18, short-haired bastard of the Vale, mule guide with "owl's eyes"; cheerful; in love with Mychel Redfort 🆕 new this chapter (squire to Ser Lyn Corbray 🆕 new this chapter), a match Catelyn doubts will happen. Ser Vardis Egen 🆕 new this chapter – silver-haired captain of Jon Arryn's household guard. Maester Colemon – Jon Arryn's former maester; nervous and thin; kept at the Eyrie by Lysa; kept by Lysa to tend Robert. Hoster Tully (Catelyn and Lysa's father, Lord of Riverrun; estranged from Brynden), Edmure (their brother; writes Catelyn), Jon Arryn (dead former Hand, Lysa's husband; his last words reportedly "the seed is strong").
  • Petyr Baelish – Littlefinger, mentioned as a ward of Hoster in Catelyn's youth.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert (Ned's friend), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tywin Lannister (her father), Jaime Lannister (her twin, Kingsguard; now Warden of the East, to Vale resentment), Littlefinger (Master of coin, once Hoster's ward), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Pycelle (Grand Maester), Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) — at court, as before. Lord Yohn "Bronze Yohn" Royce (mentioned; Nestor's kinsman).
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Syrio Forel (Arya's sword teacher), Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Vayon Poole (steward), Hodor (simple giant), Maester Luwin, Old Nan, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Mikken (smith) and others, as before.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit at Castle Black), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit, Jon's friend), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.

Places

Places
  • The Bloody Gate 🆕 new this chapter – fortified pass with twin towers and a covered bridge, entrance to the Vale; held by Ser Brynden.
  • The Vale of Arryn 🆕 new this chapter – a green, lake-dotted valley ringed by mountains; peak the Giant's Lance and waterfall Alyssa's Tears.
  • The Gates of the Moon 🆕 new this chapter – Lord Nestor Royce's castle at the foot of the mountain where the party spends the night; Tyrion held in a tower cell.
  • The Eyrie – seven white towers atop the Giant's Lance, reached by a carved stair guarded by waycastles Stone, Snow, Sky, then a chimney climb or a basket on winches; Lysa's seat; impregnable in her words.
  • The high road / mountains – dangerous clan country. Riverrun, Winterfell, King's Landing, Casterly Rock, the Wall, Highgarden, Dragonstone, Vaes Dothrak, Pentos, Seagard, Lord Frey's castles, Harrenhal.
