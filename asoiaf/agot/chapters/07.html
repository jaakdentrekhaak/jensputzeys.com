Chapter 7 – Arya

Arya flees a needlework lesson and watches from a window as Prince Joffrey mocks Robb in the practice yard and a burned knight backs him up, before she is caught by her mother and her septa.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Arya Stark – 9, POV. Plain, long-faced, brown-haired; takes after her father and Jon, hates needlework and "ladylike" things, loves riding and wants to fight. Resents Sansa. Direwolf Nymeria 🆕 new this chapter (yellow-eyed, sleeps at the foot of her bed, named after a warrior queen of the Rhoyne). Closest to Jon. Fled the lesson with a cheeky "I have to go shoe a horse" and is now caught by Septa Mordane and Catelyn.
  • Sansa Stark – 11, beautiful, auburn-haired like the Tullys, skilled at sewing, singing, dancing and poetry; delighted that Joffrey likes her (he told her she was beautiful). Her direwolf is Lady 🆕 new this chapter. Embarrassed to hear Jon called her brother; corrects it to "half brother".
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, close to Arya, jokes and teases her; with white direwolf Ghost. Sees Joffrey as "a little shit". Barred (as a bastard) from sparring with the princes. Silent and cold with anger after Joffrey's taunts. Still unaware he will be sent to the Wall.
  • Robb Stark – 14, heir, proud and hot-tempered; furious when Joffrey mocked him and refused a blunted sword. Held back by Theon.
  • Bran Stark – 7, padded and fighting Prince Tommen with wooden swords; won the bout. Rickon – youngest.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark and Catelyn Stark – parents. Catelyn is a Tully (auburn-haired); she is now with Septa Mordane waiting for Arya. Ned has agreed to become Hand and take the girls south (Arya doesn't know yet).
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, Night's Watch. Rickard, Brandon, Lyanna Stark (dead).
Winterfell household
  • Septa Mordane 🆕 new this chapter – bony, strict septa who teaches the girls; fawns over Sansa and Princess Myrcella, disapproves of Arya.
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – master-at-arms, stout with white cheek whiskers; stood up to the burned knight and insisted on blunted swords. Beth Cassel 🆕 new this chapter – his little daughter, adores Sansa. Jeyne Poole 🆕 new this chapter – steward's daughter and Sansa's best friend; used to tease Arya as "Horseface".
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward (Greyjoy hostage), wears the golden kraken; mocking and scornful; restrained Robb. Maester Luwin – Winterfell's maester. Hullen – master of horse; Harwin – his son. Jory Cassel – captain of the guard. Old Nan – storyteller. Desmond – guardsman. Stable lads Lew 🆕 new this chapter and Donnis 🆕 new this chapter helped the princes out of their padding.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister; sent a secret message accusing the Lannisters of murdering her husband Jon Arryn (dead). Son Robert Arryn. Edmure (Catelyn's sibling), Brynden (her uncle, Knight of the Gate), Hoster Tully (her father, Lord of Riverrun).
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – the king, who has offered Ned the Hand; Queen Cersei Lannister – his wife, a Lannister, named in Lysa's letter as Jon Arryn's killer.
  • Prince Joffrey Baratheon – 12, golden-haired, arrogant; wears a surcoat quartered with the stag and the lion, putting his mother's house on par with the king's. In the yard he demanded live steel, called Robb a child, and left laughing. Prince Tommen – plump, younger, lost his bout to Bran. Princess Myrcella – sewing with the girls.
  • Sandor Clegane – tall, black-haired, heavily burn-scarred, muscled like a bull; backed Joffrey's demand for a sharp sword, boasted he killed a man at twelve; Ser Rodrik called him "Clegane". Serves in the royal party with Lannister and Baratheon squires and knights.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister ("Kingslayer") – the queen's golden twin; Tyrion Lannister ("the Imp") – their dwarf brother; Lord Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – their father at Casterly Rock; Maester Pycelle – royal maester; Kingsguard – the king's white-cloaked guards.
  • Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar Targaryen (dead) – the overthrown king and his son; Daeren Targaryen (long dead) – the "Young Dragon"; Ser Arthur Dayne (dead) – Kingsguard knight slain by Ned; Ashara Dayne (mentioned) – his sister at Starfall; Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands, beaten nine years ago.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother); Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, given to Khal Drogo) and Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king); Illyrio Mopatis (fat Pentoshi magister hosting them); Khal Drogo (huge undefeated Dothraki warlord); Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight).

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; features here: the needlework tower room, the practice yard, and a window in the covered bridge between the armory and the Great Keep overlooking the yard; also a godswood with a still pool.
  • King's Landing (capital, where Ned will go as Hand), The Wall / Castle Black (Night's Watch base), Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), The Trident (river of Robert's victory), Dorne, Starfall (Dayne seat), Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
  • The Rhoyne 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – a river across the narrow sea; its warrior queen Nymeria is Arya's wolf's namesake.
