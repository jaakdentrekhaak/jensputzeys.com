Chapter 41 – Jon

Ser Alliser passes eight recruits, including Jon, on to the Lord Commander, and that night Jon rides out, nearly deserts, turns back, and asks Maester Aemon to have the unchosen Samwell Tarly made a steward or aide instead of a fighter.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, nicknamed "the Bastard"; the best swordsman and rider among the recruits; passed by Ser Alliser as one of eight sent on to the Lord Commander. Tempted to ride home along the kingsroad, but turned back before swearing his vow. Believes his uncle Benjen is still alive; wonders who his unnamed mother was, since Ned is ashamed to speak of her. Has the albino direwolf Ghost (once set on Rast's throat); gave Arya the sword Needle when he left.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell and Hand of the King; bedridden in the Tower of the Hand with a broken leg after the fight with Jaime's men; Robert forced the Hand's badge back on him. Haunted by a dream of the tower in Dorne where his sister Lyanna died.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; at the Eyrie, planning to take ship for Winterfell and her sons; never welcomed Jon. Sansa and Arya – Ned's daughters in the Red Keep (Arya has been silent since the attack). Robb (fifteen, ruling Winterfell), Bran (eight, crippled), Rickon; Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing beyond the Wall. Lyanna and Brandon – Ned's dead sister and elder brother. Direwolves Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost.
  • Winterfell household: Maester Luwin (taught Jon the meaning of a maester's chain), Ser Rodrik Cassel (master-at-arms, with Catelyn), Theon Greyjoy (ward), Osha (captive wildling woman), Hodor, Old Nan, Septa Mordane, Mikken, Hallis Mollen. In King's Landing: Alyn (acting captain of Ned's guard), Vayon Poole (steward); Jory Cassel (dead).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, gentle, bookish recruit, hopeless with weapons; not chosen, hides his misery; Jon fears Ser Alliser's boys will hurt him. Read every book in his father's library; animals like him (Ghost took to him); can do sums, read and write. Was sent to the Wall by his cruel father Lord Randyll (mentioned, who could not make him a warrior).
  • The eight chosen recruits: Jon; Pyp (jokester, big ears); Grenn (hopes to be a ranger); Toad (ugly); Halder (wants to be a builder); and others nicknamed by Ser Alliser: Stone Head, Aurochs, Lover, Pimple, Monkey ("the Mummer's Monkey"), Ser Loon. Fellow trainees at the celebration: Dareon (fetched the wine; says Jon will be a ranger), Matthar 🆕 new this chapter, Jeren. Hostile unchosen trainees: Rast and Cuger 🆕 new this chapter.
  • Ser Alliser Thorne – bitter master-at-arms who scorns the recruits ("when winter comes you will die like flies"). Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; gave the eight a feast from his own table. Maester Aemon – ancient, blind, kindly maester of the Watch; never removes his chain; promised to think on Jon's plea. Chett 🆕 new this chapter – steward who serves Aemon; boil-faced, wen on his neck, illiterate, angry; scorns Sam. Clydas 🆕 new this chapter – bald, chinless, weak-eyed steward aiding Aemon. Bowen Marsh – Lord Steward. Three-Finger Hobb – cook. Yoren – recruiter. Gueren 🆕 new this chapter – bringing five new recruits north. Ser Waymar Royce, Gared – earlier lost/executed.
  • Orders: rangers (fight, ride beyond the Wall), builders (masons, repairs), stewards (supplies, cooks, ravens, aid the maester).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, tired king; struck Cersei; wants peace with the Lannisters. Queen Cersei Lannister – proud, cold; named in Lysa's letter as Jon Arryn's killer. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled the capital; reported massing a host at Casterly Rock. Tywin Lannister – their father, lord of Casterly Rock. Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf brother; won trial by combat at the Eyrie through Bronn and is being freed. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; once lost a duel to Brandon Stark over Catelyn.
  • Also at court: Joffrey (prince), Ser Meryn Trant, Grand Maester Pycelle, Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan Selmy, Loras Tyrell, Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor Clegane, Barra (Robert's baby bastard).
Catelyn's party and the Eyrie
  • Bronn – sellsword who killed Ser Vardis Egen as Tyrion's champion. Marillion – singer; Ser Willis Wode.
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; Robert Arryn – her sickly boy lord; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, leaving to defend Riverrun; Hoster Tully – their father (possibly ill); Edmure Tully – brother, defending Riverrun. Lysa's suitors Lord Hunter, Ser Lyn Corbray, Ser Morton Waynwood; Lord Nestor Royce, Maester Colemon, Mord (gaoler), Mya Stone; Jon Arryn – dead former Hand.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, children of the forest (legend); Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo, owns a dragon egg. Viserys – her cruel brother, "the dragon". Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho (bloodriders), Aggo, Rakharo, Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah (handmaids), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), dosh khaleen.

Places
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress at the Wall: yard, common hall, Lord Commander's Tower, Hardin's Tower (Ghost's lodging), Maester Aemon's keep below the rookery. The Wall; the kingsroad – the road south that tempted Jon. Eastwatch and the Shadow Tower – Watch castles at the Wall's ends.
  • Winterfell, the Eyrie, Riverrun, Casterly Rock, King's Landing / Red Keep, Tower of the Hand, the tower in Dorne, Gulltown, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak.
