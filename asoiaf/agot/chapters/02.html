Chapter 2 – Catelyn

Catelyn finds Ned in the godswood after the execution and tells him that Jon Arryn is dead and that King Robert is coming to Winterfell with his court.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North; keeps the old gods (the heart tree in the godswood) and withdraws there after killing a man. The executed deserter was the fourth this year. Grieves for Jon Arryn, his foster father, and is delighted that his old friend the king is coming. Worries about the Night's Watch falling below a thousand men and may eventually have to call the banners against the King-beyond-the-Wall. Does not believe in signs or in the Others. Sword: Ice.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) 🆕 new this chapter – Ned's wife, from Riverrun; follows the Faith of the Seven (Ned built her a small sept) and dislikes the godswood. Proud of Bran, uneasy about the direwolf omen and the "darker things" beyond the Wall. Sister of Lysa Arryn.
  • Robb Stark – eldest son, 14; Tully-looking; found the direwolf pups.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, 14; has a white pup.
  • Bran Stark – seven; took a direwolf pup; Ned says he did well at the execution.
  • Rickon Stark – youngest, only three; not sure about the pups, a little afraid.
  • Arya Stark 🆕 new this chapter – a daughter; already in love with her pup.
  • Sansa Stark 🆕 new this chapter – a daughter; charmed and gracious about the pups.
  • Direwolf pups – six (five grey, one white), being named by the children in the kitchen.
  • Benjen (Ben) Stark 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Ned's brother, on the Wall; he writes to Ned about the Watch's shrinking numbers. A raven is to be sent to him about the king's visit.
  • Stark words: "Winter is coming."
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Winterfell's maester; will send the ravens. Says the Others never existed.
  • Jory Cassel – captain of the guard; to ride south with an honor guard to meet the royal party on the kingsroad.
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward. Hullen, Harwin, Desmond, Ser Rodrik, Old Nan – household staff and retainers (Old Nan tells tales of the Others).
House Tully / House Arryn (the Vale)
  • Jon Arryn 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Lord of the Eyrie, has just died, quickly, of a sickness attended by Maester Pycelle, who gave him milk of the poppy. Fostered Ned and Robert at the Eyrie and rebelled against the Mad King rather than hand them over; married Lysa Tully the same day Ned married Catelyn, fifteen years ago.
  • Lysa Arryn (née Tully) 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Catelyn's sister, Jon Arryn's widow; she and her young son have returned to the Eyrie. Catelyn wishes she were at Riverrun.
  • Jon Arryn's son 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – a boy, who needs other children around him.
  • Brynden 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Catelyn's uncle, named Knight of the Gate in the Vale by Jon Arryn.
  • Hoster Tully 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Lord of Riverrun, Catelyn's father.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – friend and former fellow ward of Ned; riding to Winterfell with about a hundred knights, retainers and half again as many freeriders. Wrote the letter in his own hand.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister 🆕 new this chapter – the queen, travelling with the king; reputedly increasingly proud.
  • Prince Tommen 🆕 new this chapter – the royal son aged seven, the same age as Bran; there are other royal children in the party.
  • The queen's brothers 🆕 new this chapter – two Lannisters in the party; Ned has little love for the Lannisters of Casterly Rock, who joined Robert's cause only late, when victory was nearly certain.
  • Maester Pycelle 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – tended Jon Arryn.
  • Aerys II Targaryen, "the Mad King" 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – overthrown by Robert's rebellion after demanding Ned's and Robert's heads.
The Night's Watch
  • Under a thousand men, losing men to desertion and rangings. In the prologue, three rangers met the Others: Will (seized by the reanimated Ser Waymar Royce, who was killed), Gared (left with the horses). Mormont and Maester Aemon are at the Wall.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed beings who killed Royce; Ned considers them legend, eight thousand years gone.
  • Mance Rayder, the King-beyond-the-Wall – seen by Ned as the cause of the Watch's losses.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; its godswood has three acres of ancient forest around a weirwood heart tree with a carved face and a black pool.
  • Riverrun 🆕 new this chapter – Tully seat on the Red Fork of the Trident, with a bright garden godswood and a sept.
  • The Eyrie 🆕 new this chapter – Jon Arryn's high, lonely castle in the Vale; Lysa has returned there.
  • The Vale 🆕 new this chapter – region of the Arryns; Catelyn's uncle holds the gate.
  • Casterly Rock 🆕 new this chapter – seat of the Lannisters.
  • The Wall / Castle Black, the haunted forest – the northern barrier and the wilderness beyond.
  • The kingsroad 🆕 new this chapter – road south along which the royal party travels.
  • Isle of Faces 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – where "green men" keep the last weirwoods of the south.
