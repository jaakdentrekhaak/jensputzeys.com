Chapter 61 – Daenerys

After Khal Drogo's khalasar destroys a rival khal's host and sacks a Lhazareen town, Dany stops her riders from raping captives, claims the women as her slaves, and a captive godswife treats Drogo's wounds.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Silver Lady" – exiled Targaryen princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo, expecting a son she calls Rhaego; her time is near. Survived a poisoning attempt and put her three dragon's eggs in a brazier with no visible result. On this campaign she rides a silver mare, was horrified by the rape and slaughter after the battle, and defied her khas to stop it, claiming the victims as her slaves ("I am khaleesi, heir to the Seven Kingdoms, the blood of the dragon"). Hired Mirri Maz Duur to attend her birth.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord (braid hung with bells); vowed to cross the sea and take the Seven Kingdoms for his son. Led his khalasar against Khal Ogo and Ogo's son Fogo, killing both and taking more bells; wounded (arrow in the arm, his left nipple sliced off, a gash across the chest) but refuses to rest or heed the healers, and let Dany keep her slaves. His bloodriders: Cohollo (old, shoulder wound), Qotho (cruelest, sneers at the Lamb Men and Dany), Haggo (huge). Mago 🆕 new this chapter – rider in Ko Jhaqo's khas who complained Dany took his captive.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), Dany's adviser, in green surcoat and grey plate; stopped the poisoner and now advises selling captives in Meereen for ship money; remarked that her mercy shows she is her brother Rhaegar's sister (not Viserys's). Handmaids Doreah, Irri, Jhiqui; young khas warriors Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo (killed a rapist), Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings, sends letters to Jorah. Viserys – her late brother, killed by Drogo.
  • Mirri Maz Duur 🆕 new this chapter – Lhazareen godswife (priestess-healer) of the Great Shepherd's temple, old, heavy, plain and flat-nosed; captured, claimed by Dany. Studied in Asshai with a maester named Marwyn 🆕 new this chapter (a Citadel-trained man, per Jorah) and others, knows birthing lore, claims she has never lost a babe. Treated Drogo's wounds inside the temple despite Qotho's threats ("as the khal fares, so shall you"); the bloodriders call her maegi (a feared blood-sorceress). Warned him to avoid wine and poppy; he ignored her.
  • Lhazareen ("Lamb Men", haesh rakhi) 🆕 new this chapter – squat, flat-faced herding people of the region, worshippers of the Great Shepherd; their town was sacked, perhaps ten thousand captives to be sold as slaves (Meereen suggested; its brothels pay well after a plague).
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife; reached Robb at Moat Cailin and now travels with the host, masking fear for her father, brother, husband and children. At the Twins she went alone into Walder Frey's castle and haggled the crossing, learning from him that Jon Arryn meant to foster Lysa's son with Stannis at Dragonstone (she had understood it was Casterly Rock). Takes two young Frey boys north as wards; advises Robb but tries not to usurp him.
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son, leading some twenty thousand northmen; took counsel daily from a different bannerman but is impatient (wanted to storm the Twins) until his mother rebuked him. Accepted Frey's price: he will wed one of Walder's daughters after the war, take Olyvar Frey as squire, and (if Arya is found) see Arya wed Elmar. Split his army: the horse crossed with him, while Roose Bolton leads the foot south to meet Tywin. Wolf Grey Wind.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, formerly King's Hand; held in a dark dungeon under the Red Keep, feverish and starved. Varys offered him his life for a confession and threatened Sansa; Ned refused to promise. Cersei is expected to visit.
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, still betrothed to Joffrey, kept close by Cersei; begged Joffrey for mercy for Ned and was told he must first confess.
  • Arya Stark – younger daughter; per Varys, escaped Ser Meryn and fled, and no one has found her. Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord in Winterfell; will gain Frey boys as companions. Rickon – youngest son, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, now a Night's Watch brother at Castle Black (see Night's Watch).
  • Ned's household guard: Alyn and Harwin (Hullen's son), sent after Ser Gregor and hoped to return. Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan of Winterfell), Maester Luwin (the castle's maester), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb; cocky scout-messenger between Robb and Brynden), Hodor (simple, huge stableboy who says only "Hodor"), Osha (captured wildling woman, kept in irons), Old Nan (storyteller), Mikken (blacksmith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard). Dead: Jory Cassel (Ned's guard captain), Hullen (master of horse), Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn (Stark guardsmen); Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Other wolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog (Rickon).
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort (flayed-man sigil); now commands the foot soldiers on the east bank, ordered to march south and face Tywin. The Greatjon (Lord Umber) – giant, loud lord; Rickard Karstark; Galbart and Robett Glover; Ser Helman Tallhart – chosen to command the four hundred northmen left to garrison the Twins; Lord Hornwood; Maege Mormont; Lord Cerwyn – northern lords with Robb. Ser Wylis and Ser Wendel Manderly – fat sons of Lord Wyman of White Harbor, with about 1,500 men. Howland Reed (mentioned) – little crannogman, Ned's old comrade at Greywater Watch.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey, "Lord of the Crossing" – ninety years old, wizened, bald, gouty, sharp-tongued; Hoster Tully's bannerman who keeps the only bridge over the Green Fork; scores of descendants and bastards, an eighth wife of sixteen. Held his four thousand men back until paid; resentful of the Tullys and Arryns, he bargained for the crossing and lent Robb his swords, less four hundred kept at the Twins. A Frey son is married to Tywin's sister.
  • Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, past sixty, polite, weary; Ser Jared Frey (tall, stooped, fifty), Ser Hosteen Frey, Ser Danwell Frey, Ser Perwyn Frey (youngest of the four envoys, held as Robb's guest during the talks) and Ronel Rivers (bastard) lead the Frey levies; Olyvar Frey – to be Robb's squire; Elmar Frey – Walder's youngest son, pledged to Arya; two boys both named Walder (aged eight and seven) – to be Bran's companions at Winterfell; Ryger, Whalen, Merrett – other sons named. Frey sigil: twin towers, dark blue on silver-grey.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, son of Cersei; held his first court. Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – on the small council, effectively rules; Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Small council: Tywin Lannister (Hand); Janos Slynt (new lord, given Harrenhal); Grand Maester Pycelle; Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin); Lord Varys (eunuch Master of Whisperers; visited Ned in disguise, says he serves 'the realm' and peace).
  • Kingsguard: Ser Jaime Lannister – named Lord Commander; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – made Kingsguard; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart. Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed as Lord Commander, threw down his sword and left; now wanted for treason after killing two gold cloaks sent to seize him and escaping (per Mormont's news).
  • Lords at court: Ser Dontos, Ser Balon Swann, Lord Gyles, Jalabhar Xho, the Redwyne twins, Ser Aron Santagar. Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone, feared by Cersei) named in Joffrey's summons with the Tyrells, Doran Martell, Starks, Tullys and others.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Warden of the West, now Hand of the King; fifties, bald, cold; leads about twenty thousand men north against Robb, still days south; plans to beat the Starks, then Stannis. Ser Kevan Lannister – his brother, chief lieutenant. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – crushed Edmure's host at Riverrun and now besieges it. Ser Gregor Clegane – Lannister knight who burned the Pipers and Brackens. Ser Addam Marbrand – commands their outriders, falling back south burning as he goes.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, Tywin's youngest son, freed by Catelyn; now in Tywin's camp with about three hundred hill clansmen, who insist his life is forfeit until promised steel is delivered; Varys presumes him dead. Bronn – sellsword with him; Lancel Lannister – Robert's squire, Ser Kevan's elder son. Clansmen: Shagga, Conn, Gunthor (Stone Crows), Chella (Black Ears), Ulf (Moon Brothers), Timett (one-eyed Burned Man).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother; wounded and captured by the Lannisters after losing the battle below Riverrun. Lord Hoster Tully – their father at Riverrun, sick, possibly dying. Lord Blackwood and the other survivors are besieged inside Riverrun. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, formerly Knight of the Gate; scouts ahead with picked riders, has killed Lannister scouts and watches the Twins' ravens. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance raid Tywin's rear; Jason Mallister holds Seagard; Beric Dondarrion and Thoros harass Lannister foragers; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned at the Mummer's Ford.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son, sworn brother of the Night's Watch and steward to the Lord Commander; burned his right hand badly (bandaged, healing, will scar) throwing flaming drapes onto a dead man. Back on duty and lodged in his old cell in Hardin's Tower. Has nightmares of the corpse wearing his father's face; feels craven that Robb marches to war while he stays (learned of it secretly through Sam). Now owns Longclaw, a Valyrian steel bastard sword with a white wolf's-head pommel (garnet eyes). Direwolf Ghost sleeps by his door.
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander, now living in the King's Tower; his beard was singed off and he's regrowing it. Grumbly, fond of spiced wine and his talking raven. Gave Jon Longclaw, the Mormont family sword (five centuries old, saved from the fire), which his disgraced son Jorah left behind. Believes more of the dead will come and that a terrible winter is rising. His letter for help went south; the only answer was news about Ser Barristan.
  • Maester Aemon – blind, ancient, kindly maester of Castle Black (about a hundred). Told Jon that the Watch's vows (no wives, no children, no part in the realm's wars) exist because honor is hard against love, and that everyone must choose once. Revealed he was born Aemon Targaryen, son of King Maekar and brother of King Aegon; he gave up his name for the chain and had to sit helpless as his kin were killed in the south. Clydas – his hunched, pink-eyed assistant at the rookery.
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, timid brother and Jon's friend, ex-heir of Lord Randyll Tarly (whose Valyrian sword Heartsbane will go to his brother Dickon); bought the garnets for the pommel and told Maester Aemon he'd told Jon about Robb's war, which angered Jon.
  • Jon's friends and brothers: Pyp, Grenn, Toad (fellow recruits, jokers), Halder and Pate (builders who carved the pommel), Rudge (helps in the forge), Matt. Donal Noye – one-armed armorer. Hobb – cook (the Old Bear's boiled mutton) (boiled mutton). Dywen and Hake – rangers who returned without finding Benjen Stark (Ned's brother, still missing). Bowen Marsh – round Lord Steward. Ser Endrew Tarth – arriving from the Shadow Tower as the new master-at-arms.
  • Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh, highborn master-at-arms and Jon's enemy, now relieved of his post; sent by Mormont to Eastwatch-by-the-Sea to take ship to King's Landing with the severed hand of the dead ranger Jafer Flowers as proof for King Joffrey (also puts distance between him and Jon).
  • Dead: Othor and Jafer Flowers, rangers who came back as wights (burned/cut apart); Ser Jaremy Rykker and four other men were killed by Flowers's corpse.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers), wights (animated dead), children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings, giants.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child, killed when the Lannisters took King's Landing; Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places

Places
  • The Twins – twin Frey castles linked by a great grey stone bridge over the Green Fork, with the Water Tower at its center; the only crossing for many miles. Impregnable by assault; the east castle is Lord Walder's seat. Robb's horse crossed here at evenfall.
  • The Neck and Moat Cailin (Robb's former mustering point, ruined stronghold); the Green Fork (high and fast, unfordable); Riverrun – Tully seat, under siege; Seagard, Raventree, Harrenhal, the Golden Tooth, the crossroads inn (Tywin's camp), the Mountains of the Moon, the Vale of Arryn, the kingsroad, the Trident, White Harbor, Casterly Rock.
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress; the King's Tower (Lord Commander's residence) had its top two floors gutted by Jon's fire; Hardin's Tower (Jon's cell), the rookery, the common hall; Eastwatch-by-the-Sea, the Shadow Tower (other Watch castles); Mole's Town (village nearby).
  • A Lhazareen town 🆕 new this chapter – mud-walled, burning, with a domed temple like a brown onion with the blue altar of the Great Shepherd; on the grasslands near a river bend. Meereen 🆕 new this chapter and Slaver's Bay 🆕 new this chapter (slave-trading city and bay to the south), Asshai (far eastern port of mages).
  • The Red Keep (throne room, dungeons) in King's Landing; Winterfell; the Eyrie; Dragonstone; the Wall; Vaes Dothrak (Dothraki holy city under the Mother of Mountains); the Free Cities; the Dothraki sea (grassland); the Seven Kingdoms; Bear Island.
