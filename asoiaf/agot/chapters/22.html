Chapter 22 – Arya

After a tense family supper in the Red Keep, a grieving Arya hides in her room with her sword Needle, Ned talks with her and lets her keep it, and days later a Braavosi swordsman begins teaching her.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Arya Stark – POV; 9, lonely and angry in King's Landing; blames herself for Mycah's death (she asked him to practice swords) and hates the Hound, Joffrey, the queen and the king; thinks Sansa lied about the fight to please Joffrey. Secretly owns Needle, a slender bravo's sword made by Mikken (Jon's gift, she will not betray him); Ned lets her keep it. Her wolf Nymeria is gone: Arya and Jory drove her off by throwing rocks (Arya hit her twice) to save her from the queen; Ned says he always knew she wouldn't have left willingly. Now trains in the left hand with Syrio Forel. Called "Arya Horseface" and "Arya Underfoot" before.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; tired and angry after council fights; doesn't want the tourney but will attend with his daughters. Tells Arya "winter is coming", that the lone wolf dies but the pack survives, and urges her and Sansa to stand together and begin growing up. Says Arya has the Stark "wolf blood" like Lyanna and Brandon, who both died young. Calls the murder of Mycah the Hound's (and the queen's) doing.
  • Sansa Stark – 11; wants badly to attend the tourney; at odds with Arya; sits with Septa Mordane and Jeyne Poole. Her wolf Lady is dead.
  • Catelyn (Ned's wife, on her way back to Winterfell), Robb (14, heir), Bran (7, crippled), Rickon (3), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall; taught Arya to "stick them with the pointy end"), Benjen (Ned's brother, Night's Watch). Lyanna (Ned's dead sister: beautiful, willful, died young), Brandon (Ned's dead brother), Rickard (Ned's dead father, who spoke of "the wolf blood"), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Ned's household in the Red Keep
  • Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard; kept Arya's secret about Nymeria. Vayon Poole – steward, sent Arya to her lesson. Septa Mordane – strict governess charged with making Arya a lady. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend, Vayon's daughter, told Arya about Mycah's remains. Guards (about fifty, grey wool cloaks with a silver hand pin): Fat Tom 🆕 new this chapter (genial door guard at the Tower of the Hand, calls her "Arya Underfoot"), Harwin (Hullen's son), Alyn (hopes to be a knight), Desmond, Jacks 🆕 new this chapter, Porther 🆕 new this chapter, Hullen (master of horse, endless horse talk).
  • Syrio Forel 🆕 new this chapter – bald, hooked-nose Braavosi swordsman; spent nine years as first sword to the Sealord of Braavos; Arya's new "dancing master" teaching the "water dance"; calls her "boy"; has Arya hold the wooden sword in her left hand; hollow lead-filled practice swords.
  • At Winterfell: Ser Rodrik (household knight), Maester Luwin (maester), Mikken (smith, made Needle), Hodor (simple stableboy), Septon Chayle (library), Old Nan, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward). Mycah – the butcher's boy, dead.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); Robert Arryn – her sickly son; Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – ordered the tourney in Ned's honor. Prince Joffrey – crown prince, hated by Arya, said to have lied. Queen Cersei – his mother, Robert's wife. Princess Myrcella (younger than Sansa; will attend) and Tommen – the younger royal children. Sandor "the Hound" – Joffrey's bodyguard, killed Mycah per Ned and Jeyne. Ser Jaime Lannister (Kingsguard, the queen's twin), Ser Ilyn Payne (King's Justice), Tywin Lannister (Lannister head, mentioned), Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish (master of coin), Varys (master of whisperers), Pycelle (Grand Maester), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan (Kingsguard Lord Commander), Tyrion (the queen's dwarf brother, heading south from the Wall).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms), Donal Noye (armorer), recruits (Grenn, Pyp, Todder, etc.; Jon Snow among them), Benjen (First Ranger, missing), Yoren, Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others in the prologue; Castle Black thinks him missing).
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.
