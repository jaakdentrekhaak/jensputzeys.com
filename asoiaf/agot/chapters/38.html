Chapter 38 – Tyrion

Imprisoned in a sky cell at the Eyrie, Tyrion bribes his brutal gaoler into carrying a false offer to confess, then demands trial by combat, and Bronn volunteers to be his champion.

Characters

Catelyn's party and captives
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Lannister dwarf (Tywin's youngest son), prisoner in an open-fronted "sky cell" at the Eyrie, beaten and starved, with a sloping floor; talks his way out: bribed the gaoler Mord with a promise of Lannister gold, then offered a mock "confession" (vices only; denies the murders) before Lysa's court, and demanded trial by combat. Reflects that the Starks have no proof; thinks the clumsy dagger attempt on Bran does not match the subtle death of Jon Arryn, and suspects someone else may be using him as a catspaw. Reclaimed his shadowskin cloak. Is certain Cersei and Jaime are rash.
  • Bronn – sellsword who joined Tyrion's guard on the road; volunteered to be Tyrion's champion; had carried Tyrion when his legs gave out on the last climb. Marillion – singer, new woodharp, broken fingers. Ser Rodrik Cassel – Winterfell's master-at-arms, still pale from wounds. Ser Willis Wode – Harrenhal hedge knight, with Rodrik.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; objected to Lysa's treatment, saying Tyrion is her prisoner and she wants him unharmed; skeptical he is truly broken; thought the Moon Door unwise.
House Tully / House Arryn / the Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Jon Arryn's widow and Catelyn's sister; vain, shrill, petulant; accuses Tyrion of murdering Jon Arryn; sent him to the sky cells; opened the Moon Door; has agreed to trial by combat, naming Ser Vardis Egen champion.
  • Robert Arryn – six, sickly Lord of the Eyrie, giggling, trembling, with a doll; cried "I want to see him fly."
  • Ser Vardis Egen – silver-haired, humorless captain of Jon Arryn's guard (once at King's Landing); reluctant to fight a cripple; will fight Tyrion's champion tomorrow.
  • Mord 🆕 new this chapter – gaoler of the sky cells, huge, near-wordless, scarred (missing ear), cruel; greedy and superstitiously respects writing.
  • Lords of the Vale (at the confession): Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate), Lord Nestor Royce (High Steward of the Vale), his heir Ser Albar Royce 🆕 new this chapter (black side-whiskers), Ser Lyn Corbray (slender, vain of his sword), Lord Hunter 🆕 new this chapter (gouty, old), Lady Waynwood 🆕 new this chapter (widow, with her sons), and a young knight with a green viper sigil; also Ser Donnel Waynwood (young knight who escorted Catelyn), Mya Stone (mule guide), Maester Colemon (Eyrie maester). Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Riverrun), Edmure (her brother); Jon Arryn (dead Hand).
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark (fifteen; ruling Winterfell, weighing calling the banners after Jory's death and Ned's wound), Bran (eight, crippled; attacked by deserters but saved), Rickon (youngest, small), Ned (Catelyn's husband, wounded in King's Landing), Sansa and Arya (Ned's daughters, in King's Landing), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves Summer (Bran's) and Grey Wind (Robb's). Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Wyl, Heward (guardsmen) – killed in the capital. Tyrion believes the Stark boy's attacker was sent clumsily.
  • Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy (Ned's smirking ward), Maester Luwin, Osha (captured wildling woman), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Joseth (stableman), Hodor (simple giant), Old Nan, Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Vayon Poole (steward), Mikken (smith).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tyrion's rash brother, who had Ned's men killed; Tyrion expects him to come with a host or ride north. Tywin Lannister (Tyrion's father, lord of Casterly Rock; Tyrion assumes he has sent riders), Queen Cersei (their proud sister; "low cunning"), King Robert (her husband), Joffrey (12, heir), Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of coin), Grand Maester Pycelle, Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) – at court.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, new brother), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife; at Vaes Dothrak she struck her brother Viserys across the face with a medallion belt after he abused her; keeps a green dragon egg.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her proud, cruel brother, the self-styled king ("the dragon"); mocked by the Dothraki; cut on the cheek, impatient for the army Drogo promised.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, Dothraki warlord with forty thousand riders; at Vaes Dothrak. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her (son of Jeor Mormont); hates Ned Stark. Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho – Drogo's bloodriders; Aggo, Rakharo – her khas; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – her handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses of Vaes Dothrak.

Places

Places
  • The Eyrie – Lysa's seat atop the mountain; its High Hall (blue-veined white marble, weirwood thrones, moon-and-falcon banners), the sky cells (open-fronted dungeon cells with a sloping floor and six-hundred-foot drop), and the Moon Door (weirwood door opening onto the sheer drop, used to execute) are where Tyrion is held and tried. Impregnable to attack; the climb to it is brutal.
  • The Vale of Arryn / the Bloody Gate – the valley and its gate. Winterfell, the wolfswood, Moat Cailin, Vaes Dothrak, King's Landing / the Red Keep, Casterly Rock (Lannister seat, full of gold), Storm's End, Riverrun, the Wall, Highgarden, Dragonstone, Pentos, Seagard, Harrenhal.
