Chapter 62 – Tyrion

Tyrion, shamed by his father and given a whore named Shae, leads his hill clansmen in the Lannister van when Tywin's army is attacked at dawn, is knocked down but captures a northern knight, and learns the battle was won while Robb Stark was elsewhere.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Silver Lady" – exiled Targaryen princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo, expecting a son she calls Rhaego; her time is near. Survived a poisoning attempt; her three dragon's eggs showed no sign of life in a brazier. After the sack of a Lhazareen town she defied her khas to stop the rapes, claiming the victims as her slaves ("I am the blood of the dragon"), and asked Mirri Maz Duur to attend her birth.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord (braid hung with bells); vowed to cross the sea and take the Seven Kingdoms for his son. Killed Khal Ogo and Ogo's son Fogo in battle; wounded (arrow in the arm, a gash across the chest) but refuses to rest; let Dany keep her slaves. Bloodriders: Cohollo (old, kind to Dany), Qotho (cruel, sneers at Dany), Haggo (huge, silent). Mago – rider who complained Dany took his captive.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), Dany's adviser; stopped the poisoner; advises selling the captives at Meereen to buy ships; said her mercy shows she is Rhaegar's sister (not Viserys's). Her handmaids Doreah (Lysene), Irri, Jhiqui; young khas warriors Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo (killed a rapist), Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings, writes to Jorah. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
  • Mirri Maz Duur – Lhazareen godswife (healer-priestess) of the Great Shepherd, old, heavy, flat-nosed; captured and claimed by Dany. Studied in Asshai (where a Citadel-trained maester named Marwyn taught her); claims never to have lost a babe. Treated Drogo's wounds despite Qotho's threat ("as the khal fares, so shall you"); the bloodriders call her maegi (blood-sorceress). Lhazareen ("Lamb Men") – squat, flat-faced sheep-herding people; perhaps ten thousand captives to be sold as slaves.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son (wolf Grey Wind); led his host through the Twins after Walder Frey exacted a price (Robb to wed one of Frey's daughters, Olyvar as squire, Arya to wed Elmar if found). His northern foot under Roose Bolton was beaten by Tywin on the kingsroad while Robb crossed at the Twins with most of the horse, riding hard for Riverrun.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, with Robb's host; bargained the Twins' crossing from Walder Frey, who told her Jon Arryn meant to foster Lysa's boy with Stannis. Takes two young Frey boys to Winterfell as wards.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, formerly King's Hand; held in a dark dungeon under the Red Keep, feverish and starved. Refused Varys's offer of his life for a false confession. Sansa Stark – elder daughter, still betrothed to Joffrey and kept at court; begged Joffrey for mercy for Ned. Arya Stark – younger daughter, fled Ser Meryn; no one has found her. Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord in Winterfell; Rickon – youngest, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, now at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan), Maester Luwin (castle maester), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, cocky, with Robb), Hodor (huge simple stableboy who only says "Hodor"), Osha (captured wildling woman), Old Nan (storyteller), Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard); Ned's guards Alyn and Harwin. Dead: Jory Cassel (Ned's guard captain), Hullen (master of horse), Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Wolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind, Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort; commanded the northern foot that fought Tywin on the kingsroad and was beaten; he escaped the field. Lord Hornwood (bull moose banner) is dead; Lord Cerwyn (battle-axe banner), Ser Wylis Manderly (fat son of Lord Wyman, White Harbor), Harrion Karstark (Karstark sunburst banner) and four Freys were taken prisoner. Also in the host: The Greatjon (Umber), Rickard Karstark, Galbart and Robett Glover (mailed fist banner), Ser Helman Tallhart (garrisoning the Twins), Maege Mormont, Ser Wendel Manderly. Howland Reed (mentioned) – Ned's friend at Greywater Watch. An unnamed northern knight with a morningstar who shouted "For Eddard and Winterfell!" unhorsed Tyrion and then yielded to him.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey, "Lord of the Crossing" – ninety, wizened, gouty, sharp-tongued, with an eighth wife of sixteen and scores of descendants; Hoster Tully's bannerman who holds the only bridge over the Green Fork; resentful of Tullys and Arryns. Sold Robb the crossing and his swords (four hundred kept home). A Frey son is married to Tywin's sister.
  • Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, past sixty; Ser Jared, Ser Hosteen, Ser Danwell (sons), Ronel Rivers (bastard) led the Frey levies; Ser Perwyn (youngest envoy); Olyvar Frey – Robb's new squire; Elmar Frey – youngest son, pledged to Arya; two boys both named Walder (eight and seven), wards for Winterfell. Four Freys were captured fighting for Robb on the kingsroad. Sigil: twin towers, blue on grey.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, son of Cersei; held his first court. Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – on the small council, effectively rules; Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Small council: Tywin Lannister (Hand); Janos Slynt (new lord, given Harrenhal); Grand Maester Pycelle; Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin); Lord Varys (eunuch Master of Whisperers; visited Ned in disguise, says he serves 'the realm' and peace).
  • Kingsguard: Ser Jaime Lannister – named Lord Commander; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – made Kingsguard; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart. Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed as Lord Commander, threw down his sword and left; now wanted for treason after killing two gold cloaks sent to seize him and escaping (per Mormont's news).
  • Lords at court: Ser Dontos, Ser Balon Swann, Lord Gyles, Jalabhar Xho, the Redwyne twins, Ser Aron Santagar. Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone, feared by Cersei) named in Joffrey's summons with the Tyrells, Doran Martell, Starks, Tullys and others.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Warden of the West, Hand of the King; bald, cold, in ornate crimson-and-gold armor with gold lions; usually commands the reserve from high ground. Sneered at Tyrion at table and deliberately put him in the van under Gregor as bait: he planned to feign a collapse on the left to lure Robb in, then crush him with Kevan's pikes and his reserve. Won the battle by the kingsroad, though Robb was not there to be caught. Plans to defeat the Starks, then Stannis. Ser Kevan Lannister – his brother, chief lieutenant, led the center. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – crushed Edmure's host at Riverrun, now besieges it. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – huge brutish knight (three black dogs on yellow, greatsword wielded one-handed) who burned the Pipers and Brackens; commanded the van and lost his horse. Ser Addam Marbrand – commanded the cavalry right wing (burning tree banner), dark copper hair to his shoulders. Lannister bannermen: Lord Lefford 🆕 new this chapter (sour keeper of stores and supplies), Lydden, Serrett 🆕 new this chapter, Ser Flement Brax (purple unicorn banner), the Crakehalls (boar) and Swyfts 🆕 new this chapter (bantam rooster).
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp", "Halfman" – Cersei's dwarf brother, Tywin's youngest son; arrived in his father's camp with about three hundred hill clansmen and a mercenary retinue. Humiliated by Tywin at dinner, he served under Ser Gregor in the van in mismatched armor (his own plate is at Casterly Rock), killed a swordsman, was felled by a morningstar knight and forced him to yield, but hurt his right elbow badly and wants a maester. Defiant toward his father, who admits he put him in danger and kept it secret. Varys presumes him dead. Bronn – sellsword, wry bodyguard who took Shae from another knight by threat. Shae 🆕 new this chapter – young dark-haired camp follower, slim and bold-spoken; found for Tyrion by Bronn and now sharing his tent, bound to take no other man while she is with him. Podrick Payne 🆕 new this chapter – his new squire, a quiet boy and distant cousin of Ser Ilyn Payne; a groom and body servant were also lent by Tywin. Clansmen: Shagga (huge Stone Crow, fights with two axes, wounded by arrows but alive), Conn son of Coratt (Stone Crow, killed in the battle), Gunthor (Stone Crow chief, raising more clans), Chella (Black Ears scout, took four ears), Ulf son of Umar (Moon Brothers; found lying in his blood with his arm severed among a dozen fallen followers, apparently dead), Timett son of Timett (one-eyed Burned Man war chief, alive). About half the three hundred survived.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother; wounded and captured by the Lannisters after losing the battle below Riverrun. Lord Hoster Tully – their father at Riverrun, sick, possibly dying. Lord Blackwood and the other survivors are besieged inside Riverrun. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, formerly Knight of the Gate; scouts ahead with picked riders, has killed Lannister scouts and watches the Twins' ravens. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance raid Tywin's rear; Jason Mallister holds Seagard; Beric Dondarrion and Thoros harass Lannister foragers; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned at the Mummer's Ford.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward to the Lord Commander; burned his right hand throwing flaming drapes onto a dead man (wight). Lodged in Hardin's Tower; feels craven that Robb is at war while he stays. Owns Longclaw, a Valyrian steel bastard sword given by Mormont (white wolf's-head pommel). Direwolf Ghost.
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander, now in the King's Tower; singed beard regrowing; fond of his talking raven. Believes more of the dead will come and a terrible winter is rising; his letter for help brought only news of Barristan.
  • Maester Aemon – blind, ancient maester of Castle Black; revealed he was born Aemon Targaryen, son of King Maekar and brother of King Aegon, who put aside his name for the chain. Clydas – his hunched, pink-eyed assistant.
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, timid brother and Jon's friend, former heir of Lord Randyll Tarly. Jon's friends: Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder, Pate, Rudge, Matt. Donal Noye – one-armed armorer. Hobb – cook. Dywen and Hake – rangers who found no trace of Benjen Stark (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing). Bowen Marsh – round Lord Steward. Ser Endrew Tarth – new master-at-arms coming from the Shadow Tower.
  • Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh, highborn former master-at-arms and Jon's enemy; sent to Eastwatch and on to King's Landing with the severed hand of a wight as proof for Joffrey.
  • Dead: Othor and Jafer Flowers (rangers who returned as wights and were destroyed); Ser Jaremy Rykker (killed by Flowers's corpse) and four others.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers), wights (animated dead), children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings, giants.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child, killed when the Lannisters took King's Landing; Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places

Places
  • The Twins – twin Frey castles linked by a great grey stone bridge over the Green Fork, with the Water Tower at its center; the only crossing for many miles. Impregnable by assault; the east castle is Lord Walder's seat. Robb's horse crossed here at evenfall.
  • The Neck and Moat Cailin (Robb's former mustering point, ruined stronghold); the Green Fork (high and fast, unfordable); Riverrun – Tully seat, under siege; Seagard, Raventree, Harrenhal, the Golden Tooth, the crossroads inn (Tywin's camp), the Mountains of the Moon, the Vale of Arryn, the kingsroad, the Trident, White Harbor, Casterly Rock.
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch fortress; the King's Tower (Lord Commander's residence) had its top two floors gutted by Jon's fire; Hardin's Tower (Jon's cell), the rookery, the common hall; Eastwatch-by-the-Sea, the Shadow Tower (other Watch castles); Mole's Town (village nearby).
  • A Lhazareen town – mud-walled, burning, with a domed temple like a brown onion with the blue altar of the Great Shepherd; on the grasslands near a river bend. Meereen and Slaver's Bay, Asshai (far eastern port of mages).
  • The battlefield on the kingsroad 🆕 new this chapter – hilly, farmed ground between the kingsroad and the river, a day or so south of the Twins, where the Lannister camp stood; Tywin's army defeated the northern foot here.
  • The Red Keep (throne room, dungeons) in King's Landing; Winterfell; the Eyrie; Dragonstone; the Wall; Vaes Dothrak (Dothraki holy city under the Mother of Mountains); the Free Cities; the Dothraki sea (grassland); the Seven Kingdoms; Bear Island.
