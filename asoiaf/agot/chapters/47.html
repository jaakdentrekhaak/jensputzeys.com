Chapter 47 – Eddard

Summoned at night to the king's bedside, Ned finds Robert mortally gored by a boar, writes his last will naming Ned Protector of the Realm, then plans his next moves with Renly's offer refused and Littlefinger's help accepted.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand, now named Lord Regent and Protector of the Realm in Robert's sealed will (he wrote "my heir" instead of "my son Joffrey"; the letter is unopened and to be given to the council after Robert dies). Leg still in plaster. Knows Joffrey and Tommen (and, Littlefinger assumes, Myrcella) are Jaime's bastards, so by right Stannis is heir. Refused Renly's plan to seize the queen's children; has asked Varys to undo any arrangement against Daenerys; wrote to Stannis urging him to sail to King's Landing at once; asked Littlefinger to buy the City Watch; hopes to go home to Winterfell soon. Haunted by a dream of Lyanna's statue whispering "Promise me, Ned".
  • Sansa Stark and Arya Stark – his daughters, to leave on the Wind Witch on the evening tide with a twenty-man escort. Tomard "Fat Tom" – loyal portly guardsman, commands the household guard and now the escort; will carry Ned's letter to Lord Stannis at Dragonstone. Cayn and Desmond – Ned's guardsmen. Porther – brave but headstrong guard, passed over. Varly, Alyn (rode out with Lord Beric), Harwin. Dead guards Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl. Septa Mordane, Jeyne Poole, Vayon Poole (steward, arranged the ship).
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled; Ned thinks he stumbled on the truth), Rickon, Jon Snow (bastard at the Wall), Benjen (missing); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; dead Lyanna (Robert's lost love, Ned's sister) and Brandon. Ser Rodrik Cassel. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – back from the hunt, gored from groin to chest by a boar he insisted on facing alone while drunk; the wound has festered and Pycelle says he cannot live. He killed the boar with a knife through its eye. Lucid but fading; had regrets over the order to kill Daenerys ("only a girl") and asked Ned to let her live, to help Joffrey be better, and to serve the boar at his funeral feast. Drank the milk of the poppy and sleeps.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – at his bedside; sent out. Ned hopes she flees with her children. Prince Joffrey – about twelve, heir, Sansa's betrothed. Tommen and Myrcella – her younger children. Barra – Robert's baby bastard (with her mother); Mya (in the Vale) and Gendry (smith) – other bastards Robert fathered.
  • Lord Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother, young, handsome, dark; offered Ned a hundred swords to seize Joffrey and the young princes immediately; Ned refused. Lord Stannis Baratheon – the elder of Robert's brothers, at Dragonstone, hard and unforgiving; next in line if Joffrey is not Robert's.
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – physician to the king, Cersei's creature. Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; hinted to Littlefinger about the regency; suggests the squire's wine may be suspect; says the Daenerys arrangements may be too late to undo. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; urged Ned to back Joffrey and bargain with the Lannisters, was rebuffed, then agreed to bribe Janos Slynt and the City Watch (about 6,000 gold) for Ned. Janos Slynt – Commander of the City Watch (gold cloaks), about 2,000 men.
  • Kingsguard: Ser Barristan Selmy – old Lord Commander, ashamed he failed Robert; Ser Boros Blount and Ser Preston Greenfield 🆕 new this chapter – guard Maegor's Holdfast. Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Arys Oakheart. (Not Kingsguard: Ser Robar Royce – found the king; Ser Balon Swann.) Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin lover, fled; may be riding on King's Landing. Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock; sellswords gathering there. Lancel – Robert's elder squire, Ser Kevan Lannister's son, cousin to the queen; kept the king's wine skins full. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – wanted for massacres; Robert says his head is being taken. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's shield, Gregor's brother. Lord Beric Dondarrion with Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar – sent against Clegane. Ser Loras Tyrell, Ser Ilyn Payne, Bronze Yohn Royce, Lady Tanda, Redwyne twins – lords at court whom Littlefinger lists as possible Lannister rivals. Jon Arryn – late Hand, killed (Ned says) for the secret. Yoren – Night's Watch recruiter.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, defending Riverrun; Hoster Tully – their father; Ser Raymun Darry, Ser Karyl Vance, Ser Marq Piper – riverlands knights who sought leave to strike back at Clegane. Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; Robert Arryn – her sickly boy lord; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the hill clan Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot); Littlefinger suggests releasing him as part of a peace.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, a recruit; Samwell Tarly – fat, bookish recruit; Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder – fellow recruits; Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh master-at-arms; Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Maester Aemon – blind, aged maester; Benjen – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife, expecting a son, Rhaego; Robert had wanted her dead and now regrets it. Viserys Targaryen – her brother, killed by Drogo with molten gold. Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord, her husband, with bloodriders Qotho, Haggo, Cohollo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – her handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo; Khals Jommo and Ogo; dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses of Vaes Dothrak. Rhaegar's children (mentioned) – killed when King's Landing fell.
Other
  • Captain Qos 🆕 new this chapter – master of the Wind Witch. Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master teaching Arya. Grand Maester Malleon – author of the lineage book. Lady Ashara (mentioned). Prince Aemon the Dragonknight, Queen Naerys, Jonquil and Ser Florian – song heroes.

Places

Places
  • Maegor's Holdfast 🆕 new this chapter – fortress-within-the-Red Keep with spiked dry moat and a bridge, holding the royal apartments, where Robert lies. The Red Keep / King's Landing – the capital; the Tower of the Hand; the godswood; the Iron Throne room; the kingswood (where Robert was hunting); the Blackwater.
  • Dragonstone – island seat of Stannis, with a sinister repute; ships heading north pass it. Winterfell, Braavos (home of the Wind Witch), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat, sellswords gathering), Storm's End, the Eyrie, Riverrun, Castle Black / the Wall, Vaes Dothrak.
