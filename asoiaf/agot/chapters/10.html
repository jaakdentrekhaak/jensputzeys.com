Chapter 10 – Jon

On the morning the royal party and the Starks prepare to leave, Jon says goodbye to the comatose Bran (enduring Catelyn's hostility), to Robb, and to Arya, to whom he gives a sword named Needle.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14; about to leave for the Night's Watch with Uncle Benjen, expecting to be a Sworn Brother soon. Visited Bran despite Catelyn ordering him out; wept, kissed Bran goodbye. Gave Arya a sword; jokes with Robb. White wolf Ghost at his side. Heading north today.
  • Bran Stark – 7; still unconscious, emaciated, back broken and legs twisted, but breathing. Kept alive on honey, water and herbs. His unnamed direwolf howls beneath the window.
  • Catelyn Stark (Lady Stark, Catelyn Tully) – Bran's mother; has stayed at his bedside day and night for nearly a fortnight, feeding him herself; haggard, aged, flat and bitter. Prayed that Ned would leave Bran home with her. Called Jon "bastard", said "It should have been you", then wept.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, to go south as Hand with the king; the household is packing to depart (taking Sansa and Arya).
  • Robb Stark – seems to have grown stronger and more commanding since the fall; organising the yard. Says Bran will not die. Direwolf Grey Wind. Farewell to Jon: "Farewell, Snow" / "Stark".
  • Arya Stark – young, wilful, hates southron-lady fuss; packing to go south. Received the sword Needle 🆕 new this chapter (thin, blue-sheened, made by Mikken, a bravo-style blade for stabbing) and must hide it from Septa Mordane and Sansa. Direwolf Nymeria.
  • Sansa (11, to marry Prince Joffrey, going south) and Rickon (3, staying) – other children (not seen; wolves Lady, Shaggydog).
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, Night's Watch; impatient to leave with Jon today before the snows. Rickard, Brandon, Lyanna – Ned's dead father, brother and sister.
Winterfell household
  • Septa Mordane – the girls' strict septa; insists Arya fold her clothes like a proper southron lady. Mikken – smith; made Needle. Maester Luwin – Winterfell's maester. Ser Rodrik – household knight. Jory Cassel – captain of the guard. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward. Hodor – simple stableboy. Old Nan – storyteller. Chayle – young septon who keeps the library. Hullen, Harwin – master of horse and his son.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, who accuses the Lannisters of murdering her husband Jon Arryn (dead, former Hand); her son Robert Arryn is sickly Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle (Knight of the Gate) and father (Lord of Riverrun).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – the king, taking Ned south as Hand. Queen Cersei Lannister – his wife. Ser Jaime Lannister – her twin, Kingsguard. Prince Joffrey (crown prince), Tommen, Myrcella – the royal children. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's burned bodyguard. Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother; plans to visit the Wall with Benjen and Jon. The royal party is preparing to leave Winterfell.
  • Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – Lannister head; Maester Pycelle – royal maester; Kingsguard – Ser Boros and Ser Meryn (with the party), Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander, at King's Landing); Stannis, Renly (mentioned) – Robert's brothers; Littlefinger (mentioned) – court figure; Mad King Aerys II, Rhaegar (dead) – overthrown king and his son; Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother, taking Jon north); Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, given to Khal Drogo) and Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king); Illyrio Mopatis (fat Pentoshi magister); Khal Drogo (huge Dothraki warlord); Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight).

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; today a chaos of wagons and horses as the party departs. Includes the sickroom, library tower, yard, armory, and a covered bridge to the Keep.
  • The Wall / Castle Black – Jon's destination in the north. King's Landing – the capital, "a thousand times the size of Winterfell", where Arya will go.
  • Pentos, Myr and the Free Cities (bravos fight with thin swords), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), The Trident, Starfall, Iron Islands, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
