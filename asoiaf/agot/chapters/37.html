Chapter 37 – Bran

Bran takes his first ride outside Winterfell since his fall, learns of Jory's death and Ned's injury, and is attacked in the wolfswood by Night's Watch deserters and a wildling woman until Robb and the direwolves intervene.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Bran Stark – eight, second Stark son, crippled (legs cannot feel or grip) by his fall; heir to Winterfell after Robb. Rode out of the castle for the first time since, on his filly Dancer, strapped into a saddle designed by Tyrion; cheerful, then shaken by the news from King's Landing. Cut on the thigh by the outlaws (felt nothing); calls the wolves "direwolves". His wolf is Summer.
  • Robb Stark – fifteen, eldest son, running Winterfell in Ned's and Catelyn's absence; auburn, now stubbled and shaggy. Has sent riders through the north (talk of Moat Cailin) after Catelyn's raven. Learned Jory is dead and Ned is gravely hurt; promises "this will not be forgotten" and is weighing calling the banners (Theon urges it). Killed one attacker and rode down Osha; furious at Theon and his guards afterward. His wolf is Grey Wind.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell; in King's Landing, leg shattered when his horse fell in Jaime Lannister's ambush; under milk of the poppy, unknown when he will wake. Word came by raven from Alyn.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; wrote from the Eyrie that she has taken Tyrion prisoner but gave no date for returning. Jory Cassel, Wyl, Heward – murdered by the Kingslayer in the capital (Jory long-time captain of Winterfell's guard, who used to chase Bran over the roofs and once took the boys fishing). Alyn – Ned's man in King's Landing who sent the news.
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard son, at the Wall; Bran misses him), Rickon (youngest brother, small), Sansa (elder sister, betrothed to Joffrey) and Arya (younger sister) (both in King's Landing), Benjen (uncle, First Ranger, still missing per the Lord Commander); Lyanna (Ned's dead sister).
  • Direwolves – Summer (Bran's) and Grey Wind (Robb's), half-grown, large and lean-jawed; killed two or three attackers, Summer feeding on one.
Winterfell household
  • Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward (son of Balon Greyjoy), a smiling, mocking lord's son and Robb's close companion; boasts of local girls (Kyra, Bessa); urges Robb to call the banners; shot Stiv dead with one arrow but had lingered behind to hunt a turkey and left Bran alone; Robb rebuked him. Bran has never warmed to him.
  • Maester Luwin – Winterfell's maester, cautious ("timid" to Theon), counsels Robb, rides a donkey, tended Bran's cut. Hallis (Hal) Mollen – captain of the Winterfell guard; insisted Bran have an escort. Joseth – stick-thin stableman, acting master of horse while Hullen is away; trained Dancer. Hodor – stableboy, helped Bran ride. Quent, Wayn – young guardsmen. Farlen – kennelmaster. Old Nan – ancient storyteller ("dark wings, dark words"). Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Vayon Poole (steward), Mikken (smith) – as before (the first two in King's Landing with Ned's party).
  • Osha 🆕 new this chapter – tall, lean wildling woman with a black-oak spear; captured; says she broke no oaths (the black crows have no place for women) and begs for her life; Robb is taking her back to Winterfell to be questioned. Stiv 🆕 new this chapter (bald), Wallen 🆕 new this chapter (grey-stubbled), Hali 🆕 new this chapter, and others – Watch deserters and wildlings attacking Bran; all killed except Osha.
Catelyn's party / House Tully / House Arryn
  • Bronn (sellsword), Marillion (singer), Ser Rodrik Cassel (Winterfell's master-at-arms, wounded) – with Catelyn in the Vale. Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister, Lady of the Eyrie), Robert Arryn (her sickly son), Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate), Nestor Royce (High Steward of the Vale), Ser Donnel Waynwood (young knight), Mya Stone (mule guide), Maester Colemon (Eyrie maester); Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Riverrun), Edmure (her brother); Jon Arryn (dead Hand).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin; led a Lannister ambush in King's Landing hunting his brother Tyrion and had Ned's men killed; Winterfell now calls him murderer. Tregar – his guard captain.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – prisoner at the Eyrie; Bran liked him (he designed Bran's saddle) before the name Lannister began to frighten him.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – master of coin, glib; brought the City Watch to the wounded Ned. Grand Maester Pycelle – treating Ned. King Robert (Ned's friend; has bastards, including baby Barra at Chataya's brothel and a boy at Storm's End), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) – at court. Rhaegar and Aerys – dead Targaryens.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, new brother), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife; at Vaes Dothrak she struck her brother Viserys across the face with a medallion belt after he abused her; keeps a green dragon egg.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her proud, cruel brother, the self-styled king ("the dragon"); mocked by the Dothraki; cut on the cheek, impatient for the army Drogo promised.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, Dothraki warlord with forty thousand riders; at Vaes Dothrak. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her (son of Jeor Mormont); hates Ned Stark. Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho – Drogo's bloodriders; Aggo, Rakharo – her khas; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – her handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses of Vaes Dothrak.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat; Robb rules it for now; its winter town (mostly empty village outside, fills when winter comes) and market square, alehouse the Smoking Log.
  • The wolfswood 🆕 new this chapter – forest beyond the winter town; stream with a ford where Bran was attacked. The kingsroad runs by it. Moat Cailin – ancient First Men fortress at the top of the Neck, named in Robb's orders.
  • Vaes Dothrak – sacred Dothraki city under the Mother of Mountains. The Eyrie, the Vale of Arryn – Lysa's seat. King's Landing / the Red Keep – capital; Ned lies wounded. Chataya's brothel, Storm's End, Casterly Rock, Riverrun, the Wall, Highgarden, Dragonstone, Pentos, Seagard, Harrenhal.
