Chapter 16 – Eddard

After four days of searching, Arya is found and brought before King Robert, who lets the queen have Arya's direwolf punished; Ned himself kills Sansa's wolf Lady, and the Hound brings back the butcher's boy Mycah dead.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; led the search for Arya himself for three days, barely slept. Furious at the queen for bypassing him, but dutiful to Robert. Begged the king to spare the wolf "for the love you bore my sister", then, to spare Lady a headsman's blade, killed her himself with his sword Ice, and ordered her body carried to Winterfell to be buried so the queen would not have the pelt. Cold to Robert afterwards; now confronted with Mycah's body.
  • Arya Stark – 9; found by Jory after four days hiding; hungry, repentant, but insisted Joffrey was the aggressor. Attacked Sansa in the hall for lying. Her wolf Nymeria escaped and has not been found (the queen offers a hundred golden dragons for her skin). Arya has yet to learn Mycah is dead.
  • Sansa Stark – told the court "I don't remember" rather than back Arya's account against Joffrey (Ned knew she had told him the truth the night Arya vanished). Heartbroken and desperate when Lady's fate was decided; begged for her. Her direwolf Lady – smallest, prettiest, gentlest of the litter, is dead (killed by Ned).
  • Left behind: Catelyn (Ned's wife, about to set out secretly for King's Landing), Robb (heir), Bran (7, hurt, unconscious), Rickon (3). Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, gone to the Wall. Lyanna (Ned's dead sister, whom Robert once loved), Rickard, Brandon (Ned's dead father and brother), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household (with the party)
  • Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard; found Arya and brought her in; told the court no trace of the direwolf; takes the girls away and fetches Ice. Vayon Poole (Ned's steward, the "Poole" of earlier chapters) – brings Sansa to the hall. Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Hullen (master of horse). At Winterfell: Maester Luwin, Ser Rodrik Cassel, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (new captain of the guard), Hodor, Old Nan.
  • Mycah – the butcher's boy, 13, Arya's friend; found dead, cut almost in half from shoulder to waist, ridden down by the Hound ("He ran… but not very fast").
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); her son Robert Arryn is Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – burly, bearded, weary of the quarrel; wanted it settled quickly, said children fight and no lasting harm done; told Ned to discipline Arya and he would discipline Joffrey; let the queen have the wolf but would not pay for it ("Damn you, Cersei"); left the grim task to others. Ned's old friend, "closer than any brother".
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – furious, demanded Arya be punished and the wolf killed; taunted Robert as unlike the king she thought she'd wed; wanted Ser Ilyn to execute the wolf; suspicious when Ned offered to do it. Her men (Lannister guards) delayed telling Ned that Arya was found.
  • Prince Joffrey – arm still in silken bandages; lied that Arya and Mycah attacked him with clubs; smirked when the wolf was condemned.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" and Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – both away leading searches north of the Trident; the Hound returned with Mycah's body, mocking Ned.
  • Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, grave; told the queen where the wolf was chained. Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother, Lord of Storm's End; laughed at Arya's tale of throwing Lion's Tooth in the river; sent out by the king. Ser Ilyn Payne (the King's Justice, mute) – summoned by the queen but not used. Ser Raymun Darry 🆕 new this chapter – lord of the castle where the party is staying, an unwilling host whose three older brothers died fighting for Rhaegar at the Trident; "lives under the king's peace".
  • Princess Myrcella, Prince Tommen (the younger royal children), Tywin Lannister (mentioned; Lannister head), Maester Pycelle, Stannis (Robert's other brother), Littlefinger, Varys, Balon Greyjoy (self-styled King of the Iron Islands) – all mentioned only; Mad King Aerys II (dead), Rhaegar (dead); Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother, north at the Wall.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother) and Yoren (black brother) – with Jon heading north; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • Ser Raymun Darry's castle 🆕 new this chapter – small holding half a day's ride south of the Trident where the king's party (king's, Lannister, Darry and Stark men) is crowded in; tense. The audience chamber was taken by the king.
  • The Trident – river where Robert killed Rhaegar; Joffrey's sword Lion's Tooth lies at the bottom. Searches of both banks found Arya.
  • The Neck (boggy region behind the party), King's Landing (capital, destination), Winterfell (Stark seat; Lady's body to be buried there), White Knife / White Harbor (Catelyn's planned route), The Wall / Castle Black, Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Baratheon seat), Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
