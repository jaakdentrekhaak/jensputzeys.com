Chapter 44 – Sansa

After Lord Beric's party rides out to bring Gregor Clegane to justice, Sansa and Arya fight over Joffrey, and Ned tells his daughters he is sending them home to Winterfell by ship for their safety.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter (about eleven), romantic, dreams of songs and being queen; betrothed to Joffrey, whom she defends, blaming the Kingslayer, not him, for the attack on Jory; despises Arya's rough ways; wept and begged not to leave King's Landing. Her direwolf Lady is dead; she still dreams of her. Idolizes Ser Loras.
  • Arya Stark – Ned's younger daughter, wild and tomboyish; blames Jaime for the deaths of Jory, Heward and Wyl and the Hound for killing Mycah, the butcher's boy; flung a blood orange at Sansa; takes dancing lessons from Syrio Forel and wants him to come home with her; glad to go home. Has the sword Needle (from Jon).
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; leg in plaster; sent Lord Beric to kill Clegane; fears for his daughters while Robert hunts; calls the Joffrey match a terrible mistake and means to send the girls home secretly by fast trading galley with Septa Mordane, guards and Syrio if he agrees.
  • Septa Mordane – the girls' stern septa/governess. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend, steward Vayon Poole's pretty dark-haired daughter, who moons over Lord Beric. Vayon Poole – Winterfell's steward, dines with Ned. Alyn – Ned's guard, now carrying the Stark banner with Lord Beric's party; Harwin – another of Ned's guards. Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl – dead Stark guards. Mycah – dead butcher's boy.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon, Jon Snow (bastard at the Wall), Benjen (missing); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost. Lyanna, Brandon (dead). Ser Rodrik Cassel with Catelyn. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing (sword) master teaching Arya.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunken hunter, away chasing the white hart; Ned's old friend. Queen Cersei Lannister – gave Sansa the ivory silk dress after the betrothal. Prince Joffrey – golden-haired heir, Sansa's betrothed; Arya calls him a liar and craven; fond of hunting; on the hunt.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; overheard Sansa and told her, touching her cheek, "life is not a song". Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers. Grand Maester Pycelle. Ser Ilyn Payne – mute King's Justice; makes Sansa shudder. Ser Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" – sixteen, beautiful, was refused the mission.
  • Lord Beric Dondarrion – about twenty-two, handsome lord of the forked-lightning banner; rode out at dawn leading the expedition against Clegane with Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar, under the king's stag, Stark direwolf and his own banner. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – accused of the riverlands massacres, sentenced to death; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's sworn shield. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled; massing at Casterly Rock. Tywin Lannister – his father. Renly, Stannis, Ser Barristan Selmy, Ser Arys Oakheart, Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Balon Swann, Barra.
  • Yoren – Night's Watch recruiter, crookbacked and smelly, who was given his pick of the king's dungeons. Two freeriders from the Dornish Marches pledged swords to the king.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully (defending Riverrun), Hoster Tully, Ser Raymun Darry, Ser Karyl Vance, Ser Marq Piper, Joss and other Sherrer survivors. Lysa Arryn, Robert Arryn, Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (at the Eyrie), Jon Arryn (dead Hand).
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – freed from the Eyrie, in the mountains of the Vale with Bronn, facing Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot) to whom he promised "the Vale of Arryn".
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly (fat, bookish recruit), Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder, Ser Alliser Thorne, Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear", Maester Aemon, Chett, Benjen (missing).
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife, owns a dragon egg. Viserys – her cruel brother. Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advisor. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister.

Places

Places
  • The Tower of the Hand – Ned's quarters in the Red Keep, with a solar. The Red Keep / King's Landing – the capital Sansa loves. The Iron Throne room, the kingswood, the Blackwater.
  • Winterfell – where the girls are to be sent, by sea rather than the kingsroad. Sherrer, Wendish Town, the Mummer's Ford, Riverrun, Casterly Rock, the Eyrie, the mountains of the Vale, Castle Black.
