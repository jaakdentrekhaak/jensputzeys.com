Chapter 13 – Tyrion

On the eighteenth night of the ride north, Tyrion reads about dragons by a stream, trades barbs with Jon Snow about the Night's Watch, is knocked down by Ghost, and shares wine with the boy before returning to camp.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – 14, Ned's bastard, riding north to join the Night's Watch; long, solemn, guarded "Stark face". Dismayed by the rough recruits, angered by Tyrion's mockery of the Watch but accepts the hard truth ("If that's what it is, that's what it is"); has the first watch. His albino direwolf Ghost (red eyes, silent, never howls) knocked Tyrion down on Jon's behalf.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, Night's Watch ranger leading the party; dislikes Lannisters and has made Tyrion's ride as hard as possible. Carries a cage of ravens from Maester Luwin.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, going south with the king as Hand (lent Tyrion library books). Catelyn – Ned's wife, at the sickbed of Bran (7; unconscious, legs broken). Robb (heir), Sansa (betrothed to Joffrey), Arya (secretly has the sword Needle), Rickon, with wolves Grey Wind, Lady, Nymeria, Shaggydog. Lyanna, Brandon, Rickard (Ned's dead sister, brother, father); Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin (maester; sent the ravens), Ser Rodrik (household knight), Jory Cassel (guard captain), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Septa Mordane, Mikken (smith), Hodor (simple stableboy), Old Nan, Chayle (septon-librarian), Alyn (Ned's servant), etc.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, who accuses the Lannisters of killing her husband Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); her son Robert Arryn is sickly Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Tyrion Lannister, "the Imp" – POV; dwarf with short twisted legs, uses a saddle of his own design; says his mind is his weapon and he needs books "as a sword needs a whetstone". Fascinated by dragons (once found the Targaryen dragon skulls in a King's Landing cellar; as a child imagined dragonfire burning his father or sister). Cruelly frank with Jon, then guilty; gracefully takes humiliation; accepted Benjen's old bearskin, enjoying that Lannisters "take what is offered". Rides north to see the Wall with two of his own men, including Morrec 🆕 new this chapter (cooks the stew). Slightly wrenched his back.
  • King Robert Baratheon – travelling south; hates the Targaryens, fears Daenerys's Dothraki marriage; has promised the Warden of the East to Jaime. Queen Cersei Lannister (Robert's wife, Tyrion's sister), Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" (her twin; Kingsguard, killed King Aerys), Prince Joffrey (crown prince; Tyrion's "repulsive nephew"), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (Joffrey's burned bodyguard), Tywin Lannister (Tyrion's father, Lord of Casterly Rock; was Hand of the King for twenty years), Maester Pycelle (royal maester), Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard), Stannis, Renly (Robert's brothers), Littlefinger (court figure), Varys (eunuch master of whisperers), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned; self-styled King of the Iron Islands).
  • Mad King Aerys II (dead, killed by Jaime), Rhaegar (dead, Aerys's son), Aegon the Conqueror 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – conquered the Seven Kingdoms with his sisters and three dragons (Balerion the Black Dread, Vhaghar, Meraxes) about three hundred years ago, beating King Loren of the Rock (Tyrion's ancestor, who yielded) and King Mern of the Reach (burned) at the Field of Fire.
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren 🆕 new this chapter – stooped, black-bearded, filthy, hard-as-roots black brother who joined the party at the wolfswood's edge with two sullen young recruits from the Fingers, "rapers". Benjen Stark (Ned's brother and the party's leader), Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown), Mormont (old commander), Maester Aemon. The Watch is portrayed by Tyrion as a dumping ground for thieves, poachers, bastards and rapists; no real danger from "grumkins and snarks" (he says).
  • Travelling party: eight people plus Ghost, twenty horses and the ravens.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, now wed to Khal Drogo, a huge Dothraki warlord; given three dragon eggs), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile, reportedly working for Varys), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, Drogo's bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • The kingsroad north of Winterfell – past flint hills that become mountains, into the wolfswood 🆕 new this chapter (old, dark forest of oak, evergreen and brier, echoing with wolf howls); farms thin out and then end. Party has ridden about eighteen days and camps by a stream near an abandoned holdfast.
  • The Wall / Castle Black – their destination; Benjen says there are no inns there.
  • Winterfell (library lent books on dragons), King's Landing (dragon skulls stored in a cellar), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), the Reach 🆕 new this chapter (southern plains where the Field of Fire was fought), Dragonstone (where the last dragon hatchlings were born), Summer Isles (source of Tyrion's amber wine), Fingers 🆕 new this chapter (where the recruits come from), Port of Ibben 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; hairy mammoths said to roam beyond it), Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dorne, Riverrun, the Eyrie / the Vale, Storm's End, The Trident, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands.
