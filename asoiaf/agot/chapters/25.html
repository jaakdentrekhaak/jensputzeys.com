Chapter 25 – Eddard

In sweltering King's Landing, Ned questions Grand Maester Pycelle about Jon Arryn's death, speaks with Arya about Bran's future, and is warned by Littlefinger that the Red Keep is full of spies while being offered a lead to Jon Arryn's former servants.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Hand of the King, in King's Landing; investigating Jon Arryn's death (he suggested poison to Pycelle). Distrusts Varys and Littlefinger, dislikes court intrigue; kept vigil in the castle godswood the night word came that Bran lived. Told Arya Bran can never be a knight. Asked Pycelle for the book he lent Jon; noted the queen was away when Jon died. Now has a lead to some of Jon's former servants.
  • Arya Stark – Ned's younger daughter, in King's Landing; practicing balancing on one toe as her "water dancer" teacher Syrio taught; asked if Bran will live with them and if he can still be a knight; says she will not marry a king ("that's Sansa").
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, in King's Landing, betrothed to Prince Joffrey; dreamed of Bran smiling.
  • Bran Stark – 8, crippled by his fall but alive; recovering at Winterfell. Robb (14, acting lord of Winterfell), Rickon (small youngest son), Catelyn (Ned's wife; was in King's Landing in secret, hidden by Littlefinger in a brothel, and sent home to Winterfell), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, a recruit at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing), Old Nan (ancient storyteller), Maester Luwin (Winterfell's maester; sent word of Bran), Brandon the Builder (legend). Direwolves: Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's), Ghost (Jon's); Lady (Sansa's) dead, Nymeria (Arya's) gone.
Winterfell household
  • Hodor (simple giant stableboy who carries Bran), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, Robb's companion), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Ser Rodrik Cassel (master-at-arms, went to King's Landing with Catelyn), Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Mikken (smith), Septon Chayle (keeps the library), Vayon Poole (Ned's steward), Hullen (master of horse), Harwin (guardsman), Fat Tom (guard, with Ned); Alyn – a man in Ned's service in King's Landing who announces visitors. Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Jon Arryn's widow, Catelyn's sister; fled to the Eyrie with her household and son; Pycelle calls her unstable since her last stillbirth, seeing enemies everywhere, and says she'll never forgive him for sending Maester Colemon away. Her letter accused the Lannisters of murdering her husband.
  • Jon Arryn (dead) – former Hand; fell ill the day after borrowing a book, died the next morning; last words (as told by Pycelle) a blessing for his son: "The seed is strong". Robert sat at his bedside.
  • Robert Arryn – his sickly young son, kept close by Lysa. Maester Colemon 🆕 new this chapter – Jon's young maester, treated him with purgatives; dismissed by Pycelle, went with Lysa. Ser Hugh of the Vale 🆕 new this chapter – Jon Arryn's squire, knighted by the king after Jon's death; still in King's Landing. Edmure (Catelyn's brother), Brynden (her uncle, Knight of the Gate), Hoster Tully (her father, Lord of Riverrun).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – ancient, slow-speaking Grand Maester (near forty years in office) with a jeweled chain; courteous but evasive; insists Jon Arryn died of a natural illness, dismisses poison as unlikely, and warns Ned against Varys ("Put not your trust in spiders"). Ned wonders whom he serves. Promised to send the book on great houses' lineages (by Grand Maester Malleon).
  • Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – Master of coin, small, clever, once close to Catelyn; offers help "because he promised Cat"; points out Varys's and the queen's watchers outside Ned's tower; says Ned should trust no one; claims four of Jon Arryn's servants remain in the city (a kitchen girl wed to a groom of Renly, a stablehand in the City Watch, a discharged potboy, and Ser Hugh). Ned thanks him but still doesn't trust him.
  • Varys "the Spider" – eunuch master of whisperers; born a slave in Lys (per Pycelle); has informers watching Ned; Ned's flesh crawls around him.
  • Ser Barristan Selmy – white-haired Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, tourney champion four years ago; Ned respects him deeply.
  • King Robert Baratheon – Ned's friend, fat, hard-drinking; sat with the dying Jon Arryn; Littlefinger says he tells the queen too much. Queen Cersei – Robert's wife, Lannister; was traveling to Casterly Rock with the children and Tywin when Jon died; has a spy watching Ned's door. Prince Joffrey (12, heir; tourney for his name day), Tommen, Myrcella (the younger royal children), Ser Jaime "the Kingslayer" (Cersei's twin, Kingsguard; lost the champion's crown), Tywin Lannister (their father, Warden of the West), Tyrion (the dwarf "Imp", Tywin's son; went south from Winterfell), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane (Joffrey's bodyguard; unhorsed by Selmy last year), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless king's justice), Renly and Stannis Baratheon (Robert's brothers; Stannis at Dragonstone), Balon Greyjoy (rebel Lord of the Iron Islands, defeated), Mad King Aerys II (Robert overthrew him). Syrio Forel (Braavosi sword master, Arya's teacher).
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren (black brother and recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms, hostile to Jon), Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter), recruits.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend), children of the forest, Mance Rayder, wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, traveling with his horde to Vaes Dothrak), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, Dany's adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.
