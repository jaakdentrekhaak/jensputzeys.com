Chapter 67 – Sansa

Grieving and half-starved in her tower room, Sansa is dragged out by Joffrey, beaten by Ser Meryn, made to watch the cruelty of his court, and then forced onto the battlements to look at her father's head on a spike.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Silver Lady" – exiled Targaryen princess, wife of Khal Drogo, at term with a son she calls Rhaego; in early labor after the fight at the tent. Promised Mirri Maz Duur freedom and accepted her price ("only death may pay for life", though not Dany's). Stopped the rape of Lhazareen captives and claimed them as slaves (including the timid girl Eroeh). Her three dragon's eggs showed no sign of life earlier.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord; vowed to conquer the Seven Kingdoms for his son. His chest wound festered; he fell from his horse, delirious with fever, and lies in a bath of horse blood while the spell is worked. All three bloodriders died in the revolt against the "maegi": Cohollo, Haggo, and Qotho (maimed by Jorah). Rival kos Jhaqo and Pono would fight over the khalasar; many Dothraki are leaving.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), Dany's adviser; fought Qotho and is badly wounded (half an ear gone, gashed hip). Handmaids Doreah, Irri, Jhiqui; khas warriors Aggo (archer), Rakharo, Jhogo; Quaro was killed by Qotho. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
  • Mirri Maz Duur – old Lhazareen godswife and healer, Dany's slave; studied in Asshai and with the maester Marwyn; sacrificed Drogo's red stallion and is chanting in the sealed tent where shadows dance. Lhazareen ("Lamb Men") – herding people now slaves in the khalasar.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, still betrothed to Joffrey and held in a tower room of Maegor's Holdfast; after her father's execution she wept and starved herself for days, tempted to leap from the window. Joffrey dragged her to court (Ser Meryn struck her bloody for telling Joffrey she hated him), forced her up on the gatehouse battlements to see Ned's tarred head on a spike, and had her struck again for joking that Robb might give her Joffrey's head. Has learned to say "as it please Your Grace" and hide her feelings; once, on the wall, she thought of shoving Joffrey over. Says she wrote letters as the queen dictated (earlier). Dislikes and distrusts her Lannister maids.
  • Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord of Winterfell, with direwolf Summer; Rickon – youngest, with direwolf Shaggydog. Winterfell has just had word of Ned's death.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, former King's Hand; confessed to treason and was beheaded by Ser Ilyn Payne on Joffrey's order (with Ice, Ned's own greatsword); his head, dipped in tar, is on a spike on the gatehouse wall. Joffrey says he was spared flaying only for Sansa's sake.
  • Maester Luwin (Winterfell's grey, skeptical maester, bitten by Shaggydog), Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan), Osha (wildling servant in ankle shackles), Hodor (huge simple stableman), Old Nan, Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (captain of guard, with Robb), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb). Dead: Septa Mordane (Sansa's septa, executed as a traitor; her rotting head is on the wall), Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Heward, Wyl, Tomard, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf).
  • Arya Stark – younger daughter, seized by Yoren in King's Landing after Ned's death (Sansa does not know). Catelyn Stark – mother, with Robb's host. Robb Stark "the Young Wolf" – eldest son, 15; his defeat of Jaime has Joffrey vowing to raise a host and bring Sansa Robb's head as a name-day gift. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard son at Castle Black. Jeyne Poole, Syrio Forel – Sansa's friend and Arya's dancing master, not seen here.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort; his foot was beaten by Tywin on the kingsroad, but he escaped. Lord Hornwood died there; Lord Cerwyn, Ser Wylis Manderly (fat son of Lord Wyman, White Harbor), Harrion Karstark and four Freys were captured.
  • At the ambush: the Greatjon (Lord Umber), Maege Mormont (Lady Mormont), Rickard Karstark (lost two sons), Galbart Glover, Lord Jason Mallister; Ser Helman Tallhart holds the Twins. Robb's guard: Theon Greyjoy (eager, bloodthirsty), Patrek Mallister, Smalljon Umber, Robin Flint, Ser Wendel Manderly, Dacey Mormont (Maege's eldest, heir to Bear Island, wields a morningstar), Olyvar Frey. Dead: Torrhen and Eddard Karstark (Lord Karstark's sons) and Daryn Hornwood, killed by Jaime. Howland Reed (mentioned) – Ned's friend at Greywater Watch.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey, "Lord of the Crossing" – ninety, wizened, gouty, sharp-tongued, with an eighth wife of sixteen and scores of descendants; Hoster Tully's bannerman who holds the only bridge over the Green Fork; resentful of Tullys and Arryns. Sold Robb the crossing and his swords (four hundred kept home). A Frey son is married to Tywin's sister.
  • Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, past sixty; Ser Jared, Ser Hosteen, Ser Danwell (sons), Ronel Rivers (bastard) led the Frey levies; Ser Perwyn (youngest envoy); Olyvar Frey – Robb's new squire; Elmar Frey – youngest son, pledged to Arya; two boys both named Walder (eight and seven), wards for Winterfell. Four Freys were captured fighting for Robb on the kingsroad. Sigil: twin towers, blue on grey.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, vain, petulant and cruel; insists on "Your Grace"; says he gave Ned a clean death only because he was Sansa's father. At court he had a thief's hand chopped off, ordered a land dispute settled by duel to death, jailed a woman who wished to bury an executed traitor, and made a tavern singer who mocked Robert choose between fingers and tongue. His name day approaches; he plans to raise a host afterward and kill Robb himself, and keeps two empty spikes for his uncles Stannis and Renly. Told Sansa he'll behead her and find a cleverer wife if she bears a stupid child.
  • Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – sits on the small council; told Joffrey a king should not strike his wife himself, insists the betrothal stands, wept at Jaime's defeat and wants Joffrey to stay in King's Landing in case his uncles attack. Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Small council: Lord Tywin (Hand, with the army), Lord Janos Slynt (frog-faced, commander of the gold cloaks, nods to every sentence; threw Ned down for the headsman), Grand Maester Pycelle (examined Sansa and gave her a sleeping draught), Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin), Lord Varys (eunuch spymaster).
  • Kingsguard: Ser Meryn Trant – dour, rusty-haired, in white armor with a sunburst crest; beats Sansa on Joffrey's orders without feeling. Ser Arys Oakheart – Kingsguard, with Meryn. Ser Ilyn Payne – the King's Justice, silent executioner, wields Ice; chopped the thief's hand. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – burned-faced, Joffrey's guard dog; tells Sansa to "give him what he wants" and gently dabs her bloody lip. Ser Jaime Lannister – Lord Commander, captive of Robb's host. Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed, wanted for treason. Lord Renly fled; Lord Stannis at Dragonstone.
House Lannister
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's golden twin, Lord Commander of the Kingsguard by decree; captured by Robb at the ambush near Riverrun and held in irons (Lord Karstark wants his head). Captured with him: about a hundred knights and a dozen lords (Lord Westerling, Lord Banefort, Ser Garth Greenfield, Lord Estren, Ser Tytos Brax, Mallor the Dornishman) and three of Tywin's nephews.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Warden of the West, Hand of the King; won the kingsroad battle but missed Robb. Ser Kevan Lannister – his brother. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – huge knight who burned the Pipers and Brackens. Ser Addam Marbrand – cavalry commander. Lord Lefford, Lydden, Serrett, Ser Flement Brax, Crakehalls, Swyfts – bannermen.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf brother of Cersei, with Tywin's army; hurt his right elbow in battle. Bronn – sellsword bodyguard; Shae – young camp follower sharing his tent; Podrick Payne – squire; clansmen Shagga, Timett, Chella, Gunthor (Conn killed; Ulf fallen).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, captive of the Lannisters. Lord Hoster Tully – their father at Riverrun, sick. Lord Blackwood and survivors are besieged inside Riverrun. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, scouted for Robb (Knight of the Gate) and drew the map for the ambush. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance raid Tywin's rear; Beric Dondarrion and Thoros (red priest) harass Lannister foragers; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Yoren – old, ragged Night's Watch recruiter (greasy black hair, tangled beard, rotten teeth, patched black cloak) who once visited Ned; found Arya in the crowd, called her "boy," hauled her away, returned her sword Needle, and at the chapter's end holds her by the hair with a knife.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward to the Lord Commander at Castle Black; owns the Valyrian sword Longclaw; direwolf Ghost. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander. Maester Aemon – blind maester, born Aemon Targaryen. Samwell Tarly – fat, timid friend of Jon. Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder, Pate, Rudge, Matt – Jon's brothers; Donal Noye – armorer; Hobb – cook; Clydas – aide to Aemon; Bowen Marsh – Lord Steward; Ser Endrew Tarth – new master-at-arms; Dywen, Hake – rangers. Ser Alliser Thorne – sent south with a wight's severed hand for Joffrey. Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing. Dead: Othor, Jafer Flowers (wights, destroyed) and Ser Jaremy Rykker.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, wights, children of the forest (small, dark Dawn Age folk with obsidian weapons and greenseers; Luwin says they are gone, Osha says they and the giants live north of the Wall), First Men, Andals, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child (per Varys, killed by the Lannisters); Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa, executed as a traitor (see Stark household). Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places

Places
  • Maegor's Holdfast – moated inner fortress of the Red Keep, where Sansa's tower room is. The Red Keep – throne room with the Iron Throne and a balcony, the practice yard, and the gatehouse battlements with a wall of heads on iron spikes (Ned's, Septa Mordane's, older ones), seventy or eighty feet above the bailey, overlooking the city.
  • King's Landing – the Great Sept of Baelor (on Visenya's Hill, where Ned died), the Street of the Sisters, the Dragonpit (fire-blackened ruin), the Gate of the Gods, the fish market and docks, the Blackwater Rush, Flea Bottom.
  • Winterfell (crypts, Maester Luwin's turret), Riverrun (besieged), the Twins, Harrenhal, the Neck, White Harbor, the Eyrie, Casterly Rock, Dragonstone, Castle Black, the Wall, the southern trail, Vaes Dothrak, Pentos, Asshai, Bear Island.
