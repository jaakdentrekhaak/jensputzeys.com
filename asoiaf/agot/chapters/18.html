Chapter 18 – Catelyn

Catelyn and Ser Rodrik reach King's Landing by galley and are secretly summoned to the Red Keep by Littlefinger and Varys, who identify her Valyrian-steel dagger as having belonged to Tyrion Lannister.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark (Lady Stark, née Tully) – POV; arrived by sea in King's Landing after a storm-tossed voyage; her fingers are badly maimed from the dagger (two on her left hand can't bend), the pain a "scourge". Staying incognito, avoiding the castle until summoned by Littlefinger, whom she trusts only a little and Varys not at all. Tells them nothing of Bran or her purpose ("a wife may yearn for her husband"), but after Varys asked to see the dagger she handed it over and learned from Littlefinger that it was his, won by Tyrion Lannister. Family words: "Family, Duty, Honor."
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; still with the royal party to the north; Catelyn expects he has not reached the city. Killed Sansa's wolf Lady himself with his sword Ice.
  • Arya Stark – 9; wolf Nymeria escaped; does not yet know Mycah is dead. Sansa Stark – lost her wolf Lady (killed by Ned) after refusing to back Arya's account.
  • Bran Stark – 7; woke from his coma in ch17 (cannot feel his legs); direwolf Summer; Catelyn does not know he has woken. Robb Stark – 14, acting master of Winterfell. Rickon (3, youngest), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, gone to the Wall).
  • Brandon Stark (Ned's dead elder brother, once Catelyn's betrothed; Petyr challenged him over her and Brandon spared him, leaving a scar), Lyanna (Ned's dead sister), Rickard (Ned's dead father), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – master-at-arms at Winterfell, loyal and stubborn; seasick on the voyage, shaved off his famous white whiskers (now looks ten years older and unrecognizable); went to the castle to find Ser Aron Santagar (the king's master-at-arms, vain but honest) about the dagger, and waits with him in the inn's common room.
  • At Winterfell: Maester Luwin (maester), Hodor (simple stableboy), Mikken (smith), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard), Old Nan. With Ned: Jory Cassel (guard captain), Vayon Poole (steward), Septa Mordane, Hullen (master of horse). Mycah – Arya's friend, the butcher's boy, killed by the Hound.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); Petyr says he was always her friend. Robert Arryn – her sickly son. Edmure – Catelyn's brother (gave Petyr the nickname Littlefinger). Brynden – her uncle. Hoster Tully – her father, Lord of Riverrun, once Petyr's guardian (Tully words: Family, Duty, Honor).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Petyr Baelish, "Littlefinger" – Lord Baelish, master of coin on the small council, not yet thirty; small, slender, silver-flecked dark hair, pointed beard, grey-green eyes, sly and charming; wears a silver mockingbird cloak pin (his seal is a mockingbird). Was Hoster Tully's ward, grew up with Catelyn and loved her, challenged Brandon Stark at fifteen and was wounded; Catelyn burned his later letter. Now has her brought to the castle and offers help. Says the dagger was his, lost to Tyrion in a bet on Ser Jaime at the tourney on Prince Joffrey's name day when Loras Tyrell unhorsed Jaime.
  • Lord Varys, "the Spider" / "the Whisperer" – eunuch master of whisperers on the council; plump, powdered, hairless, lilac-scented, soft-voiced, giggling; has "little birds" everywhere and knew of her arrival, Rodrik's movements, and even of the dagger. Sweet to Catelyn, offers a Free City healer for Bran.
  • Maester Pycelle – royal maester on the small council, in the city. Stannis Baratheon – Robert's elder brother, gone to Dragonstone. Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard Lord Commander) and Renly Baratheon (Robert's youngest brother) – rode north to meet the king. Loras Tyrell 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – knight who unhorsed Jaime in the tourney on Joffrey's name day, costing Littlefinger and others their bets.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – named as winner of the dagger in the wager; currently at the Wall with Benjen and Jon.
  • King Robert Baratheon, Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Prince Joffrey (crown prince), Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" (Kingsguard, the queen's twin), Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (Joffrey's burned bodyguard), Ser Ilyn Payne (mute King's Justice), Ser Raymun Darry (host to the royal party, castle south of the Trident), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Tywin Lannister (Lannister head, mentioned), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned; self-styled King of the Iron Islands), Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar (dead). The royal party is in the Riverlands, heading south.
  • City Watch (gold cloaks, black ringmail) escorted Catelyn to the castle.
  • Captain Moreo Turnitis 🆕 new this chapter – Tyroshi trader (forked green beard) who carried them on his galley Storm Dancer; paid; apparently sold information of their arrival. Ser Aron Santagar 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – the king's master-at-arms.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother) and Yoren (black brother) – with Jon heading north; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • King's Landing – capital on three hills at the mouth of the Blackwater Rush, built where Aegon the Conqueror landed; the Red Keep (pale red stone, seven drum-towers) now flies Baratheon stag banners; Great Sept of Baelor on Visenya's hill; the Dragonpit (ruined, doors closed a century); Street of the Sisters; busy harbor. Catelyn lodged at a rambling inn on Eel Alley (hostess not interested in names) before being taken to a tower room in the Red Keep.
  • The Bite (bay), Dragonstone (storm passed off it; Stannis there), the Fingers / Three Sisters, White Harbor (where they took ship), Tyrosh (Moreo's home).
  • The Trident – where Robert killed Rhaegar.
  • The Neck, Winterfell (Stark seat), White Knife, The Wall / Castle Black, Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End, Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak.
