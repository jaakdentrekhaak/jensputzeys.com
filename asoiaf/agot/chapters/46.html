Chapter 46 – Daenerys

In Vaes Dothrak, Daenerys eats a raw stallion's heart and names her unborn son Rhaego, then at the feast her brother Viserys draws a sword on her and Khal Drogo executes him.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" / Dany – exiled Targaryen princess, wife of Khal Drogo, pregnant. Ate a wild stallion's raw heart whole at the dosh khaleen ceremony, was hailed as the mother of a prince, and named her son Rhaego after her dead brother Rhaegar. Learning Dothraki (her handmaids translate) and teaching Drogo Common Tongue words. Says she has no one left now, yet watched Viserys die calmly: "fire cannot kill a dragon". Keeps the three dragon eggs Illyrio gave her as a bride gift.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her older brother, the self-styled king in exile ("the dragon"), proud, cruel, drunk and desperate for the army and crown Drogo had promised; planned to steal the eggs (Jorah threatened to cut off his hand). He drew a sword in the sacred city, where blades and bloodshed are forbidden, and threatened Dany and her unborn child. Drogo had his bloodriders hold him down and poured molten gold over his head as a "crown" for the "Cart King" / "Sorefoot King"; Viserys is dead.
  • Khal Drogo – Dany's husband, huge Dothraki warlord with a braid hung with gold bells, "sun-and-stars"; leads a khalasar; mocked Viserys and ordered his death. His bloodriders Qotho (seized Viserys), Haggo (shattered his wrist) and Cohollo (helped Dany onto her horse). Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo – Dany's Dothraki guards/companions. Khal Jommo 🆕 new this chapter (four wives) and Khal Ogo 🆕 new this chapter (with his son, the khalakka Fogo 🆕 new this chapter) – other khals in Vaes Dothrak, who sit at honor beside Drogo and laughed at Viserys.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight, Dany's sworn sword and advisor; told her of Viserys's plan, tried to stop him at the feast, and defends her. Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – Dany's handmaids and language teachers. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who arranged the match, gave Dany the eggs and lent Viserys the sword. The dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses, widowed khaleesis who rule from Vaes Dothrak; their one-eyed eldest prophesied Dany's son as "the stallion who mounts the world", a khal of khals who will unite the Dothraki and ride over the earth.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; leg healing in the Tower of the Hand. Has confirmed from Cersei that her three children are Jaime's, not Robert's; warned her to flee before the king returns from hunting so he can tell Robert the truth. Arranged for Sansa and Arya to sail home on the Wind Witch from Braavos.
  • Sansa Stark – elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey; Arya Stark – younger daughter, wild; both to be sent home. Tomard "Fat Tom" – loyal, portly guardsman now commanding Ned's guard; Varly – guardsman; Alyn (riding with Lord Beric), Harwin – Stark guards; Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl – dead guards; Septa Mordane, Jeyne Poole, Vayon Poole (Winterfell's steward).
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon, Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (missing); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; dead Lyanna and Brandon. Ser Rodrik Cassel with Catelyn. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan. Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master teaching Arya.
Royal court
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – lover of her twin Jaime and mother of his three children; hates Robert; Ned exposed her secret and told her to leave. King Robert Baratheon – hunting a monstrous boar; unaware. Prince Joffrey – heir (really Jaime's son), Sansa's betrothed.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; reports sellswords gathering at Casterly Rock. Grand Maester Pycelle – Cersei's creature. Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers. Ser Barristan Selmy – old, rigid Lord Commander of the Kingsguard. Lord Beric Dondarrion – riding against Gregor Clegane with Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – accused of massacres, sentenced to death; Sandor "the Hound" – Joffrey's shield, his brother. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled to his father. Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock. Ser Loras Tyrell, Ser Ilyn Payne, Renly, Stannis, Barra (Robert's baby bastard). Yoren – Night's Watch recruiter. Jon Arryn – late Hand, believed by Ned to have died for the secret.
Riverlands / Vale / Lannister / Night's Watch
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother defending Riverrun; Hoster Tully – their father; Lysa Arryn and Robert Arryn – Catelyn's sister and her sickly son at the Eyrie; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the Stone Crows clan (Gunthor, Shagga).
  • Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly (fat, bookish recruit), Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder, Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms), Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Chett – Night's Watch at Castle Black; Benjen – missing First Ranger.

Places

The Womb of the World 🆕 new this chapter – sacred lake where the first man is said to have risen on the first horse; Dany washed there. Khal Drogo's hall 🆕 new this chapter – roofless feasting hall with huge firepits. The Western Market – bazaar where traders and sellswords gather.
