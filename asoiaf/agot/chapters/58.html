Chapter 58 – Eddard

Imprisoned in a lightless dungeon, feverish and starving, Ned is visited by a disguised Varys, who tells him he is as good as dead unless he confesses and denies his son and Stannis, and threatens Sansa.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi" – exiled Targaryen princess, pregnant wife of Khal Drogo; two days after a poisoning attempt she left Vaes Dothrak with his khalasar, riding south and west; expects a son she calls Rhaego; put three dragon's eggs in a brazier with no visible result.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord; vowed after the attempt to cross the sea and take the Seven Kingdoms for his son. Bloodriders Cohollo (oldest), Qotho (cruel), Haggo (huge, silent).
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island, Dany's adviser, stopped the poisoner. Handmaids Doreah (Lysene), Irri, Jhiqui; young Dothraki guards Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo, Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, formerly King's Hand; in a pitch-black, windowless cell beneath the Red Keep with a plastered broken leg that is feverish and swelling, given only water and no food. Blames himself (for trusting Cersei, for mercy, for not telling Robert). Takes comfort in the hope that Robert's brothers, Catelyn and the northern and river lords will rise. Varys offers him his life — a confession of treason, commanding Robb to lay down his sword, denouncing Stannis and Renly, and taking the black — and threatens Sansa's life; Ned refuses to give his word, but is stricken at the threat. Cersei is to visit tomorrow. Varys tells him Cersei gave Robert's squire Lancel the wineskins for the king's last hunt, and says Ned's mercy in warning Cersei is what doomed Robert.
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, still betrothed to Joffrey and kept close by Cersei; pleaded at court for her father's mercy a few days ago. Varys uses her as a threat against Ned.
  • Arya Stark – younger daughter; per Varys, escaped Ser Meryn and fled, and neither Varys nor the Lannisters can find her. Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord in Winterfell. Rickon – youngest son, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall; Ned thinks of him with shame and longing.
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son; leads about eighteen thousand northmen down the Neck from Moat Cailin, called a traitor in Joffrey's decrees. Varys calls him 'a boy with an army'. Wolf Grey Wind. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; went to her father at Riverrun; Ned wonders where she is. She let her captive Tyrion go; Varys expects him dead in the Mountains of the Moon.
  • Ned's household guard: Alyn and Harwin (Hullen's son), sent after Ser Gregor and hoped to return. Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan of Winterfell), Maester Luwin (the castle's maester), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb), Hodor (simple, huge stableboy who says only "Hodor"), Osha (captured wildling woman, kept in irons), Old Nan (storyteller), Mikken (blacksmith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard). Dead: Jory Cassel (Ned's guard captain), Hullen (master of horse), Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn (Stark guardsmen); Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Other wolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog (Rickon).
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton (pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort, flayed-man sigil), the Greatjon (Lord Umber) (giant, loud lord), Rickard Karstark (sunburst), Galbart and Robett Glover (red fist), Ser Helman Tallhart (three sentinel trees), Lord Hornwood, Maege Mormont (Lady Mormont, black bear), Lord Cerwyn – northern lords with Robb. Manderlys of White Harbor: Ser Wylis and Ser Wendel (fat sons of Lord Wyman) with about 1,500 men. Howland Reed (mentioned) – little crannogman, Ned's old comrade at Greywater Watch.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, son of Cersei; held his first court session, wearing a crown of rubies and black diamonds; cruel and petulant (called Barristan's defiance "boy" and ordered him seized). Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – now on the small council, in effect rules; calls Sansa "daughter-to-be". Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Decrees (ch57): Tywin Lannister named Hand of the King in place of the "traitor" Ned; Cersei seated in place of "traitor" Stannis; Janos Slynt (frog-faced, balding City Watch commander, a commoner) raised to lord, given Harrenhal, a bloody-spear sigil and a council seat (nobles resent him; two ugly sons). Grand Maester Pycelle reads the decrees; Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers (spymaster); disguised as a turnkey to visit Ned, he claims to serve only 'the realm' and its peace, admits he stood by while the queen's men slaughtered Ned's guard, will not free him, and says Stannis (the 'true' heir, just and merciless) is Cersei's greatest fear; Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin, mocks Barristan.
  • Kingsguard: Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed as Lord Commander by Cersei and Joffrey (for letting Robert die; "too old"), after serving three kings since his twenty-third year; threw down cloak, armor and sword in contempt at Jaime's appointment and left; Joffrey ordered him seized by Slynt's gold cloaks. Ser Jaime Lannister named the new Lord Commander. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – made a Kingsguard in his place (refusing knight's vows). Ser Meryn Trant (Kingsguard knight Arya escaped from), Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart – the remaining Kingsguard in the hall.
  • Lords at court: Jalabhar Xho (black-skinned exile prince from the Summer Isles), Ser Aron Santagar (gloomy), the Redwyne twins Horror and Slobber, sickly Lord Gyles (feigns coughing), drunken Ser Dontos (funny; tries to greet Sansa), Ser Balon Swann; only about twenty attend, where a hundred once did, and they shun Sansa. Summoned to swear fealty or be named traitors: Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone, King Robert's brothers), Ser Loras Tyrell, Lord Mace Tyrell, Doran Martell (Prince of Dorne), the Starks, Lysa Arryn, the Tullys, Mallister, Blackwood, Frey, Vance, Bracken, Whent, Caron, Royce, Beric and Thoros.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Tyrion's and Cersei's father, now Hand of the King; Lord of Casterly Rock and Warden of the West, bald with golden side-whiskers; cold, never smiles. Commands about twenty thousand men at the crossroads inn on the Green Fork; has driven the Tully lords from the field, taken Harrenhal, and plans to march against Robb, then deal with Stannis. Reads Ned's capture as ending the Stark threat.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, freed from the Eyrie's cells (Catelyn's captive until then), now at his father's camp with about three hundred hill clansmen he bought with promises of silk, steel and horses; they will ride with Tywin only if Tyrion goes with them, his life forfeit until they hold their steel.
  • Ser Kevan Lannister – Tywin's brother and chief lieutenant (portly, favors patience). Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tyrion's brother and Cersei's twin; now Lord Commander of the Kingsguard by decree; beat the Tullys under Riverrun, captured Ser Edmure and besieges the castle. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – Lannister knight who burned out the Pipers and Brackens. Ser Addam – Lannister commander told to harass Robb's flanks. Ser Flement Brax – knight who greeted Tyrion. Bannermen in camp: Presters, Crakehall, Marbrand, Lydden.
  • Lancel Lannister – Robert's squire, Ser Kevan's elder son and Cersei's cousin; per Varys, Cersei had him give Robert the wineskins for his last hunt. Bronn – sellsword who follows Tyrion. Brorm – rides with Tyrion's party.
  • The hill clans (Mountains of the Moon): Shagga son of Dolf (huge Stone Crow), Conn son of Coratt and Gunthor son of Gurn (Stone Crows), Chella daughter of Cheyk (Black Ears woman, scout), Ulf son of Umar (speaks for the Moon Brothers), Timett son of Timett (one-eyed Burned Man, young war chief); also Torrek, Jaggot.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, captive of the Lannisters. Lord Hoster Tully – their father, at besieged Riverrun, possibly dying. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, with Catelyn. Lord Blackwood – led survivors back to Riverrun; Lady Whent – yielded Harrenhal after Raventree fell. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance – young riverlords raiding Tywin's rear. Jason Mallister – holds Seagard. Walder Frey – gathering levies at the Twins (Tywin says he fights only when victory is certain). Beric Dondarrion – young lord harassing Lannister foragers, with Thoros, a red priest of Myr who sets his sword aflame. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned at the Mummer's Ford.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander; confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a dead man in Mormont's solar. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander. Samwell Tarly – fat, bookish recruit, Jon's friend. Maester Aemon – blind old maester. Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh master-at-arms. Ser Jaremy Rykker – ranger who led sweeps for Benjen. Bowen Marsh – round Lord Steward. Donal Noye – one-armed armorer. Pyp, Grenn, Toad – Jon's fellow recruits. Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers), wights (animated dead), children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings, giants.
Past figures (Ned's memories and Varys's talk)
  • Robert Baratheon (dead; Ned's dream of him), Rhaegar Targaryen – the late crown prince, who won the Harrenhal tourney and gave the queen of beauty's crown of winter roses to Lyanna rather than his wife, the Dornish princess Elia Martell; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister, who whispered 'Promise me' from a bed of blood; Brandon Stark – Ned's dead brother; Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Kingsguard of King Aerys; Rhaenys – Rhaegar's young daughter, with a black kitten named Balerion; Varys says the Lannisters broke down her door; the Martells still mourn Elia and her children.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places
  • The dungeons of the Red Keep 🆕 new this chapter – lightless stone cell far below the castle (red stone, nitre-stained, a four-inch iron-studded door) where Ned is held; no window, bed or slop bucket.
  • The Red Keep throne room – stripped of Robert's hunting tapestries; scene of Joffrey's first court. Harrenhal – ruined great castle, which Lady Whent yielded to Tywin; Joffrey's decree grants it to Lord Slynt. The crossroads inn – Tywin's headquarters on the Green Fork. The Mountains of the Moon – home of the hill clans; the Vale of Arryn.
  • Riverrun – Tully seat, under siege; Raventree; Seagard; the Twins; the Golden Tooth; the Red Fork, the Green Fork, the Trident; Moat Cailin – ruined stronghold at the Neck where Robb mustered; White Harbor; Casterly Rock – Lannister seat.
  • Vaes Dothrak, the Free Cities, the Seven Kingdoms, King's Landing (Red Keep), the Iron Throne, Winterfell, the Eyrie, Dragonstone, Castle Black, the Wall, Bear Island.
