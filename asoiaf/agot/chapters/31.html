Chapter 31 – Tyrion

Days after his capture at the inn, Tyrion is hauled by Catelyn's party up the high road into the mountains, argues his innocence about the dagger, and fights beside his captors when hill clansmen attack.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; has taken Tyrion prisoner on suspicion of sending the assassin who attacked Bran, using the dagger as proof (her hands bear its scars). Tricked pursuers by announcing Winterfell, but actually leads the party up the eastern high road toward the Eyrie and her sister Lysa. Gaunt, windburnt and relentless; insisted on burying the dead. Rejected Tyrion's claim that Littlefinger lied, though a flicker of doubt showed; insists Littlefinger only loved her as a boy; killed a clansman to save Tyrion. Returned his axe and dirk ("I trust you no more than before").
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – Winterfell's master-at-arms, now shorn of whiskers; wounded in the arm and neck but alive; Catelyn's chief support.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark (Catelyn's husband, Hand of the King in King's Landing), Sansa and Arya (their daughters there), Bran (crippled by a fall, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord), Rickon (youngest), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing), with their direwolves (Lady dead, Nymeria gone). Jory Cassel (captain of Ned's guard), Syrio Forel (Arya's sword teacher), Septa Mordane (the girls' septa) are in King's Landing.
Catelyn's party and captives
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – POV; Lord Tywin's dwarf son, brother of the queen and of Jaime; taken prisoner at the crossroads inn; bound and hooded at first, now unbound, riding hard and aching. Cunning, bitter; keeps a mental list of those who mistreated him to repay ("a Lannister always pays his debts"). Swears he did not send the dagger, says it was not his and a fool would not arm a footpad with his own blade; says Littlefinger lied, noting he claimed to have bedded Catelyn. Fought his first battle with an axe, hacked down a rider and a clansman and helped save Catelyn. His mare was killed and eaten. Believes his father may not bother to rescue him. Claims "I never bet against my family."
  • Jyck – Tyrion's man-at-arms; killed in the fight (a bold bareback charge). Morrec – Tyrion's groom/servant, good with a bow; alive.
  • Bronn 🆕 new this chapter – sellsword (bone-thin, black hair/eyes, cynical); sided with Catelyn at the inn, fought fiercely, now stays loyal to the strongest side; suggests Tyrion needs a woman. Chiggen 🆕 new this chapter – squat sellsword, skinned the horse, looted the dead.
  • Ser Willis Wode 🆕 new this chapter – Lady Whent's hedge knight from Harrenhal, stiff-necked; first to rise for Catelyn at the inn; leads the way with a spiked morningstar. Lharys, Mohor, Kurleket 🆕 new this chapter – Lord Jonos Bracken's men-at-arms (Kurleket, a pig-faced brute, wanted to cut Tyrion's tongue; Kurleket and Mohor died in the fight). Marillion – young singer who insists on riding along to make a song; cowardly; had four fingers broken by Tyrion's heel (accident of the fight) plus broken ribs and harp; has a shadowskin cloak taken from the clansmen.
  • Yoren (Night's Watch recruiter; stayed out of the quarrel), Masha Heddle (the innkeeper who begged them not to fight there), Lord Walder Frey (very old river lord; his Freys at the inn mostly sat down again), Lord Jonos Bracken (river lord, whose men joined Catelyn), Lady Whent (of Harrenhal, whose knight joined).
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn, in the Eyrie; Tyrion knew her slightly at court and expects no friendship. Jon Arryn (dead former Hand), Robert Arryn (Lysa's sickly son), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Lord of Riverrun), Edmure (her brother), Brynden (her uncle).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Lord Tywin Lannister (Tyrion's father, Warden of the West; Casterly Rock; cares little for Tyrion but tolerates no slight on his House), Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" (Tyrion's brother, Kingsguard), Queen Cersei (their sister), King Robert (her husband), Joffrey (12, heir), Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish (Master of coin; told Catelyn the dagger was his, lost to Tyrion in a tourney wager; Tyrion says he lies and boasts of Catelyn), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Loras Tyrell (the "Knight of Flowers"), Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor Clegane (brothers, knights), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Pycelle (Grand Maester), Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard) remain at King's Landing.
The Night's Watch
  • Yoren (see above), Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly, Jeor Mormont, Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne, Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast, Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce, Gared (executed).
Hill clans of the Mountains of the Moon
  • The mountain clans 🆕 new this chapter – lean, poorly armed brigands who attack the party at dawn with cheap or rusted weapons; the leader in a shadowskin cloak was killed by an arrow. Lharys guesses Milk Snakes or Moon Brothers. Nine clansmen died; one rider fled.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.
