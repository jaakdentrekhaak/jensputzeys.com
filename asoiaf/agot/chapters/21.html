Chapter 21 – Tyrion

On his last night at Castle Black, Tyrion dines with the Night's Watch officers, listens to Lord Mormont's warnings and plea for men, then climbs the Wall and shares a farewell with Jon Snow.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, Night's Watch recruit at Castle Black; drawn night guard on the Wall (arranged by Ser Alliser's allies to exhaust him), carrying a spear and a warhorn. Helps Pyp and Grenn train. Sends messages home via Tyrion: joking to Robb, Rickon can have his things, "help" for Bran. Shakes Tyrion's hand and calls him "friend". Vows that if Benjen does not return he and Ghost will go find him. Direwolf Ghost (white, now chest-high to Tyrion).
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Hand of the King; Mormont says he sent Gared's head from Winterfell. Catelyn (his wife), Robb (14, heir, at Winterfell), Bran (7, crippled), Rickon (3, can't read yet), Arya and Sansa (with Ned in King's Landing), Benjen Stark (Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing). Brandon, Lyanna, Rickard (Ned's dead brother, sister, father), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel (household knight), Maester Luwin (maester), Hodor (simple stableboy), Mikken (smith), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard), Old Nan (storyteller); with Ned: Vayon Poole (steward), Jory Cassel (guard captain), Septa Mordane, Hullen. Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); Robert Arryn – her sickly son; Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • At King's Landing (not seen here): Petyr Baelish, "Littlefinger" (master of coin), Lord Varys (eunuch master of whisperers), Maester Pycelle (Grand Maester), Renly Baratheon and Stannis Baratheon (Robert's brothers; Stannis at Dragonstone), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard Lord Commander), King Robert Baratheon, Queen Cersei, Prince Joffrey, Tommen, Myrcella, Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" (Tyrion's twin-of-Cersei brother; Tyrion jokes Jaime will wonder where he is), Sandor "the Hound", Ser Ilyn Payne (King's Justice), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned), Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar (dead).
  • Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – Tyrion's father, the most powerful lord in the realm; fond of spiked heads; gave Thorne and Rykker the choice of the black or the spikes after taking King's Landing.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's youngest son; wine-loving and sharp-witted; about to leave Castle Black for Winterfell and then King's Landing, with three Watch escorts, his men Jyck and Morrec, and Yoren riding south too. Humiliated Thorne with a crab fork at dinner. Promised Mormont to tell the king, his father and Jaime about the Watch's need, though privately expects no one to care. Says he knows what it is to love a brother; touched by Jon. Left uneasy by the haunted forest. Owner of the dragonbone-hilted dagger that Ned and Catelyn suspect was meant for Bran; Tyrion has not heard the suspicion.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jeor Mormont ("the Old Bear") – Lord Commander of the Night's Watch, nearly 70 in two years, strong as a bear; talking raven ("Fool!", "Duel!", "Corn"). Grim and weary; says the Watch is under a thousand men (600 at Castle Black, 200 at Shadow Tower, fewer at Eastwatch, a third of them fighters), about three men per mile of the 100-league Wall. Warns that summer (nine years long) is ending, tells of white walkers glimpsed on the shore by Eastwatch fisherfolk and mountain people fleeing south past the Shadow Tower, and begs Tyrion to carry word of the darkness coming. His son dishonored himself, and his sister Maege 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) rules Bear Island. Refused to let Jon go south ("the young ones need to forget").
  • Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; humorless, scornful; a knight who fought for the Mad King at King's Landing and was given the choice to take the black. Targeted by Tyrion's mockery.
  • Ser Jaremy Rykker 🆕 new this chapter – Night's Watch officer, also fought on the losing side at King's Landing and took the black under Tywin's ultimatum; dry humor.
  • Bowen Marsh – Lord Steward of the Watch, round and red-faced.
  • Maester Aemon – ancient, blind (milk-white eyes), around 100; called Tyrion "a giant"; has had letters from the Citadel agreeing summer is ending.
  • Donal Noye (one-armed armorer), Cotter Pyke (commands Eastwatch), Denys Mallister 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; commands the Shadow Tower; writes of the mountain people moving south).
  • Recruits (Jon's training mates): Grenn (big, held his own against Halder), Pyp (Pypar) – small boy with large ears who asked Jon for help, Halder, Todder (Toad), Jeren, Dareon.
  • Benjen Stark – First Ranger, missing; Mormont sent him after Ser Waymar Royce (son of Yohn Royce 🆕 new this chapter, lost on his first ranging with two good men; Castle Black has no word of him). Gared – one of Royce's two men, believed a deserter; Ned sent Mormont his head from Winterfell. The other man (Will) is unaccounted for. Yoren – black brother, riding south again.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others / "white walkers" (reported by Mormont; fisherfolk near Eastwatch have glimpsed them), Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings and "mountain people" fleeing south.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • Castle Black – Night's Watch keep; the Commander's Keep, King's Tower (Tyrion's quarters), an iron cage lift beside the well running up the Wall, and a wooden stair up the south face. Mole's Town 🆕 new this chapter – small village half a league south along the kingsroad.
  • The Wall – ~100 leagues long; top wider than the kingsroad, gravel spread against melting; catapults, cranes, watch posts. North of it a half-mile cleared strip, then the haunted forest.
  • Eastwatch (by the Sea) (sends crabs), the Shadow Tower. Bear Island 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned, Mormont's house seat). The Citadel 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; maesters' seat; letters to Aemon). Lannisport 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; Lannister port town, merlings joke).
  • Winterfell (Stark seat; Tyrion's next stop), King's Landing (capital; Tywin once spiked heads on the King's Gate), Red Keep, Moat Cailin, White Harbor, Dragonstone, Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), the Trident, the Eyrie, Riverrun, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak.
