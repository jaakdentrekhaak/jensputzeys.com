Chapter 36 – Daenerys

The khalasar reaches the sacred city of Vaes Dothrak, where Daenerys's gifts to Viserys end in a fight and she strikes him in the face.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell; resigned as Hand but still in King's Landing. Ambushed by Jaime Lannister's men after visiting Chataya's brothel (where Robert's bastard Barra was born); leg broken, being treated by Pycelle in the Red Keep. Told Jaime that Tyrion was seized on his order. Ser Jorah Mormont hates him (took everything he loved over poachers).
  • Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard; killed in Jaime's ambush.
  • Heward and Wyl – young Stark men-at-arms; both killed in the ambush (Heward speared, Wyl dragged off his horse).
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; holding Tyrion prisoner at the Eyrie and, as Ned warned Jaime, a threat to Tyrion's life if Ned dies. Sansa and Arya (his daughters, in the Red Keep), Bran (crippled, at Winterfell), Robb (acting lord), Rickon (youngest), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves as before. Lyanna – Ned's late sister, once betrothed to Robert; remembered warning Ned that Robert would never keep to one bed (died; Ned made her promises as she lay dying).
Catelyn's party and captives
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – prisoner of Catelyn, last seen at the Eyrie; his brother Jaime is hunting him. Bronn (sellsword), Marillion (singer), Ser Rodrik Cassel (Winterfell's master-at-arms, wounded) as before.
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Lysa Arryn (widow of Jon Arryn, Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister), Robert Arryn (her sickly young son), Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle), Nestor Royce (High Steward of the Vale), Ser Donnel Waynwood (young knight), Mya Stone (mule guide), Maester Colemon (Eyrie maester), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Riverrun), Edmure (her brother); Jon Arryn (dead Hand; Ned is convinced he was murdered and, after Littlefinger's talk of Robert's bastards, wonders whether it was over the king's baseborn children, though Littlefinger scoffs at that).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin and Tyrion's brother, Kingsguard knight; led about twenty Lannister guards in the rain seeking Tyrion; ambushed Ned and had his men killed after Ned warned Catelyn would kill Tyrion; his gilded sword is the one that killed Aerys.
  • Tregar – Jaime's Lannister guard captain; his helm was split by Ned's sword.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of coin, glib, mocking; led Ned to the brothel; fled when Jaime told him to leave, returned with the City Watch (gold cloaks). Told Ned of Robert's several bastards, including the acknowledged boy at Storm's End and rumored babies at Casterly Rock (rumor only).
  • Barra – baby girl, King Robert's bastard, with dark hair like his, born at Chataya's brothel; her red-haired young mother (unnamed, freckled, hoping Robert will return) asked Ned to carry word to Robert; Ned promised Barra "shall not go wanting."
  • Chataya – tall, elegant black-skinned woman who runs the brothel to which Littlefinger led Ned.
  • King Robert (lecherous; Ned thinks he has trained himself to look away from unpleasant things), Queen Cersei (his Lannister wife), Joffrey (12, heir), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father; "quite vexed" about Tyrion per Jaime), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly (king's youngest brother), Stannis (king's middle brother, wed to Selyse Florent; has not answered Ned's request to return from Dragonstone to the council), Grand Maester Pycelle (old maester, tended Ned), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard lord commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) — at court, as before. Rhaegar Targaryen – Dany and Viserys's dead elder brother, killed at the Trident; his host of about forty thousand broke when he fell. Aerys – their late father, the king whose throat the Kingslayer opened; Dany knows Ser Barristan Selmy as the Kingsguard knight who went over to Robert ("the Usurper").
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Syrio Forel (Arya's Braavosi dancing master), Vayon Poole (steward), Hodor (simple giant stableboy), Maester Luwin, Old Nan, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Mikken (smith), and others, as before.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, new brother), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – young exiled princess, wife of Khal Drogo and pregnant with his son (whom she believes is "the true dragon"); more confident now, rides a silver horse; dreams of home. Gave Viserys new Dothraki clothes as a peace offering; when he threatened and grabbed her she hit him in the face with a bronze medallion belt and threatened that Drogo would gut him. Sleeps with a green-and-bronze dragon egg.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her brother, self-styled king ("the dragon"); proud, cruel, ignorant of Dothraki customs; mocked by the Dothraki as the "Sorefoot King" and "Cart King" after walking in the grass and riding in a cart (she hid the mockery from him). Filthy, impatient for the ten thousand warriors Drogo promised; abused handmaid Doreah; now has a cut cheek and swears she will "rue this day."
  • Khal Drogo – Dany's husband, huge Dothraki warlord with a khalasar of forty thousand mounted warriors; has just brought it to Vaes Dothrak and gone up the Mother of Mountains for the night to sacrifice for his safe return.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight, Dany's adviser; thinks the Dothraki could break a western army in open battle but not take castles; scornful of Viserys; bitterly hates Ned Stark, who "took from me all I loved" over poachers and his honor.
  • Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho – Drogo's three bloodriders (sworn "blood of my blood"): Cohollo oldest, bald, broken-toothed, kindly to Dany; Haggo huge and silent; Qotho cruel, hurts her handmaids. Aggo and Rakharo – warriors of Dany's khas (her escort).
  • Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – Dany's handmaids (Irri and Jhiqui Dothraki; Doreah a pale Lysene girl, taught her pillow-arts, once bedded by Viserys). Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who arranged the match, back in Pentos.
  • Dosh khaleen – crones of Vaes Dothrak who will present Dany and prophesy over her child.

Places

Places
  • Vaes Dothrak – sacred city of the Dothraki, no walls, vast and mostly empty, built by slaves in assorted styles; entered by the Horse Gate (two giant bronze stallions) and a godsway lined with statues plundered from conquered cities; no blades or blood of free men may be shed there. Has the Western Market (Free City traders) and Eastern Market (caravans from Yi Ti, Asshai); Drogo's wooden hall with silk roof and earthen hill-houses sit near the Mother of Mountains, which only men may climb.
  • Chataya's brothel / King's Landing streets – scene of Ned's ambush by Jaime; Ned lies wounded in the Red Keep.
  • The Eyrie / The Vale of Arryn / the Gates of the Moon / the Bloody Gate – Lysa's mountain seat and surrounding valley, where Catelyn and Tyrion now are.
  • Storm's End – Baratheon seat where Robert's acknowledged bastard boy was sent. Casterly Rock – Lannister seat. Riverrun, Winterfell, the Wall, Highgarden, Dragonstone, Pentos, Seagard, Harrenhal.
