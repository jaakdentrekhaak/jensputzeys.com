Chapter 40 – Catelyn

At the Eyrie, Bronn kills Ser Vardis Egen in the trial by combat, so Tyrion is declared innocent and Lysa is forced to free him, while Catelyn learns the Lannisters are massing at Casterly Rock and decides to leave for Winterfell with her uncle.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell and Hand of the King, bedridden in the Tower of the Hand with a broken leg; Robert forced the Hand's badge back on him after he demanded justice against Jaime. Haunted by a dream of an old fight at a tower in Dorne where his friends died and Lyanna, his sister, lay dying.
  • Alyn – Ned's man in King's Landing, acting captain of his guard; Jory Cassel – dead former captain; Vayon Poole – Winterfell's steward, with Ned.
  • Sansa (elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey) and Arya (younger daughter) – with Ned in the Red Keep; Arya has not spoken since the attack. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; at the Eyrie, still holding Tyrion as "her prisoner"; wants to leave for Winterfell and her sons, taking ship from Gulltown; vividly remembers Petyr Baelish's duel with Brandon (her first betrothed, Ned's older brother) at Riverrun. Robb (fifteen, ruling Winterfell), Bran (eight, crippled), Rickon (youngest, small), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves Summer (Bran's) and Grey Wind (Robb's). Lyanna – Ned's dead sister, once betrothed to Robert. Brandon Stark – Ned's dead elder brother; Catelyn's first betrothed; fought Petyr (Littlefinger) in a duel at Riverrun over her, urged him to yield, then felled him with a deep wound.
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – Winterfell's master-at-arms, Catelyn's old knight; recovered from his wounds (whiskers regrown white); queasy about ships; commands her escort.
  • Old friends from Ned's past (dream): Howland Reed (crannogman survivor), Martyn Cassel, Theo Wull, Ethan Glover, Ser Mark Ryswell, Lord Dustin.
  • Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Maester Luwin, Osha (captive wildling woman), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Hodor (simple giant), Old Nan, Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Mikken (smith), Joseth (stableman).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, tired king; struck Cersei, wants peace with the Lannisters; insists Ned stay as Hand.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – proud, cold; bruised by Robert; Lysa's letter had named her as Jon Arryn's killer.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled King's Landing; now reported massing a host at Casterly Rock. Tywin Lannister – his father, lord of Casterly Rock; has not answered Edmure's demand to state his intent.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – see below. Ser Meryn Trant (Kingsguard), Grand Maester Pycelle, Joffrey (12, heir), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) – at court. Barra – Robert's baby bastard. Rhaegar and Aerys – dead Targaryens; Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent, Ser Gerold Hightower – Aerys's Kingsguard, killed in Ned's dream-fight.
  • Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – now Master of coin in the capital; as a boy fostered at Riverrun, loved Catelyn, challenged Brandon Stark, nearly died, was sent home to the Fingers.
Catelyn's party and Tyrion (the Eyrie)
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Cersei and Jaime's brother; accused by Lysa of poisoning Jon Arryn (he denies it). Claimed trial by combat; Bronn won, so he is declared innocent and freed: Lysa orders him escorted to the Bloody Gate with horses, supplies and weapons returned, enough to reach the Trident; the high road is dangerous. Quoted the Arryn words "As High as Honor."
  • Bronn – sellsword, Tyrion's champion; lightly armored, fast; wore Ser Vardis down, killed him; lip smashed, a tooth broken.
  • Marillion (singer, with Catelyn), Ser Willis Wode (Harrenhal hedge knight).
House Tully / House Arryn / the Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; moody, inconstant, indulges her son; believes Tyrion murdered her husband; courted by many suitors; furious but bound to free Tyrion.
  • Robert Arryn – sickly, childish Lord of the Eyrie and Defender of the Vale, about whom Catelyn thinks needs taking from his mother; giggles at puppets, wanted to see Tyrion "fly".
  • Ser Vardis Egen – dead; captain of Lysa's guard, her champion, fought with Jon Arryn's silver sword (broken).
  • Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate; quit his post in anger after Lysa refused him men; leaving with Catelyn to defend Riverrun.
  • Lysa's suitors: Lord Eon Hunter (old, gouty), Ser Lyn Corbray (handsome, reckless, poor), Ser Morton Waynwood; other Vale lords: Lord Nestor Royce, Ser Albar Royce, Lady Waynwood, Ser Donnel Waynwood. Maester Colemon (anxious, says Jon Arryn planned to foster Robert at Dragonstone; Catelyn says Casterly Rock). Mord (brutal gaoler of the sky cells), Mya Stone (mule guide).
  • Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father, lord of Riverrun; no word of him in Edmure's message, Catelyn fears he is ill. Edmure Tully – her brother, commanding Riverrun's defense; sent Lords Vance and Piper to guard the pass below the Golden Tooth. Jon Arryn – dead Hand, Lysa's husband. Alyssa Arryn – legendary woman whose endless tears form Alyssa's Tears.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit at Castle Black), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (ancient, blind), Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, 14, Drogo's pregnant wife at Vaes Dothrak, owns dragon eggs; struck Viserys. Viserys Targaryen – her cruel brother, "the dragon," impatient for an army. Khal Drogo – her husband, Dothraki warlord with forty thousand riders. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her (son of Jeor Mormont). Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho – Drogo's bloodriders; Aggo, Rakharo – her khas; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – her handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister); dosh khaleen (crone-prophetesses of Vaes Dothrak).
