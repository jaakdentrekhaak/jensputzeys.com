Chapter 72 – Daenerys

Dany builds Khal Drogo's funeral pyre, binds Mirri Maz Duur to it, and walks into the flames, emerging unharmed with three newly hatched dragons as her remaining followers pledge themselves to her.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Stormborn" – last of House Targaryen since her brother Viserys's death, widow of Khal Drogo (whom she smothered after he was left blind and mindless). Calls herself daughter, bride and mother of dragons. Built Drogo's pyre, set her three dragon eggs on it, lit it, and walked into the fire; came out unburnt (hair burned off, clothes turned to ash) with three living dragons. Freed the slaves among her followers, named three of her khas her bloodriders (ko), and swore to Jorah she would not die with Drogo. Dothraki, handmaids and Jorah knelt to her.
  • Her dragons 🆕 new this chapter – newborn, hatched from the eggs in the pyre: cream-and-gold (suckling her left breast), green-and-bronze (right breast), and black-and-scarlet (draped on her shoulders, red eyes, smoke from its nostrils). A red comet appeared in the east as the pyre was lit; she took it for a sign.
  • Khal Drogo – dead; cremated in his painted vest with braided hair hung with bells, head toward the Mother of Mountains, his treasures, a slain stallion and the eggs; his hair had never been cut. His khalasar of forty thousand deserted earlier, with Pono and Jhaqo (now khals) taking most.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), now sworn "first of her Queensguard"; begged her not to burn with Drogo and offered to take her east (Yi Ti, Qarth, the Jade Sea, Asshai); urged selling the eggs; reluctantly bound Mirri to the pyre at her order. Knelt when he found her in the ashes. Dany promises him one day a Valyrian steel longsword.
  • Jhogo, Aggo, Rakharo – young warriors of her khas, named her ko: Jhogo took a silver-handled whip, Aggo a dragonbone bow, Rakharo a gold-chased arakh (all three at first demurred, then knelt crying "Blood of my blood"). Irri, Jhiqui (Dothraki) and Doreah (Lysene) – her handmaids who bathe and perfume her. Fewer than a hundred followers remain: freed slaves, women, children and the old.
  • Mirri Maz Duur – Lhazareen godswife and healer, called "maegi" ("wise"); bound, whipped by Jhogo for speaking, tied to the pyre and burned alive at Dany's order. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark "the Young Wolf", "the King in the North" – fifteen-year-old eldest son, leader of the northern host (wolf Grey Wind); returned in triumph to Riverrun after breaking the siege. At the war council he refused to accept Joffrey or Renly as king ("Renly can't be king before Lord Stannis"), would give Lannisters "no peace" but his sword, and was hailed by the Greatjon, Karstark, Lady Mormont and the river lords as the King in the North, the first such cry in three hundred years. Prayed in the godswood beforehand. Betrothal pledge: to wed a daughter of Walder Frey after the war.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's widow, back at Riverrun for the first time since carrying infant Robb north; mourns Ned, furious she was not told of her father's illness, and pleaded at the council for peace to win back her daughters (trade the Lannister prisoners for Sansa and Arya) and take Robb home; she was overruled. Carries guilt over seizing Tyrion.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – beheaded on Joffrey's orders; his death is the cause of the northern rage. Sansa Stark – elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey, held in the Red Keep; Arya Stark – younger daughter, taken away by Yoren (Catelyn believes the queen holds both girls). Bran Stark (crippled, wolf Summer) and Rickon (wolf Shaggydog) at Winterfell; Jon Snow at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Maester Luwin, Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan), Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen and Theon Greyjoy (with Robb; boastful retelling of the Whispering Wood). Dead: Septa Mordane, Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Heward, Wyl, Tomard, Varly, Cayn; Lady.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • The Greatjon (Lord Umber) – huge, loud lord with an immense greatsword; first to kneel and name Robb King in the North ("it was the dragons we married, and the dragons are all dead"). Rickard Karstark – gaunt Lord of Karhold, grieving two sons dead in the Whispering Wood (his eldest son Harrion was captured on the Green Fork); wants vengeance and the Kingslayer's head; hails Robb. Maege Mormont (Lady Mormont) – "King of Winter," thinks Stannis has the better claim; Galbart Glover; Theon Greyjoy; Ser Helman Tallhart (holds the Twins).
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort; has re-formed the battered remnants of the other host at the mouth of the causeway (the Neck). Lord Hornwood died on the kingsroad; Cerwyn, Ser Wylis Manderly and four Freys were captured there. Dead: Torrhen and Eddard Karstark, Daryn Hornwood.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey – ninety, gouty, sharp-tongued Lord of the Crossing; still holds the Twins with Ser Helman Tallhart's northern garrison; Robb is pledged to wed one of his daughters. Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, "weaselly smile," represents Frey at the council and urges waiting for the two kings to fight and offering Tywin a truce and ransom; the lords shouted him down. Other sons: Ser Jared, Ser Hosteen, Ser Danwell, Ser Perwyn; Olyvar Frey – Robb's squire; Elmar Frey – pledged to Arya.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king, vain and cruel; had Ned beheaded; talks of leading the City Watch against Renly himself and of killing Robb. Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – commands Tywin to bring the army to defend the Red Keep "in the name of the king and council." Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead.
  • Small council: Lord Janos Slynt (frog-faced City Watch commander, made a lord with Harrenhal; bloody-spear sigil), Grand Maester Pycelle, Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin), Lord Varys (eunuch; hears whispers about Stannis).
  • Kingsguard: Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Arys Oakheart, Ser Ilyn Payne (King's Justice), Sandor "the Hound" Clegane; Ser Jaime Lannister (Lord Commander, captive). Ser Barristan Selmy – "Barristan the Bold," dismissed and wanted for treason; Tywin thinks Joffrey a fool to lose him.
House Baratheon / Tyrell (claimants)
  • Renly Baratheon – late King Robert's younger brother, wed Margaery Tyrell at Highgarden and crowned himself; backed by the Tyrells, Highgarden and Storm's End (Marq Piper claims Dorne and five of seven great houses will follow). Robb declines to recognize him: Stannis is elder. Stannis Baratheon – elder brother, on Dragonstone; Lady Mormont says his claim is better. Joffrey is still, in Robb's view, the lawful heir of Robert; he says he means Joffrey to die, and then Tommen.
House Lannister
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Lord of Casterly Rock, Hand of the King; his army has crossed the Trident and is marching on Harrenhal, burning the riverlands with Gregor Clegane, Vargo Hoat and Amory Lorch; has sent Tyrion to King's Landing to rule in his stead. Calls Ned's killing "madness."
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Tywin's dwarf son, injured elbow; ordered to King's Landing to rule at court and curb Joffrey (refused to leave his clansmen or Shae behind). Shae – his young camp follower; Bronn – sellsword; Podrick Payne – squire; clansmen Shagga, Timett.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tywin's son and Cersei's twin, Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; held in irons at Riverrun after the Whispering Wood; Riverrun's lords want to keep him (Karstark: "we must not give up the Kingslayer") while Frey urges ransom. Three Lannister nephews and other lords were captured with him; Catelyn counts "four Lannisters" as bargaining chips for her daughters. His siege army was destroyed.
  • Ser Kevan Lannister – Tywin's brother, steady, married to a daughter of Ser Harys Swyft. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – huge vassal burning the riverlands. Ser Addam Marbrand – cavalry captain screening the march.
  • Lord Lefford (stores; suggests ransom, mentions the queen holds the Hand's daughters), Ser Harys Swyft (chinless, craven, wails for peace and slanders Jaime), Ser Flement Brax (silver-and-purple; his father Lord Brax drowned crossing a river in armor), Ser Forley Prester (commanded the south camp; retreated in order with two thousand spears and as many bowmen), Lord Serrett, a Crakehall messenger (boar sigil), Vargo Hoat and Ser Amory Lorch (raider captains).
  • Cersei Lannister – queen regent in King's Landing, orders the army to defend the Red Keep; has not told Joffrey about Renly.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lord Hoster Tully – Catelyn's father, Lord of Riverrun, once big and now shrunken, white-haired, bedridden and dying in agony ("the crabs in my belly"; Maester Vyman gives him dreamwine and milk of the poppy); hid his illness from his enemies; watched Robb's night attack from the gatehouse; asks after his estranged daughter Lysa and his brother Brynden, whom he nagged to marry.
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, stocky, auburn-haired and fiery-bearded, freed from the Lannister camp by Blackwood's sortie, now presiding at his father's high seat; neck bandaged; vows vengeance for Ned. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, Hoster's brother, Knight of the Gate, back from the Vale.
  • Lord Tytos Blackwood – hard, hook-nosed lord in jet-inlaid yellow armor and a raven-feather cloak; led the Riverrun sortie; urges marching on Harrenhal; feuds with Lord Jonos Bracken (blustering, glowering lord whose seat Stone Hedge was burned by Gregor Clegane; urges swearing to Renly). Lord Karyl Vance (his father died beneath the Golden Tooth) and Ser Marq Piper (young, hot-tempered; wants to strike Casterly Rock and join Renly); a young Darry (Ser Raymun's son, about Bran's age); Lord Jason Mallister (advises patience at Riverrun). Beric Dondarrion and Thoros (red priest) – harass Lannister foragers. Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, at the Eyrie with her son Robert Arryn, too frightened to come.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward (and squire) to the Lord Commander; burned hand now unwrapped. Rode south at night on a mare with Ghost to avenge his father and join Robb, leaving Longclaw on his bed and knocking Sam aside; his friends caught him and brought him back. Calls himself "bastard and oathbreaker." Mormont talked him round: Jon swore he is the Old Bear's man and will not run again, though privately he was tempted to try again. Direwolf Ghost gave him away. Has just been told to put his sword back on.
  • Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander at Castle Black; had Jon watched and expected both flight and return (Maester Aemon predicted it). Drinks beer with lemon, eats boiled eggs; his talking raven repeats words. His sister Maege Mormont is probably in Robb's host with her daughters; his son Jorah fled in disgrace. Says the walking dead matter more than who sits the Iron Throne; reports of vast elk and mammoth herds fleeing south, huge footprints near Eastwatch, abandoned villages and great fires in the mountains. Means to lead the Watch in force beyond the Wall to find Benjen, "alive or dead," and face the King-beyond-the-Wall and the Others, with Jon and Ghost.
  • Samwell Tarly – fat, gentle friend of Jon; tried to stop him, then woke the others. Pyp, Grenn "the Aurochs", Toad, Halder, Matthar – Jon's fellow recruits who surrounded him, recited the Night's Watch vow and brought him back. Maester Aemon – old blind maester, born Aemon Targaryen, predicted Jon would go. Hobb ("Three-Finger Hobb") – cook. Donal Noye – armorer; Ser Alliser Thorne – sent south with a wight's hand; Yoren – recruiter now at King's Landing with Arya.
  • Cotter Pyke – commander at Eastwatch; Ser Denys – at the Shadow Tower; Quorin Halfhand – ranger who took a captive swearing Mance Rayder is massing all his people in a new secret stronghold. Benjen Stark – Jon's uncle, First Ranger, missing along with other rangers. Dead: Ser Jaremy Rykker, Othor, Jafer Flowers.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others, wights (dead men who walk; fire destroys them), children of the forest, giants, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall, said to be gathering all the wildlings), wildlings.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child (per Varys, killed by the Lannisters); Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa, executed as a traitor (see Stark household). Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.

Places
  • The pyre site 🆕 new this chapter – red, dead, parched land with little good wood where Dany's remnant khalasar built Drogo's pyre. Vaes Dothrak – Dothraki holy city beneath the Mother of Mountains, where khals' widows join the dosh khaleen (the crones); Rakharo will take her only that far. The night lands – where dead Dothraki riders go, burned beneath the open sky to become stars. Yi Ti, Qarth, the Jade Sea, Asshai (by the Shadow), Pentos and the Free Cities – places Jorah names.
  • Riverrun – Tully castle where Robb was hailed King in the North; Harrenhal; the Twins; the Whispering Wood; Stone Hedge; Highgarden; Storm's End; Casterly Rock; Dragonstone; King's Landing (Red Keep); Winterfell; the Eyrie; Castle Black; the Wall; Bear Island; the Seven Kingdoms.
