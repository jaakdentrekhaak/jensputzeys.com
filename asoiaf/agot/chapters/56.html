Chapter 56 – Tyrion

Tyrion leads his hill-clan followers into his father's camp at the crossroads inn, learns how the war in the riverlands has gone, and finds his clansmen agreeing to ride with Lord Tywin against the Starks, with Tyrion as their guarantee.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Khal Drogo's pregnant wife (son to be Rhaego); survived a poisoning attempt, put her three dragon eggs in a brazier with no visible result, and left Vaes Dothrak with Drogo's khalasar, riding south and west. Khal Drogo – her Dothraki warlord husband; vowed to cross the sea and take the Seven Kingdoms for his son; bloodriders Cohollo, Qotho, Haggo. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island, her adviser; handmaids Doreah, Irri, Jhiqui; guards Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo, Quaro. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark – fifteen-year-old eldest son, has called the banners and leads about eighteen thousand northmen from Moat Cailin; per Tywin's scouts the Stark host is now marching down the causeway. His wolf is Grey Wind. Tywin dismisses him as an untempered child.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; reached Robb at Moat Cailin, steadied him, and said she would go to Riverrun, where her father Hoster may be dying (by Tywin's account, Riverrun is now besieged).
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; held as a traitor in a dungeon under the Red Keep, per Tywin. Sansa Stark – confined in King's Landing. Arya Stark – younger daughter, whereabouts unknown. Bran Stark – crippled, left as lord in Winterfell. Rickon – youngest, with direwolf Shaggydog. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan, coming north from White Harbor), Maester Luwin, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, with Robb), Hodor, Osha (captured wildling), Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen. Dead: Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Direwolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton (pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort), the Greatjon (Umber), Rickard Karstark, Galbart and Robett Glover, Ser Helman Tallhart, Lord Hornwood, Maege Mormont, Cerwyn – northern lords with Robb. Manderlys of White Harbor: Ser Wylis and Ser Wendel (fat sons of Lord Wyman) with about 1,500 men. Howland Reed (mentioned) – Ned's friend at Greywater Watch.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead, per Tywin. Joffrey – his reputed heir, now reigning in King's Landing; Queen Cersei Lannister – Tyrion's sister, holds Ned and Sansa, effectively ruling. Myrcella, Tommen – her younger children. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin; Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; Grand Maester Pycelle; Janos Slynt – City Watch commander; Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart – Kingsguard; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's shield; Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon – Robert's brothers; Stannis, at Dragonstone, is the next foe Tywin intends to deal with.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp", "the halfman" – Cersei's dwarf brother, Tywin's youngest son; freed from the Eyrie's cells (Catelyn's captive until then), now back in the riverlands at the head of about three hundred hill clansmen (more being raised) whom he bought with promises of silk, steel and horses, and who treat him as part commander, part captive. Needles his father; lost his men Morrec and Jyck; wants three thousand helms and hauberks and arms for the clans and has his own plan to ravage the Vale. Tywin offered him a command against Marq Piper, Karyl Vance and Beric Dondarrion; the clans now agree to ride with Tywin only if Tyrion goes with them, his life forfeit until they hold his promised steel.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Tyrion's father, Lord of Casterly Rock and Warden of the West, in his middle fifties, bald with great golden side-whiskers and green eyes flecked with gold; cold, never smiles. Commands about twenty thousand men, camped at the crossroads inn on the Green Fork (hanged the innkeep). Has driven the Tully lords from the field, burned holdfasts and taken Harrenhal; plans to march against Robb and lure him south, then deal with Stannis. Reads Ned's capture as ending the Stark threat.
  • Ser Kevan Lannister – Tywin's only surviving brother and Tyrion's uncle, portly and balding with a yellow beard, his chief lieutenant; favors waiting behind pits and spikes. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Tyrion's brother; routed Vance and Piper at the Golden Tooth, beat the Tullys under Riverrun's walls, captured Ser Edmure and now besieges the castle. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – Lannister knight, Sandor's brother, who burnt out the Pipers and Brackens. Ser Addam 🆕 new this chapter – Lannister commander ahead (harassing Robb's host), ordered to harass Robb's flanks and fall back. Ser Flement Brax 🆕 new this chapter – knight in silver-and-amethyst armor with a unicorn on his shield, who first greets Tyrion.
  • Lannister bannermen in camp 🆕 new this chapter: Presters (red ox), Crakehall (boar), Marbrand (burning tree), Lydden (badger).
  • Bronn – sellsword who follows Tyrion, "of no particular allegiance". Brorm 🆕 new this chapter – one of Tyrion's men, who rides with him.
  • The hill clans (Mountains of the Moon): Shagga son of Dolf (huge Stone Crow, wants to chop off manhoods and feed them to goats; broke the guard captain's sword); Conn son of Coratt (Stone Crow); Gunthor son of Gurn (Stone Crow, raising more clans); Chella daughter of Cheyk 🆕 new this chapter (small hard Black Ears woman, scout; her sons took fifty ears); Ulf son of Umar 🆕 new this chapter (speaks for the Moon Brothers); Timett son of Timett 🆕 new this chapter (one-eyed Burned Man, a "red hand" or war chief, under twenty, feared by other clans). Also named earlier: Torrek, Jaggot.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, said to be a captive of the Lannisters after the fighting below Riverrun. Hoster Tully – their father at Riverrun, possibly dying. Lord Blackwood – led survivors back to Riverrun, now besieged by Jaime; Raventree 🆕 new this chapter (a Blackwood seat) fell; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal to the Lannisters. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance – (Robb had heard Vance was slain, but Tywin names both) raiding Tywin's rear across the Red Fork. Jason Mallister – lord who holds Seagard. Walder Frey – gathering levies at the Twins (Tywin says he fights only when victory is certain). Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, with Catelyn. Lord Beric Dondarrion – young lord (sent by Ned against Gregor Clegane) now harassing Lannister foragers, with Thoros of Myr, a fat priest who sets his sword aflame; Tywin calls them Ned Stark's afterthoughts. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned at the Mummer's Ford.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander; confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a dead man in Mormont's solar. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly; Maester Aemon; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Donal Noye – armorer; Pyp, Grenn, Toad; Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, missing First Ranger.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master; Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend; Septa Mordane; Captain Qos.

Places

Places

The Mountains of the Moon – home of the hill clans (Stone Crows, Moon Brothers, Black Ears, Burned Men); the Vale of Arryn.
  • Riverrun – Tully seat, under siege; Harrenhal; Raventree; Seagard; the Twins; the Golden Tooth; the Red Fork, the Green Fork, the Trident; Moat Cailin – ruined stronghold at the Neck where Robb mustered; White Harbor; Casterly Rock – Lannister seat.
  • Vaes Dothrak, King's Landing (Red Keep), Winterfell, the Eyrie, Dragonstone, Castle Black / the Wall.
