Chapter 54 – Daenerys

While Khal Drogo hunts, Dany visits Vaes Dothrak's Western Market, where Ser Jorah stops a wine merchant from poisoning her, and afterward Drogo swears to cross the sea and conquer the Seven Kingdoms for their unborn son.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Daenerys Stormborn" – exiled Targaryen princess, Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak, expecting a son she calls Rhaego; calls Robert "the Usurper". Wants Drogo to lead his people west to the Seven Kingdoms, which she knows only from her late brother's tales (she remembers a big house with a red door). Nearly poisoned; afterward put her three dragon's eggs into a lit brazier on a sudden impulse, but nothing visibly happened. Rides a silver horse (Drogo's bride gift).
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord whose hair has never been cut; "the stallion who mounts the world"; like the Dothraki, loathes the sea and had refused to hear of ships, but after the attempt vowed before the Mother of Mountains to take his khalasar west, ride "wooden horses" across the water, give his son the Iron Throne and kill, enslave and plunder the Seven Kingdoms. Returned from hunting the great white lion (hrakkar), scratched on the leg; rewarded Jhogo and Jorah with horses; two days later his khalasar left Vaes Dothrak, riding south and west with Dany beside him. His bloodriders include Cohollo (carried the lion carcass), Qotho, Haggo.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island, Dany's adviser and protector; noted for hairy chest and strength; once read Illyrio's letter he suspected poison, forced the wineseller to taste, shielded her from the thrown cask. Told her to be patient and that they will go home; keeps his errands with the caravan captain private.
  • Her handmaids: Doreah (caught her when she fell; got a fertility charm), Irri, Jhiqui. Her khas (young Dothraki guards): Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo (whip-wielder, tripped the wineseller), Quaro.
  • Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings; his letter to Viserys reported that Robert offers lands and lordships for the death of Dany and her child. Viserys – her late brother, killed by Drogo ("crowned" in a joke by Irri). Rhaego – her unborn son. The dosh khaleen – crones of Vaes Dothrak. Khals Jommo and Ogo.
  • Wineseller 🆕 new this chapter – slim blond Lysene-styled merchant with a caravan; tried to poison Dany with a Redwyne-marked cask and fled; now chained naked to her horse's halter, running behind the khalasar. Byan Votyris 🆕 new this chapter – Norvoshi merchant captain with a blue mustache, from Pentos by way of Norvos and Qohor; apologized and gifted the wineseller's goods. A wizened Pentoshi sausage-seller (horsemeat, since her pigs died) also appears in the market.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell and the late King's Hand; held as a traitor in King's Landing. Robb Stark – his eldest son, marching south with the northern host to Riverrun. Bran Stark – crippled, left as lord of Winterfell. Rickon – youngest son, with direwolf Shaggydog. Sansa Stark – confined in King's Landing, wrote letters at the queen's urging. Arya Stark – younger daughter, fled in the Red Keep after Syrio's last stand; whereabouts unknown. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, in the south. Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, at the Wall.
  • Winterfell household: Maester Luwin, Hodor (simple giant stableboy), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, riding with Robb), Hallis Mollen, Osha (captive wildling), Old Nan, Mikken, Ser Rodrik Cassel. Dead: Jory Cassel, Hullen, Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf), Lyanna, Brandon. Direwolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind (Robb), Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog. Northern bannermen (Karstark and sons, the Greatjon Umber, Roose Bolton, Robett Glover, Maege Mormont, Cerwyn, Hornwood) have joined Robb's army.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead; had offered lands for Dany's death. Joffrey – boy king, son of Cersei. Queen Cersei Lannister – holds Ned and Sansa. Myrcella, Tommen – her younger children. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin who betrayed Ned; Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; Grand Maester Pycelle; Janos Slynt – City Watch commander; Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" and Lord Tywin Lannister – Cersei's brother and father; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart – Kingsguard knights; Sandor Clegane "the Hound"; Lord Renly Baratheon and Lord Stannis Baratheon – Robert's brothers (Stannis at Dragonstone).
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, at the Eyrie with her son Robert Arryn; Hoster Tully and Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's father and brother at Riverrun; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – her uncle.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, freed by Catelyn, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the hill clan Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, steward to the Lord Commander; confined after attacking Ser Alliser Thorne and fighting a dead man in Mormont's solar. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly; Maester Aemon; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Ser Jaremy Rykker; Bowen Marsh; Donal Noye – armorer; Pyp, Grenn, Toad; Benjen Stark – missing First Ranger.

Other

Places

Places
  • Vaes Dothrak – Dothraki sacred city; Dany's home is a hollow earth hummock; stolen monuments of conquered peoples; the Mother of Mountains, the Womb of the World. The Western Market – square for caravans from the Free Cities and the west (smells of garlic, pepper, Lys perfumes); the Eastern Market – stranger goods and peoples (Asshai, Yi Ti, Qarth).
  • The Dothraki sea – the grass plains. The black salt sea / poison water – the sea the Dothraki fear. The Jade Sea – Drogo's alternative target to the east. Pentos, Tyrosh, Lys, Myr, Norvos, Qohor – Free Cities. The Arbor – wine island (Redwyne sigil). The Seven Kingdoms / Westeros, King's Landing, the Iron Throne, Winterfell, Riverrun, the Eyrie, Dragonstone, Castle Black, the Wall, Bear Island (Ser Jorah's lost home).
