Chapter 65 – Arya

Arya, hiding and scavenging in Flea Bottom, finds her way blocked from leaving King's Landing, sees strangers in Stark colors guarding the ship meant to take her home, and watches Ned confess to treason before the Great Sept as Joffrey orders his head taken, until a black-cloaked man drags her away and threatens her with a knife.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Dany", "Khaleesi", "Silver Lady" – exiled Targaryen princess, wife of Khal Drogo, at term with a son she calls Rhaego; in early labor after the fight at the tent. Promised Mirri Maz Duur freedom and accepted her price ("only death may pay for life", though not Dany's). Stopped the rape of Lhazareen captives and claimed them as slaves (including the timid girl Eroeh). Her three dragon's eggs showed no sign of life earlier.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, huge Dothraki warlord; vowed to conquer the Seven Kingdoms for his son. His chest wound festered; he fell from his horse, delirious with fever, and lies in a bath of horse blood while the spell is worked. All three bloodriders died in the revolt against the "maegi": Cohollo, Haggo, and Qotho (maimed by Jorah). Rival kos Jhaqo and Pono would fight over the khalasar; many Dothraki are leaving.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight of Bear Island (disgraced son of Jeor Mormont), Dany's adviser; fought Qotho and is badly wounded (half an ear gone, gashed hip). Handmaids Doreah, Irri, Jhiqui; khas warriors Aggo (archer), Rakharo, Jhogo; Quaro was killed by Qotho. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister who sheltered the siblings. Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo.
  • Mirri Maz Duur – old Lhazareen godswife and healer, Dany's slave; studied in Asshai and with the maester Marwyn; sacrificed Drogo's red stallion and is chanting in the sealed tent where shadows dance. Lhazareen ("Lamb Men") – herding people now slaves in the khalasar.
House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Robb Stark "the Young Wolf" – fifteen-year-old eldest son, leader of the northern host (wolf Grey Wind); after crossing the Twins he ambushed Jaime's cavalry at night near Riverrun and took Jaime prisoner (refusing to behead him: "more use alive"). Under the Twins deal he will wed one of Walder Frey's daughters after the war. Unaware, in this chapter, of his father's fate.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – Ned's wife, with Robb's host; wants her father, Edmure, daughters and Ned back; told Robb they had won a battle, not a war.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, formerly King's Hand; brought out of the dungeon, thin and in pain, held up by gold cloaks, his broken leg's cast grey and rotten. On the High Septon's pulpit outside the Great Sept of Baelor he publicly confessed to treason (plotting to depose and murder Joffrey and seize the throne) and declared Joffrey the true heir; stones struck him. Joffrey rejected the mercy asked by Cersei and Sansa and ordered Ser Ilyn Payne to bring him his head; Ned was forced down on the marble and Ilyn raised his greatsword. Arya was dragged away before seeing the stroke and heard the crowd's sigh; Yoren says "They're done here."
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey; stood among the court in sky-blue silk, hid her face during the confession, then fell screaming to her knees when Joffrey ordered the execution.
  • Arya Stark – younger daughter, fled the Red Keep after Ned's arrest and has hidden since in Flea Bottom dressed as a boy, sleeping on rooftops, killing pigeons with her wooden stick sword and trading half a bird for stew; her silver bracelet and spare clothes were stolen but she keeps Needle (the sword Jon gave her) hidden under her cloak. Cannot leave the city: gates are watched and no one can go out. Recalled Syrio's "look with your eyes" and noticed the three Stark-cloaked guardsmen at the Wind Witch (the ship Ned hired to take her home) were strangers. Saw her father's confession and tried to fight through the crowd; broke a thumbnail. Seized by Yoren, who tangled his hand in her hair and raised a knife.
  • Bran Stark – crippled second son, lord in Winterfell; Rickon – youngest, with direwolf Shaggydog; Jon Snow – Ned's bastard at the Wall; Arya recalls playing hoops with them and Old Nan's tales.
  • Winterfell household: Ser Rodrik Cassel (castellan), Maester Luwin (castle maester), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward, cocky, with Robb), Hodor (huge simple stableboy who only says "Hodor"), Osha (captured wildling woman), Old Nan (storyteller), Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard); Ned's guards Alyn and Harwin. Dead: Jory Cassel (Ned's guard captain), Hullen (master of horse), Desmond, Tomard, Heward, Wyl, Varly, Cayn; Lady (Sansa's direwolf). Wolves: Summer (Bran), Grey Wind, Ghost (Jon), Shaggydog.
Robb's host / Northern bannermen
  • Roose Bolton – pale-eyed Lord of the Dreadfort; his foot was beaten by Tywin on the kingsroad, but he escaped. Lord Hornwood died there; Lord Cerwyn, Ser Wylis Manderly (fat son of Lord Wyman, White Harbor), Harrion Karstark and four Freys were captured.
  • At the ambush: the Greatjon (Lord Umber), Maege Mormont (Lady Mormont), Rickard Karstark (lost two sons), Galbart Glover, Lord Jason Mallister; Ser Helman Tallhart holds the Twins. Robb's guard: Theon Greyjoy (eager, bloodthirsty), Patrek Mallister, Smalljon Umber, Robin Flint, Ser Wendel Manderly, Dacey Mormont (Maege's eldest, heir to Bear Island, wields a morningstar), Olyvar Frey. Dead: Torrhen and Eddard Karstark (Lord Karstark's sons) and Daryn Hornwood, killed by Jaime. Howland Reed (mentioned) – Ned's friend at Greywater Watch.
House Frey (the Twins)
  • Lord Walder Frey, "Lord of the Crossing" – ninety, wizened, gouty, sharp-tongued, with an eighth wife of sixteen and scores of descendants; Hoster Tully's bannerman who holds the only bridge over the Green Fork; resentful of Tullys and Arryns. Sold Robb the crossing and his swords (four hundred kept home). A Frey son is married to Tywin's sister.
  • Ser Stevron Frey – eldest son and heir, past sixty; Ser Jared, Ser Hosteen, Ser Danwell (sons), Ronel Rivers (bastard) led the Frey levies; Ser Perwyn (youngest envoy); Olyvar Frey – Robb's new squire; Elmar Frey – youngest son, pledged to Arya; two boys both named Walder (eight and seven), wards for Winterfell. Four Freys were captured fighting for Robb on the kingsroad. Sigil: twin towers, blue on grey.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Joffrey Baratheon – boy king (in crimson silk with stags and lions, gold crown); told the crowd Cersei bids him let Ned take the black and Sansa begged mercy, but called that soft-hearted and ordered Ned's head struck off. Queen Regent Cersei Lannister – in black mourning with a veil of black diamonds beside him. Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children. King Robert Baratheon – dead; the city's rumors disagree on how (boar, poison, Ned, Renly, Varys, the queen, pox).
  • Small council: Lord Tywin Lannister (Hand); Janos Slynt (frog-faced City Watch commander made a lord; the stout man in black-and-gold armor who prodded Ned to speak louder is apparently he); Grand Maester Pycelle; Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" (Master of Coin; possibly the short man with the silvery cape and pointed beard Arya noticed); Lord Varys (eunuch Master of Whisperers, in damask, tried to stop Joffrey). The High Septon – squat, very fat, grey with age, in a crown of spun gold and crystal; asked Joffrey for mercy or sentence. Gold cloaks hold the walls and gates for the Lannisters.
  • Kingsguard (four around Joffrey on the plaza): Ser Jaime Lannister – Lord Commander by decree (now Robb's prisoner); Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – in a white cloak over dark grey armor; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Boros Blount, Ser Mandon Moore, Ser Arys Oakheart. Ser Ilyn Payne – the King's Justice, tall, fleshless executioner in iron mail with a dark two-handed greatsword. Ser Barristan Selmy – dismissed as Lord Commander, threw down his sword and left; wanted for treason after killing two gold cloaks sent to seize him (per Mormont's news).
  • Lords at court: Ser Horas and Ser Hobber Redwyne – identical orange-haired, freckled twins ("Ser Horror" and "Ser Slobber" to Sansa and Jeyne); Ser Dontos, Ser Balon Swann, Lord Gyles, Jalabhar Xho, Ser Aron Santagar. Lord Renly Baratheon fled the city in the night (rumors blame him for Robert's death); Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone, feared by Cersei) is named with other lords in Joffrey's summons.
House Lannister
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – golden twin of Cersei, Lord Commander of the Kingsguard by decree; besieged Riverrun, then was lured out and captured by Robb after killing Torrhen and Eddard Karstark and Daryn Hornwood in a charge at Robb. Held in irons; Lord Karstark wants his head. About a hundred knights and a dozen lords were captured with him (Lord Westerling, Lord Banefort, Ser Garth Greenfield, Lord Estren, Ser Tytos Brax, Mallor the Dornishman) plus three of Tywin's nephews.
  • Lord Tywin Lannister – Warden of the West, Hand of the King; bald, cold; won the battle on the kingsroad (placing Tyrion's clansmen in the van as bait for a feigned rout) but missed Robb; Jaime's army was broken without his knowledge. Ser Kevan Lannister – his brother and chief lieutenant. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – huge, brutish knight who burned the Pipers and Brackens; led the van. Ser Addam Marbrand – cavalry commander. Bannermen: Lord Lefford (stores), Lydden, Serrett, Ser Flement Brax, Crakehalls, Swyfts.
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Cersei's dwarf brother, Tywin's youngest son; fought on the kingsroad with about three hundred hill clansmen, killed a swordsman, took a knight prisoner, but badly hurt his right elbow. Bronn – sellsword bodyguard. Shae – young camp follower sharing his tent. Podrick Payne – his quiet squire, cousin of Ser Ilyn Payne. Clansmen: Shagga (huge Stone Crow), Chella (Black Ears scout), Timett (one-eyed Burned Man), Gunthor; Conn was killed and Ulf fell, apparently dead.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, captive of the Lannisters. Lord Hoster Tully – their father at Riverrun, sick. Lord Blackwood and survivors are besieged inside Riverrun. Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, scouted for Robb (Knight of the Gate) and drew the map for the ambush. Marq Piper and Karyl Vance raid Tywin's rear; Beric Dondarrion and Thoros (red priest) harass Lannister foragers; Lady Whent yielded Harrenhal. Lysa Arryn and son Robert Arryn – at the Eyrie, not stirring. Ser Raymun Darry – killed fighting for Ned.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Yoren – old, ragged Night's Watch recruiter (greasy black hair, tangled beard, rotten teeth, patched black cloak) who once visited Ned; found Arya in the crowd, called her "boy," hauled her away, returned her sword Needle, and at the chapter's end holds her by the hair with a knife.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward to the Lord Commander at Castle Black; owns the Valyrian sword Longclaw; direwolf Ghost. Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander. Maester Aemon – blind maester, born Aemon Targaryen. Samwell Tarly – fat, timid friend of Jon. Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder, Pate, Rudge, Matt – Jon's brothers; Donal Noye – armorer; Hobb – cook; Clydas – aide to Aemon; Bowen Marsh – Lord Steward; Ser Endrew Tarth – new master-at-arms; Dywen, Hake – rangers. Ser Alliser Thorne – sent south with a wight's severed hand for Joffrey. Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing. Dead: Othor, Jafer Flowers (wights, destroyed) and Ser Jaremy Rykker.
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (white walkers), wights (animated dead), children of the forest, Mance Rayder (King-beyond-the-Wall), wildlings, giants.
Other
  • Past figures (mentioned): Rhaegar Targaryen – late crown prince; Lyanna Stark – Ned's dead sister; Elia Martell and her daughter Rhaenys – Rhaegar's Dornish wife and child, killed when the Lannisters took King's Landing; Ser Gerold Hightower, Ser Arthur Dayne, Ser Oswell Whent – Aerys's Kingsguard.
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing master who taught Arya. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Captain Qos – ship captain Ned sent to Dragonstone.
