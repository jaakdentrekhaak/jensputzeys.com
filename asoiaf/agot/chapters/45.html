Chapter 45 – Eddard

Recovering from his broken leg, Ned hears of Lannister threats, then meets Queen Cersei alone in the godswood, confronts her with what he has learned, and warns her to flee before the king returns.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; leg still healing in the Tower of the Hand. Trusts almost no one in the city. Has worked out from Jon Arryn's book, Sansa's remark and the black-haired bastards that Cersei's children are not Robert's, and has had Cersei confirm it. Has arranged for Sansa and Arya to sail on the Wind Witch from Braavos three days hence; doubled the guard on the Tower. Intends to tell Robert the truth when he returns and gave Cersei a chance to flee first; troubled by a dream of Rhaegar's murdered children and determined to save Cersei's children from that fate. Believes Bran stumbled on the truth, which is why he was attacked.
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey; her innocent remark that Joffrey is nothing like the old drunken king helped Ned understand. Arya Stark – his younger daughter. Both are about to be sent home by ship.
  • Tomard "Fat Tom" – portly, ginger-whiskered, affable, loyal guardsman of near fifty; now commands Ned's household guard (with Jory dead and Alyn away). Varly – guardsman who helps carry Ned. Alyn – guard riding out with Lord Beric; Harwin – another Stark guard. Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl – dead Stark guards. Septa Mordane, Jeyne Poole, Vayon Poole – the girls' septa, Sansa's friend, and Winterfell's steward (who booked the ship).
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie; Cersei says she seized Tyrion. Robb (ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon, Jon Snow (bastard at the Wall, whom Cersei has seen), Benjen (missing); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; dead Lyanna and Brandon. Ser Rodrik Cassel with Catelyn. Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – the king's wife; still bruised where Robert struck her. Admitted to Ned that she and her twin Jaime are lovers since childhood, that all three of her children are Jaime's, that she once conceived Robert's child and secretly aborted it, and that she hates Robert (he called her "Lyanna" on their wedding night). Tried to tempt Ned into alliance, slapped him, mocked his bastard (mentioning Lady Ashara, who she says drowned herself), and warned that in the game of thrones "you win or you die".
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunken hunter, away in the forest chasing a monstrous boar after wolves ruined the white hart; Ned's friend. Merciful to honest enemies (pardoned Barristan, Pycelle, Varys, Balon Greyjoy) but, Ned believes, would never forgive this betrayal.
  • Prince Joffrey – golden-haired heir, Cersei's eldest, back from the hunt with the Royces and Ser Balon Swann. Cersei's other children are Tommen and Myrcella.
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – old, chain-jangling healer; brought word of Lord Tywin's angry letter to the queen; Ned thinks he is Cersei's creature. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin, quipping and untrustworthy to Ned though he hid Catelyn; reports freeriders and sellswords flocking to Casterly Rock. Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers; knows too much, does too little. Ser Barristan Selmy – old, rigid Lord Commander, would tell Ned to do his duty. Lord Beric Dondarrion – leading the king's justice expedition against Gregor Clegane under the king's banner, with Thoros of Myr, Ser Gladden, Lord Lothar. Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" – accused of the riverlands massacres, sentenced to death; Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's sworn shield, loathes his brother but is likely to inherit his lands; back at court, went to the queen; Ned finds him the most worrying Lannister man. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin and lover; fled to his father after attacking Ned's men. Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock, angry at Beric's mission; Ned recalls dreaming of him laying Rhaegar's murdered children beneath the Iron Throne. Ser Loras Tyrell, Ser Ilyn Payne, Renly, Stannis, Ser Arys Oakheart, Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Balon Swann, Lord Royce / Ser Robar Royce, Barra, Lady Tanda (mentioned; hosts lunches, has a fat daughter).
  • Yoren – Night's Watch recruiter who left with his pick of dungeon prisoners. Two Dornish Marches freeriders pledged to the king.
  • Rhaegar's children (mentioned) – a boy and a little princess, killed under Tywin's orders when King's Landing fell, per Ned's dream. Jon Arryn – the late Hand; Ned believes he was killed for learning the secret. Lady Ashara (mentioned) – Dornish lady, sister of a man Ned killed, said by Cersei to have drowned herself.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, defending Riverrun; Hoster Tully – Riverrun's old lord; Ser Raymun Darry, Ser Karyl Vance, Ser Marq Piper – riverlands knights who asked the king's leave to strike back at Clegane; Joss – Sherrer brewer-survivor. Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister; Robert Arryn – her sickly boy lord; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle, bound for Riverrun.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing hill clansmen of the Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot) to whom he offered "the Vale of Arryn".
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, a recruit about to be passed to the Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly – fat, bookish recruit Jon is shielding; Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder – fellow recruits; Ser Alliser Thorne – harsh master-at-arms; Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Maester Aemon – blind, aged maester; Chett – hostile steward; Benjen – Ned's brother, First Ranger, missing.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife, owns a dragon egg. Viserys Targaryen – her cruel brother, "the dragon". Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord husband. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Braavosi dancing (sword) master teaching Arya. Grand Maester Malleon (author, mentioned) – wrote the big book of great-house lineages Jon Arryn was reading; its records show black hair always overcoming gold in stag-lion matches. Prince Aemon the Dragonknight, Queen Naerys, Jonquil and Ser Florian – song heroes.

Places

Places
  • The godswood (Red Keep) – quiet garden with a faceless oak heart tree, where Ned meets Cersei "so the gods can see". The Tower of the Hand – Ned's quarters, guard doubled. The Red Keep / King's Landing – the capital. The Iron Throne room, the kingswood (where the king hunts), the Blackwater.
  • Winterfell – where the girls are to be sent by sea. Braavos – port the Wind Witch sails from. Casterly Rock – Lannister seat, gathering swords. Riverrun, the Eyrie, the mountains of the Vale, Castle Black / the Wall, the Free Cities, the Summer Isles, the Port of Ibben (exile destinations Ned names).
