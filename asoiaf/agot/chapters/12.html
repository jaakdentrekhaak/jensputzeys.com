Chapter 12 – Eddard

Riding out alone with King Robert at dawn on the kingsroad, Ned hears that Daenerys Targaryen has wed a Dothraki khal, argues against the king's wish to kill her, and learns Robert has secretly promised the Warden of the East post to Jaime Lannister.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, travelling south with the king's party to become Hand; honourable, blunt, and uneasy about leaving Winterfell and Catelyn in her grief. Feels helpless to restrain Robert ("no Jon Arryn"). Admits he dishonoured himself and Catelyn by fathering Jon; refuses to discuss the boy's mother, Wylla 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; a common girl). Detests the Targaryen killings of children and distrusts Jaime for the manner of Aerys's death; was once estranged from Robert over the murder of Rhaegar's children. Squire/servant Alyn 🆕 new this chapter wakes him.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; stays at Winterfell with her comatose son Bran (7; back broken, still unconscious). Robb (heir), Sansa (betrothed to Joffrey), Arya (secretly carries the sword Needle) and Rickon with their direwolves (Grey Wind, Lady, Nymeria, Shaggydog) are travelling or staying behind as planned.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14, on the road north to the Wall with his uncle Benjen (Night's Watch; wolf Ghost).
  • Lyanna (dead) – Ned's sister; Robert's lost love, who died after Rhaegar took her (Robert says he raped her; Ned recalls her deathbed plea "Promise me, Ned"). Brandon and Lord Rickard (father) – killed by the Mad King Aerys (Robert calls it unspeakable). Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Left at Winterfell: Maester Luwin (maester), Ser Rodrik (household knight), Mikken (smith), Hodor (simple stableboy), Old Nan, Chayle (septon-librarian). With the party: Jory Cassel (guard captain), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Septa Mordane (girls' septa), Hullen and Harwin (master of horse and son).
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, who accuses the Lannisters of murdering her husband Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand and Ned's foster father; Ned thinks of her accusation), Robert Arryn (sickly boy, Ned's nephew; Robert refuses to make him Warden of the East), Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully (Catelyn's brother, uncle and father).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – huge, bluff, impatient, restless with the slow royal progress (hates the creaking wheelhouse); fond of Ned but heedless of his advice. Hates the Targaryens obsessively ("I will kill every Targaryen…"); wanted a knife sent to Daenerys; regrets not killing the exiles earlier on Jon Arryn's advice. Fears the Dothraki (said to be 100,000 strong) and those who still call him "Usurper". Has promised the Warden of the East to Jaime Lannister; unmoved by Ned's objections. Ned's old comrade-in-arms; won the crown at the Trident, wounded by Rhaegar.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister, "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin, Kingsguard. As a seventeen-year-old he killed Aerys at the sack of King's Landing and sat the Iron Throne in his gilded armour and lion helm, jesting to Ned that he was "keeping it warm for Robert". Ned distrusts him; Robert does not.
  • Tywin Lannister – Lord of Casterly Rock, Warden of the West, father of Cersei, Jaime and Tyrion; delivered Rhaegar's wife and children's corpses to Robert (the children murdered by his soldiers); took King's Landing by feigned loyalty to Aerys.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – Robert's wife; her wheelhouse is a source of the king's irritation. Prince Joffrey, Tommen, Myrcella – their children. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – Joffrey's burned bodyguard. Tyrion "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother, heading to the Wall. Ser Boros and Ser Meryn – Kingsguard knights escorting the king this morning. Maester Pycelle – royal maester. Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, at King's Landing. Stannis (mentioned) – Robert's brother, proved himself at the siege of Storm's End. Renly (mentioned) – Robert's other brother. Littlefinger (mentioned) – court figure. Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands, beaten nine years ago.
  • Lord Varys 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – eunuch, the king's master of whisperers, served Aerys before Robert; sent word that Daenerys has wed.
  • Mad King Aerys II (dead; killed by Jaime; killed Ned's father and brother), Rhaegar (dead; killed at the Trident), his wife and two small children (murdered by Tywin's soldiers in the sack of King's Landing).
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother); Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen – 13, newly married to Khal Drogo, who is said to command a huge horde; Robert wants her dead. Viserys Targaryen – her brother, "the beggar king". Illyrio Mopatis (fat Pentoshi magister who arranged the marriage), Khal Drogo (her huge Dothraki husband), her handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, and Drogo's bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exile: house Mormont of Bear Island (Stark bannermen); sold poachers to a Tyroshi slaver, fled five years ago before Ned could reach him. Now in Pentos with the exiles; Robert says he is working as an informant for Varys in hopes of a pardon. Ned despises him ("I would rather he become a corpse").

Places

Places
  • The kingsroad / the north – the royal party camps and creeps south from Winterfell; flat brown plains with barrows of the First Men (ancient burial mounds common across the north).
  • Winterfell (Stark seat), King's Landing (capital; the Red Keep and the Iron Throne with dragon skulls on the walls), The Trident (where Robert won the crown), Storm's End (Baratheon seat; Stannis's famed siege), Bear Island 🆕 new this chapter (Mormont seat, cold and poor), Pentos (Free City where the exiles stay), Free Cities (Tyrosh, Myr), Vaes Dothrak, The Wall / Castle Black, Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, Riverrun (Tully seat), the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Starfall, Iron Islands, Dragonstone.
