Chapter 23 – Daenerys

Riding east with the khalasar, Daenerys recalls how she endured and then came to love riding and the plains, humbles her brother Viserys when he attacks her, and reaches out to her husband Drogo, learning she is pregnant on her fourteenth name day.

Characters

House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen ("Dany", "Khaleesi") – POV; turns 14 in this chapter; wife of Khal Drogo. Suffered terribly in the first days of riding, then (after a dream of a black-and-blood dragon swallowing her in fire) grew strong and sure; her gift silver filly (Dothraki do not name animals, so she thinks of it only as "the silver") is her "real teacher" and she loves it. Now at the head of the khalasar beside Drogo and his bloodriders. Humbled Viserys, ordering him to walk (the Dothraki treat a man afoot as dishonored); now says aloud that he will never take back the Seven Kingdoms. Deliberately took the initiative with Drogo (as Doreah taught), who called her name; is pregnant (Jhiqui noticed; Dany says she knows). Keeps three dragon eggs (gift from Illyrio) in her tent; one, black-and-scarlet, feels warm; she tells herself the sun warms them. Believes dragons are dead.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her brother, calls himself king/"Lord of the Seven Kingdoms"; miserable and awkward on the plains; refused Illyrio's offer to wait in Pentos, staying until Drogo pays "the debt" with a crown. Attacked Dany for commanding him and was whipped by Jhogo; shamed by being made to walk back; hostile and sobbing, his eyes "full of poison". Even Jorah calls him "less than the shadow of a snake".
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, the great Dothraki khal leading the khalasar east to Vaes Dothrak; ignores her by day, drinks and watches fights with his warriors at night; comes to her tent before dawn; this night follows her outside to open air and calls her name. Bells in his braid.
  • Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight from Bear Island, bull-necked and hairy; swore his sword to Viserys but now openly sides with Dany; tells her Viserys wouldn't make a good king and the common people just pray for peace and rain; longs for home. Warned Viserys to stay on the ridge.
  • Handmaids: Irri (Dothraki girl, Dany's age, teaches her to ride and translates; says dragons are evil, the moon is god and wife of the sun), Jhiqui (Dothraki, Dany's age; she and Irri were taken as slaves when Drogo destroyed their father's khalasar; teaches Dothraki), Doreah (Lysene, ~20, blond, found by Illyrio in a Lys pleasure house, taught Dany lovemaking; tells a Qarth tale that the moon is a cracked egg).
  • Her khas: Jhogo 🆕 new this chapter (young whip-wielding rider who saved her; asked if she wanted Viserys dead), Quaro 🆕 new this chapter (suggested taking Viserys' ear). Drogo's bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.
  • Illyrio Mopatis – fat Pentoshi magister back in Pentos; gave Dany the eggs, offered Viserys hospitality, tells them the common people sew dragon banners (Dany reports).
  • Rhaegar (her late brother, "the last dragon", died on the Trident), Aegon the Conqueror (founder of the dynasty), Aegon III "Dragonbane" 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned; in his reign the last Targaryen dragons died, about a century and a half ago).

Wolves: Ghost, Summer; Lady dead; Nymeria gone.
