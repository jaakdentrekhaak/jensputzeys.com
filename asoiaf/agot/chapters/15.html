Chapter 15 – Sansa

On the kingsroad by the Trident, Sansa is frightened by the king's new escort, then spends a wine-warmed day riding with Prince Joffrey until they come upon Arya and her friend Mycah sparring with sticks, and a fight breaks out.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – POV; Ned's eldest daughter, 11ish, auburn hair; devoted to songs and courtly ideals, already in love with her betrothed Joffrey. Embarrassed by Arya, afraid of the Hound and Ser Ilyn. Spent a day alone with Joffrey (drank more wine than ever before) and, at the end, was told coldly by him to go away and not touch him. Direwolf Lady (growls at threats, calmed by Sansa).
  • Arya Stark – Sansa's younger sister, scrawny, "horsey-faced", looks like Jon; hates the queen, refuses court manners, rides with the column's commoners, wears riding leathers; has bruises from sparring. During the trek she picked "poison kisses" flowers for Ned. Refused the queen's invitation to go look for Rhaegar's rubies at the ford. Hit Joffrey with a stick, threw his sword into the Trident, and fled with Nymeria. Direwolf Nymeria (grey; bit and mauled Joffrey's sword arm at Arya's side, called off by Arya). Her friend Mycah is with her; she secretly has Needle (from earlier).
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, now Hand of the King; left before dawn to join the king's hunt (aurochs). Treats Arya's wildness indulgently.
  • Left behind: Catelyn (Ned's wife), Robb (heir), Bran (7, hurt, unconscious), Rickon (3). Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, gone north to the Wall; Arya looks like him. Lyanna, Rickard, Brandon – Ned's dead sister, father, brother; Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household (with the party)
  • Septa Mordane – Sansa's and Arya's strict chaperone; insists on ladylike conduct. With the party (not seen here): Ned's household, e.g. Jory Cassel (guard captain) and Hullen (master of horse). Left at Winterfell: Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Maester Luwin, Ser Rodrik Cassel, Hodor, Old Nan, Hallis Mollen (new captain of the guard).
  • Mycah 🆕 new this chapter – thirteen, a butcher's boy who sleeps in the meat wagon, red-haired and freckled; Arya's friend who spars with her ("she ast me to"). Joffrey cut his cheek with a sword; Mycah fled into the trees.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand); her son Robert Arryn is Lord of the Eyrie. Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father at Riverrun.
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Prince Joffrey Baratheon – about 12, golden hair, Sansa's betrothed; charming and gallant when it suits, boasts of his father killing Rhaegar and of "uncle Jaime" killing Aerys. Wields sword Lion's Tooth (now lost in the Trident). Drunk, he tormented Mycah and was struck by Arya; Nymeria mauled his sword arm (badly torn and bloody). Said he would "tell my mother." Treats the Hound as his guard ("my mother's dog").
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – gracious in public; welcomed the council's riders; postponed the wheelhouse ride; told Sansa to relay apologies to Arya for Myrcella's ride. She dislikes the wolves, wouldn't allow Nymeria in the wheelhouse. King Robert Baratheon – the king, off on a hunt with Ned; he killed Rhaegar at the Trident and won the crown.
  • Ser Barristan Selmy ("Barristan the Bold") – old, white-haired Lord Commander of the Kingsguard and councillor; served Aerys and now Robert; white enamelled scale armor; courteous, kind to Sansa.
  • Renly Baratheon – Robert's youngest brother, Lord of Storm's End and king's councillor; near twenty, handsome, black hair, green eyes, green armor with a golden-antlered helm; laughing and teasing.
  • Ser Ilyn Payne 🆕 new this chapter – the King's Justice (royal executioner); gaunt, pockmarked, near-bald with long wisps of hair, greatsword on his back, pale cold eyes; mute, his tongue ripped out by King Aerys with hot pincers fourteen years ago. Terrifies Sansa and Lady.
  • Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – burned-faced bodyguard of Joffrey, set to guard him by Cersei; mocked Sansa's fear and the wolves ("The Starks use them for wet nurses"); sent away by Joffrey.
  • Princess Myrcella – nearly 8, gentle, afraid of wolves; Sansa admires her. Prince Tommen – her younger brother. Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – the queen's twin, Kingsguard (Joffrey calls him uncle and says he killed Aerys). Tyrion "the Imp" – their dwarf brother, gone north with Jon. Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – Lannister head. Maester Pycelle (mentioned), Stannis (mentioned, Robert's other brother), Littlefinger, Varys (court figures, mentioned), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned; self-styled King of the Iron Islands), Mad King Aerys II (dead, overthrown; had Ilyn's tongue ripped out), Rhaegar (dead, killed at the Trident by Robert).
  • The king's party has swollen to over 400. The council has sent riders and an honor guard from King's Landing.
The Night's Watch
  • Benjen Stark (Ned's brother) and Yoren (black brother) – travelling north with Jon; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (old commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys Targaryen (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.

Places

Places
  • The Trident 🆕 new this chapter – river on the kingsroad in the Riverlands, camp site of the royal party; where Robert killed Rhaegar and won the crown. Arya talks of "Rhaegar's rubies" there; Joffrey and Sansa rode along its north bank to the battleground at the river bend, where the fight with Arya happened. An inn (large, pale stone) holds a fraction of the party.
  • The Neck – boggy region the party crossed in twelve days along a narrow causeway (quicksand, snakes, lizard-lions, poison kisses flowers).
  • King's Landing – where the council's riders came from; the royal party is heading there.
  • Winterfell (Stark seat), White Knife / White Harbor (Catelyn's planned route), The Wall / Castle Black, Riverrun (Tully seat), Casterly Rock (Lannister seat), Dorne, the Eyrie / the Vale (Arryn seat), Storm's End (Renly's seat), Starfall, Myr, Iron Islands, Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone.
