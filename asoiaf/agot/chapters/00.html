Prologue – Will (Prologue)

Three rangers of the Night's Watch follow a wildling band north of the Wall, find the dead bodies gone, and are attacked by pale, silent figures in the dark.

Characters

The Night's Watch
  • Will 🆕 new this chapter – point-of-view ranger of the Night's Watch, four years on the Wall, a veteran of "a hundred rangings". Former poacher, given the choice of the black or losing a hand after Mallister freeriders caught him. Silent in the woods, a scout. Last seen in a tree-top ambush, seized by the throat by Royce's reanimated body.
  • Ser Waymar Royce 🆕 new this chapter – 18-year-old youngest son of an ancient house; Sworn Brother for under half a year, on his first ranging, in charge of the party. Arrogant and fond of his fine black clothes and sable cloak; ignores advice. Killed on-page by the pale figures (shouted "For Robert!" in his last fight), then rose again with a blue-burning eye and attacked Will.
  • Gared 🆕 new this chapter – old man-at-arms, over fifty, forty years in the Watch; lost his ears (cut away by Maester Aemon), toes and a finger to frostbite. Wary and frightened; resents Royce. Left guarding the horses at the bottom of the ridge; fate unknown.
  • Mormont 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – "that old bear", the commander who ordered the party to track the wildlings.
  • Maester Aemon 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – maester at the Wall who treated Gared's frostbite.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others 🆕 new this chapter – tall, gaunt, milk-pale beings with colour-shifting armour, ice-blue eyes and thin crystalline swords that shatter steel; they speak in a cracking, mocking tongue. Appear silently, with cold. Killed Royce in a group of at least five.
  • Dead wildling band 🆕 new this chapter – eight men and women Will found dead and unmarked (no blood, no cold strong enough to kill them), including a woman up a tree. Their bodies vanished when the party returned, leaving the weapons.
Other
  • Mallisters 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – a lord's household whose woods Will poached in.
  • Robert 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – the name Royce cried in defiance; context not yet explained.

Places

Places
  • The Wall 🆕 new this chapter – great barrier in the north guarded by the Night's Watch; found "weeping" (melting) recently, so it has not been very cold.
  • Castle Black 🆕 new this chapter – the Watch's base at the Wall, eight or nine days' ride south of where the party is.
  • The haunted forest 🆕 new this chapter – dark wilderness beyond the Wall where the party rides; the clearing with the dead camp lies beside a stream, near a great sentinel tree.
