Chapter 29 – Sansa

Sansa watches the first day of the Hand's tourney, sees a young knight killed in the lists, receives a rose from Ser Loras, is charmed by Joffrey at the feast where the king and queen quarrel, and is escorted home by the Hound, who tells her how he was scarred.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – POV; Ned's elder daughter, in King's Landing, dazzled by the tourney and by Joffrey (who she calls her prince/betrothed); has stopped hating him over Lady's death, blaming the queen and Arya instead. Composed when a man dies before her. Given a red rose by Loras. The Hound made her promise not to repeat his story and threatened to kill her if she tells anyone.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Sansa's father, Hand of the King; investigating Jon Arryn's death; promised Sansa good seats at the tourney. Arya (younger sister, blamed by Sansa for Lady's death), Bran (crippled by a fall, alive), Robb (acting lord of Winterfell), Rickon (youngest), Catelyn (her mother, traveling north in secret), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (uncle, missing), Old Nan. Direwolves: Ghost (Jon's), Summer (Bran's), Grey Wind (Robb's), Shaggydog (Rickon's); Lady (Sansa's, killed), Nymeria (Arya's, gone).
Winterfell household (and Ned's party)
  • Septa Mordane – Sansa's chaperone (and Arya's septa); approves her composure; dozes drunk at the feast. Jeyne Poole – Sansa's friend, steward's daughter; squeamish, left the tourney ill, romantic about Lord Beric. Jory Cassel – rode in the tourney in plain blue-grey armor; won two jousts, lost to freerider Lothor Brune. Alyn, Harwin – also jousted for the north and lost (Harwin to Ser Meryn, Alyn to Ser Balon Swann). Ser Rodrik (master-at-arms, with Catelyn); Ned's guards Varly, Jacks, Tomard, Desmond, Porther. At Winterfell: Hodor (simple giant), Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Maester Luwin, Mikken (smith), Vayon Poole (steward); Mycah (butcher's boy, dead).
House Tully / House Arryn / river lords
  • Lysa Arryn (Catelyn's sister, in the Eyrie), Robert Arryn (her sickly son), Jon Arryn (dead former Hand), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Lord of Riverrun), Edmure (her brother), Brynden (her uncle). Lord Jason Mallister (Seagard, indigo and silver eagle; killed three of Rhaegar's men at the Trident; jousting) and son Patrek. Lord Walder Frey (very old Tully bannerman) and six of his sons/grandsons "of the Crossing" (six Freys jousted), Martyn Rivers (Walder's bastard).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • Joffrey Baratheon – prince, golden-haired; wooed Sansa with courtesy at the feast, boasts he will one day "do for them all"; coldly leaves her to the Hound. His right arm is still troubled.
  • King Robert Baratheon – got very drunk at the feast, publicly humiliated Queen Cersei ("You do not tell me what to do, woman") by insisting he will fight in tomorrow's melee, shoved Jaime to the ground. Queen Cersei – stormed off silent and pale.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – golden armor under a Kingsguard cloak; rode brilliantly, beat Selmy, one of the last four. Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – burned face; wears a dog-head tunic; ordered by Joffrey to escort Sansa; drunk and bitter, says he is no knight and spits on knights' vows; tells Sansa that his brother Gregor shoved his face into a brazier when he was six and was never punished; says Gregor killed the young knight deliberately; as Sansa's pity grows, he then threatens to kill her if she tells. Unhorsed Renly.
  • Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain That Rides" 🆕 new this chapter – huge, brutal knight, five years older than the Hound; killed a poor young knight from the Vale in the joust (the Hound says on purpose); one of the final four.
  • Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" – 16, youngest son of Mace Tyrell, beautiful, flower-enameled armor and a white stallion; threw white roses to ladies and a red one to Sansa; one of the final four, favorite of the crowd.
  • Renly Baratheon – jousted in golden-antlered armor; unhorsed by the Hound, took it gracefully; calms the king. Stannis (the king's other brother, at Dragonstone), Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father), Tyrion (her dwarf brother, traveling south), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Ser Ilyn Payne (tongueless king's justice).
  • Petyr "Littlefinger" Baelish – approached Sansa, says her mother was his "queen of beauty" once; stroked her hair; wears a silver mockingbird. Varys (eunuch spymaster), Grand Maester Pycelle, Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander of the Kingsguard; beat two younger knights, lost to Jaime), Janos Slynt (City Watch commander), Syrio Forel (Arya's sword teacher), Balon Greyjoy (defeated rebel lord), Mad King Aerys II, Rhaegar Targaryen (knighted Gregor).
  • Lord Beric Dondarrion – lost his horse to a hedge knight's foul play, then unhorsed by Thoros. Thoros of Myr 🆕 new this chapter – red-robed, shaven-headed warrior priest who once scaled the walls of Pyke with a flaming sword; unhorsed Beric. Moon Boy 🆕 new this chapter – the king's pie-faced fool who mocks everyone on stilts. Ser Meryn (Kingsguard), Ser Balon Swann, Ser Andar Royce and Ser Robar Royce (sons of Lord Yohn "Bronze Yohn" Royce, runic bronze armor; Robar beaten by Loras), Ser Horas and Ser Hobber Redwyne, Lord Bryce Caron, Ser Aron Santagar, Jalabhar Xho (exile prince from the Summer Isles), Lothor Brune (freerider) 🆕 new this chapter – tourney competitors. Seven Kingsguard knights wear white.
  • Tobho Mott (armorer) and Gendry (his apprentice, a bastard of the king) – King's Landing.
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, recruit at Castle Black), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (14, pregnant, wed to Khal Drogo, riding with his horde), Viserys (her exiled brother), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exiled knight, her adviser), handmaids, bloodriders.
On the road (Catelyn's party)
  • Catelyn and Ser Rodrik (traveling north); Tyrion and Yoren at the crossroads inn; Marillion (singer), Masha Heddle (innkeeper). Catelyn's accusation of Tyrion is unresolved from Sansa's side.
