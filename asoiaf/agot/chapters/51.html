Chapter 51 – Sansa

Confined in Maegor's Holdfast for three days, Sansa is brought before the queen and her councillors, told her father is a traitor, and agrees to write letters urging her family to submit to Joffrey, while her friend Jeyne Poole is quietly removed.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Sansa Stark – Ned's elder daughter, obedient and romantic, besotted with Joffrey. Confessed to Cersei that Ned meant to send her home, which led to her confinement; locked in a high tower room of Maegor's Holdfast and told nothing for two days. Persuaded that her father is a traitor, she agreed to write four letters (to Catelyn, Robb and her brothers at Winterfell, Lysa Arryn and Hoster Tully) telling them to keep the king's peace and swear fealty to Joffrey, in exchange for hope of marrying him. Forgot to ask about Arya. Looks to Joffrey as her gallant prince and believes he would be merciful.
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, King's Hand; the queen says he has "not been harmed" and his fate is for the king to decide. Branded a traitor for naming Stannis heir.
  • Arya Stark – Sansa's younger sister, whom Sansa has not asked about; the queen recalls Arya setting her direwolf on Joffrey.
  • Jeyne Poole – steward Vayon Poole's daughter and Sansa's friend, a weepy girl; shut in with Sansa, then taken away on the queen's orders to Littlefinger's apartments, told she would see her father. Vayon Poole – Winterfell's steward, unarmed (his fate is not stated; Jeyne weeps for him). Septa Mordane – the girls' septa.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie; Robb (eldest son, ruling Winterfell), Bran (crippled), Rickon (youngest); Jon Snow (sworn brother of the Watch); the queen and Pycelle call Ned's sons "mere boys" and Littlefinger says Catelyn and the Tullys are the real concern. Direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost; Ser Rodrik Cassel; Winterfell household (Theon Greyjoy, Maester Luwin, Osha, Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen).
  • Stark men: the captain of Ned's household guard was found dead with Ned's letter to Stannis; Hullen, Desmond, Tomard, Varly, Cayn, Jory Cassel, Heward, Wyl – dead; Porther, Alyn, Harwin.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – dead; the great bell and the bells of the Great Sept tolled for him at sunset on the second day.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – in black silk with ruby teardrops; sweet and motherly to Sansa while manipulating her, uses Ned's blood-stained letter to Stannis as proof of treason, had Jeyne removed. Joffrey – styled king, blond; Sansa's intended husband; not seen this chapter. Myrcella, Tommen – their younger children.
  • Small council (all in mourning black): Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin, whose gaze makes Sansa feel unclothed; will take Jeyne; says Sansa is the image of Catelyn. Lord Varys – eunuch Master of Whisperers, flowery scent, wringing soft hands; holds Ned's seal and stamped Sansa's letters. Grand Maester Pycelle – aged, big soft beard; claims Ned swore to protect the princes and says traitor's blood tells.
  • Kingsguard: Ser Boros Blount – ugly, bandy-legged, grey-haired, escorted Sansa; Ser Mandon Moore 🆕 new this chapter – Kingsguard knight with a curiously dead face, who guards and escorts her; Ser Arys Oakheart – led Sansa to her tower; Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Preston Greenfield, Sandor Clegane "the Hound" (reportedly broke down Jeyne's door with a warhammer), Ser Barristan Selmy (Lord Commander, not mentioned this chapter), Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" (fled for his father), Lord Tywin Lannister. Janos Slynt and the gold cloaks of the City Watch; Lannister guardsmen now patrol Maegor's Holdfast walls.
  • Lord Renly Baratheon (fled with Ser Loras Tyrell), Lord Stannis Baratheon (at Dragonstone; Ned's letter invited him to take the crown), Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain" (Sandor's huge brother), Lord Beric Dondarrion and Thoros of Myr (sent against Gregor), Ser Ilyn Payne (mute King's Justice), Lancel (Robert's squire, queen's cousin), Ser Balon Swann, Jon Arryn (dead Hand), Yoren, Robert's bastards Gendry, Mya, Barra.
Riverlands / Vale
  • Lysa Arryn – Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister (Sansa's aunt), with her sickly son Robert Arryn; Lord Hoster Tully – Sansa's grandfather, lord of Riverrun; Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's brother, defending Riverrun; Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully – Catelyn's uncle.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed from the Eyrie, in the Vale mountains with his sellsword Bronn, facing the hill clan Stone Crows (Gunthor, Shagga, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot).
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, sworn brother and steward to the Lord Commander; Samwell Tarly – his fat bookish friend, Maester Aemon's assistant; Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" – Lord Commander; Maester Aemon – blind, aged maester; Ser Alliser Thorne – master-at-arms; Bowen Marsh (Lord Steward), Othell Yarwyck (First Builder), Ser Jaremy Rykker (leads the rangers), Grenn, Pyp, Toad, Dareon, Cotter Pyke (Eastwatch); Benjen Stark – Ned's brother, missing First Ranger.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – Drogo's pregnant wife in Vaes Dothrak; Khal Drogo – her Dothraki warlord husband; Viserys – her brother, killed by Drogo; Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; Aggo, Rakharo, Jhogo.
Other
  • Syrio Forel – Arya's Braavosi dancing master; last seen facing Ser Meryn Trant. Captain Qos – master of the Wind Witch, the ship the girls were to take.

Places

Places
  • Maegor's Holdfast – the keep-within-a-keep of the Red Keep, ringed by a dry moat with iron spikes (a body is impaled there), a drawbridge and portcullis; Sansa is held in its highest tower. The council chambers – long table, carved screen and twin sphinxes by the door; where the queen interviewed Sansa. The Great Sept of Baelor (on Visenya's Hill) – where Sansa is promised she will wed the king. The Tower of the Hand – Ned's quarters; bloodied stairs.
