Chapter 43 – Eddard

Sitting the Iron Throne in Robert's absence, Ned hears riverlands survivors blame raiders led by Gregor Clegane and sends Lord Beric Dondarrion with a party to bring the king's justice to Clegane's keep.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell and Hand of the King; leg still in a cast, he sits the Iron Throne while Robert hunts. Honorable and blunt: believes Tywin sent Gregor Clegane, disguised as a brigand, to burn riverlands villages and provoke Riverrun; refused the riverlords vengeance, instead denounced Clegane, stripped his lands and sentenced him to death, sending Lord Beric with twenty-man parties. Still haunted by his dream of Lyanna dying.
  • Alyn and Porther – his guards, who help him up and down the throne; Vayon Poole – steward; Jory Cassel – dead former captain.
  • Sansa – Ned's elder daughter, watching court from the gallery with Septa Mordane, her teacher. Arya – younger daughter, silent since the attack; has the sword Needle. Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie; her seizure of Tyrion started the trouble. Robb (fifteen, ruling Winterfell), Bran (eight, crippled), Rickon, Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (missing First Ranger); direwolves Summer, Grey Wind, Ghost. Lyanna and Brandon – dead sister and elder brother.
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel (master-at-arms, with Catelyn), Maester Luwin, Theon Greyjoy (ward), Osha (captive wildling), Hodor, Old Nan, Mikken, Hallis Mollen – Winterfell household.
Royal court (Baratheons, small council, Kingsguard)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, hunting-mad; away across the Blackwater chasing a white hart for days, with Renly, Ser Barristan, Joffrey, Sandor Clegane and Balon Swann; asked Ned to sit the throne for him.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – proud, cold, Tywin's daughter. Prince Joffrey – her son, heir, on the hunt.
  • Varys "the Spider" – eunuch Master of Whisperers, soft-spoken, probing; tells Ned he should have sent Loras (a man with Lannister enemies should court the Tyrells) and that Ser Ilyn may be insulted. Grand Maester Pycelle – defends the Lannisters, urges deferring to Tywin's justice. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – Master of Coin, wry; asks the knights sharp questions.
  • Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, hunting. Ser Arys Oakheart 🆕 new this chapter – Kingsguard knight in white armor, at the foot of the throne. Ser Meryn Trant, Ser Balon Swann (hunting with the king), Ser Ilyn Payne – mute, pale-eyed King's Justice (the Paynes are Lannister bannermen); Ned bypassed him, and he watched displeased from the back of the hall. Renly Baratheon and Stannis – the king's brothers. Ser Loras Tyrell "the Knight of Flowers" – sixteen, handsome, volunteered to kill Clegane and was refused ("justice, not vengeance"); left sullen.
  • Sandor Clegane "the Hound" – with the hunt; Ser Gregor Clegane "the Mountain That Rides" – his immense brother, Tywin's bannerman, accused by the survivors of leading the massacres (a giant who flew no banner); sentenced to death. Tywin Lannister – lord of Casterly Rock, "as much fox as lion". Barra (Robert's baby bastard). Ser Robar Royce – knight Ned sends to bring the king word.
  • Lord Beric Dondarrion – young red-gold-haired lord, given command of the force sent against Clegane. Thoros of Myr – red priest; Ser Gladden 🆕 new this chapter and Lord Lothar 🆕 new this chapter – each to raise twenty men for it.
Riverlands (House Tully and vassals)
  • Ser Edmure Tully – Catelyn's young brother, commanding Riverrun's defenses; gallant rather than wise. Hoster Tully – old lord of Riverrun, who sent the knights to ask the king's leave before striking back.
  • Ser Raymun Darry – scornful riverlands knight; says the raiders were Lannister brigands. Ser Karyl Vance 🆕 new this chapter – sad-eyed, winestain birthmark, son of Lord Vance, who holds the pass below the Golden Tooth. Ser Marq Piper 🆕 new this chapter – swaggering, hot-blooded young knight, Edmure's friend. Joss 🆕 new this chapter – brewer from Sherrer, one of the survivors brought to court.
  • Lysa Arryn, Robert Arryn, Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle), Ser Lyn Corbray, Maester Colemon, Jon Arryn (dead Hand) – at the Eyrie / in the Vale.
House Lannister
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – dwarf, Tywin's son; freed by Bronn's victory, now in the mountains of the Vale with Bronn, surrounded by Stone Crows to whom he offered "the Vale of Arryn". Bronn – sellsword, his hired sword. Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – fled the capital, said to be at Casterly Rock.
  • Stone Crows (hill clan): Gunthor son of Gurn (chief), Shagga son of Dolf, Conn, Torrek, Jaggot.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jon Snow, Samwell Tarly (fat, bookish recruit), Pyp, Grenn, Toad, Halder (recruits), Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms), Jeor Mormont "the Old Bear" (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon (blind, aged), Chett, Clydas, Yoren, Benjen (missing).
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife, owns a dragon egg. Viserys – her cruel brother, "the dragon". Khal Drogo – Dothraki warlord husband. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her. Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister.

Places
