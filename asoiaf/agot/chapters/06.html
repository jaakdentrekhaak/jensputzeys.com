Chapter 6 – Catelyn

After the feast, a hidden letter from Lysa Arryn accuses the Lannisters of murdering Jon Arryn, and Ned and Catelyn decide that he will go south as Hand, with Catelyn staying behind and Jon being sent to the Wall.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, Warden of the North. Had planned to refuse the Hand, but after Lysa's letter has agreed to go south, reluctantly ("the south is a nest of adders"). Fears it: his own father went south at a king's summons and never returned. Plans to leave in about a fortnight, taking Sansa and Arya; leaving Catelyn to govern the north. Still refuses to speak of Jon's mother. Loved Robert like a brother and cannot imagine him doing harm.
  • Catelyn Stark (née Tully) – POV; Ned's wife, of Riverrun; wed him fifteen years ago in the sept at Riverrun, having been betrothed at twelve to his elder brother Brandon. Pushed Ned to accept the Hand; read Lysa's letter in their childhood private language and burned it. Will rule Winterfell in Ned's stead and hopes for another child. Coldly resents Jon (never forgave Ned for keeping him); insisted "Jon must go". Dreads losing Ned, the girls and Bran.
  • Jon Snow – Ned's bastard, 14. Does not yet know he is to be sent to the Wall; Ned will tell him himself and wants him to enjoy his last days. Ned will not name Jon's mother; Winterfell gossip told Catelyn that Ned slew Ser Arthur Dayne in single combat and carried his sword to Dayne's sister Lady Ashara Dayne at Starfall. Ned angrily forbade her to ask about Jon again, so what the gossip implies is unconfirmed.
  • Robb Stark – 14, heir; Ned wants him to learn to rule under Catelyn and Luwin. There is bad feeling between him and Prince Joffrey.
  • Sansa Stark – 11; to marry Joffrey and go south. Arya Stark – to go south to learn court ways.
  • Bran Stark – 7, loves to climb; Ned wants him to go south and befriend the young princes. Catelyn begged to keep him home; Ned has not changed his mind. Rickon Stark – youngest (3), to stay.
  • Benjen Stark – Ned's brother in the Night's Watch; has spoken to Luwin about Jon's wish to join and will be asked by Ned to take him.
  • Rickard, Brandon and Lyanna Stark (all dead) – Ned's father, eldest brother and sister; Howland Reed (mentioned) – crannogman present when Lyanna died.
  • Direwolf pups – one per child. Catelyn recalls the dead direwolf in the snow with a broken antler lodged in her throat.
Winterfell household
  • Maester Luwin – small, grey counsellor and healer, delivered all Catelyn's children. Found Lysa's letter hidden in a false-bottomed box that held a lens from Myr; helped persuade Ned to go south and proposed that Jon take the black. Ned asks him to advise Catelyn and teach Robb.
  • Desmond – guard at the chamber door. Theon Greyjoy – Ned's ward. Hullen – master of horse; Harwin – his son. Jory Cassel – captain of the guard. Ser Rodrik – household knight; reports the Robb–Joffrey tension. Old Nan – storyteller.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Jon Arryn's widow, Catelyn's sister; sent a secret letter (sealed with the Arryn moon-and-falcon) saying Jon Arryn was murdered by the Lannisters, "the queen", and implying she has more than suspicion. In the Eyrie with her son Robert Arryn (6, sickly Lord of the Eyrie). Jon Arryn (dead) – former Hand, Ned's and Robert's foster father.
  • Edmure Tully 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – Catelyn's sibling, remembered from Riverrun childhood. Brynden – her uncle, Knight of the Gate. Hoster Tully – her father, Lord of Riverrun.
Royal court
  • King Robert Baratheon – wants Ned as Hand and has offered Joffrey for Sansa. Said by Catelyn to have fathered many bastards, none seen at court because of the queen.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – named by Lysa's letter as having a hand in Jon Arryn's death. Prince Joffrey – Robert's heir, Sansa's intended. Prince Tommen and Princess Myrcella – the younger royal children.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister ("Kingslayer") – the queen's twin. Tyrion Lannister ("the Imp") – their dwarf brother. Lord Tywin Lannister (mentioned) – their father at Casterly Rock. Sandor Clegane – burned-faced rider in the royal party. Maester Pycelle – royal maester who tended Jon Arryn. Kingsguard – the king's white-cloaked guards.
  • Ser Arthur Dayne 🆕 new this chapter (dead, mentioned) – the "Sword of the Morning", deadliest of Aerys's seven Kingsguard, slain by Ned in single combat. Ashara Dayne 🆕 new this chapter (mentioned) – his tall, fair, violet-eyed sister at Starfall.
  • Mad King Aerys II and Rhaegar Targaryen (dead) – the overthrown king and his son; Balon Greyjoy (mentioned) – self-styled King of the Iron Islands, beaten nine years ago.
The Night's Watch
  • Understrength. Benjen Stark; Will, Gared, Ser Waymar Royce (prologue rangers; Royce killed by the Others, Will seized by his reanimated body, Gared's fate unknown); Mormont (commander); Maester Aemon.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce. Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall. Wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen (13, given by her brother Viserys, who calls himself king, to Khal Drogo). Illyrio Mopatis – fat Pentoshi magister hosting them. Khal Drogo – huge undefeated Dothraki warlord. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight who sold poachers to slavers.

Places

Places
  • Winterfell – Stark seat, built over hot springs whose heated water runs through its walls and glass gardens; Catelyn's chambers are very hot, which Ned dislikes. Its maester has an observatory.
  • King's Landing – capital, to which Ned must go as Hand.
  • The Wall / Castle Black – where Jon will be sent.
  • Riverrun, the Eyrie / the Vale, Storm's End, Casterly Rock – seats of Tully, Arryn, Baratheon, Lannister. The Trident, Dorne, Iron Islands – river of Robert's victory; southern land; Greyjoy home.
  • Starfall 🆕 new this chapter – Dayne castle on the shores of the Summer Sea, home of Ashara Dayne. Myr – Free City famed for its lenscrafters (source of the lens).
  • Pentos, Vaes Dothrak, Dragonstone – Free City; Dothraki city; Targaryen island seat.
