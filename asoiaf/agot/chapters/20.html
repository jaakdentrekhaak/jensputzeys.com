Chapter 20 – Eddard

Ned reaches King's Landing and attends his first small council meeting, where he learns of the king's costly tournament and the Crown's huge debts, then is secretly led by Littlefinger to a brothel where Catelyn tells him of the attack on Bran and the dagger.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – POV; Lord of Winterfell, now arrived at the Red Keep as Hand of the King; given Jon Arryn's old chambers in the Tower of the Hand. Tired, uneasy among councillors he thinks are "flatterers and fools"; realizes here he is only "first among equals". After hearing Catelyn, believes Tyrion's dagger was meant to kill Bran, refuses to drop the matter, and fears the queen (or even Robert) may be behind it though he will not believe the king knew. Sends Catelyn home; orders defenses readied in the North (see Catelyn). Plans to learn the truth about Jon Arryn's death and then go to Robert. Haunted by guilt over killing Sansa's wolf and by the butcher's boy's death.
  • Catelyn Stark – Ned's wife; secretly in King's Landing, hidden in Littlefinger's brothel. Her left hand is scarred and two fingers stiff from grabbing the assassin's blade. She told Ned everything (library tower fire, the dagger, Varys, the guardsmen, Littlefinger); fears Varys has some "dark art" of knowing things. Ned orders her back to Winterfell with Ser Rodrik, to relay: Helman Tallhart and Galbart Glover to raise 100 bowmen each and fortify Moat Cailin; Lord Manderly to strengthen White Harbor; a careful watch on Theon Greyjoy (his father's fleet may matter in a war). Has accepted Littlefinger as "a brother I'd thought lost".
  • Sansa Stark – grieves for her dead wolf Lady, blames Arya, said it should have been Nymeria that died; cries herself to sleep. Arya Stark – broods silently, mourning Mycah; her wolf Nymeria is gone. Both daughters are in the Red Keep; Ned has told Jory to keep Arya from exploring.
  • Bran Stark – 7, crippled but alive; Ned thinks his direwolf (Summer) saved his life. Robb (14, heir, acting master of Winterfell), Rickon (3), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, Night's Watch). Brandon Stark (Ned's brother, dead ~14 years; Littlefinger carries a scar from him), Lyanna (Ned's dead sister; Ned recalls her pleading), Rickard (Ned's dead father), Howland Reed (mentioned).
Winterfell household
  • Ser Rodrik Cassel – household knight, Catelyn's escort, clean-shaven and in rough brown clothes; to ride with her back to Winterfell. Vayon Poole – Ned's steward, settling the girls in. Jory Cassel – captain of Ned's guard; keeps watch on the girls. Septa Mordane – the girls' septa. Hullen – master of horse. At Winterfell: Maester Luwin, Hodor (simple stableboy), Mikken (smith), Hallis Mollen (captain of the guard), Old Nan, Theon Greyjoy (Ned's ward; to be watched). Mycah – Arya's friend, the butcher's boy, dead.
House Tully / House Arryn
  • Lysa Arryn – Catelyn's sister, widow of Jon Arryn (dead, the former Hand; Catelyn has told Petyr her suspicions about his death); Robert Arryn – her sickly son; Edmure, Brynden, Hoster Tully – Catelyn's brother, uncle and father.
Royal court (small council, House Baratheon, House Lannister)
  • Petyr Baelish, "Littlefinger" – master of coin; smirking, mocking, insolent toward Ned; says he knew Brandon "rather too well" and bears a token of his esteem. Owns the brothel where Catelyn hides and led Ned there via a secret door and cliff niches. Promises to help Ned find the truth and says he "holds Varys in the palm of his hand"; advises Ned to drop the dagger in the river since Tyrion will say it was lost or stolen. Admits the Crown owes over six million gold dragons (the Lannisters ~3 million, also Lord Tyrell, the Iron Bank of Braavos, Tyroshi cartels, the Faith). Claims to be sentimental and wicked only by reputation.
  • Lord Varys, "the Spider" – eunuch master of whisperers; powdered, flowery-scented; Ned likes him least; knew of Ned's troubles on the road.
  • Grand Maester Pycelle – old, bald, many-linked chain; tires easily; convened the council; served Jon Arryn.
  • Lord Renly Baratheon – King Robert's youngest brother, Lord of Storm's End and a small councillor; looks like a younger Robert; wears rich clothes, laughs a lot; delivered the king's order for a tournament.
  • King Robert Baratheon – still on the road, said to be drunk in a huge wheelhouse; orders a great tourney in Ned's honor (40,000 dragons to the champion, 20,000 runner-up, 20,000 melee, 10,000 archery) which Ned thinks is folly. He once spoke of sending hired knives after the Targaryen princess; Ned remembers him turning away from Rhaegar's infant son's killing and from the butcher's boy's killing. Told Ned in the crypts he is surrounded by flatterers and fools.
  • Stannis Baratheon – Robert's elder brother and a councillor, gone to Dragonstone. Ser Barristan Selmy – Lord Commander of the Kingsguard, riding with the king.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister (said "We have a wolf" softly), Prince Joffrey (crown prince, recovering from Nymeria's bite), Tommen, Myrcella (younger royal children), Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" (Kingsguard, her twin), Sandor "the Hound" (Joffrey's bodyguard), Ser Ilyn Payne (King's Justice; Littlefinger says accusing the king means dancing with him), Tywin Lannister (Lannister head, the Crown's biggest creditor), Balon Greyjoy (mentioned; Theon's father), Mad King Aerys II (died on his throne room floor), Rhaegar (dead). The Lannister party is behind the Starks on the road.
  • Ser Aron Santagar (Rodrik spoke to him; Varys learned of it anyway).
Lords of the North (mentioned)
  • Helman Tallhart 🆕 new this chapter and Galbart Glover 🆕 new this chapter – northern lords Ned orders to raise a hundred bowmen each and fortify Moat Cailin; Lord Manderly 🆕 new this chapter – lord of White Harbor, to strengthen its defences.
The Night's Watch (Castle Black)
  • Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Bowen Marsh (officer), Cotter Pyke (commands Eastwatch), Ser Alliser Thorne (master-at-arms), Donal Noye (one-armed armorer), recruits (Grenn, Todder, Pyp, etc.), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, a recruit), Benjen (First Ranger, overdue), Yoren, Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others in the prologue; Castle Black thinks him missing). Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – the queen's dwarf brother, a guest at Castle Black; owner of the dragonbone-hilted dagger, now (privately) suspected by Ned and Catelyn.
Beyond the Wall
  • The Others – pale, ice-eyed killers of Royce; Mance Rayder – King-beyond-the-Wall; wildlings – northern raiders.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Pentos and Dothraki
  • Daenerys (13, wed to the Dothraki warlord Khal Drogo), Viserys (her brother, styles himself king), Illyrio Mopatis (Pentoshi magister), Ser Jorah Mormont (exile), handmaids Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah, bloodriders Haggo, Cohollo, Qotho.
