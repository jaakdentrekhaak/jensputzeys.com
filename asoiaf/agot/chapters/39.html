Chapter 39 – Eddard

Ned wakes after six days, dreaming of an old fight at a tower, to learn the Kingslayer has fled and then argues with King Robert, who strikes Cersei, refuses justice against Jaime, and forces the Hand's badge back on him.

Characters

House Stark (Winterfell)
  • Eddard (Ned) Stark – Lord of Winterfell, bedridden in the Tower of the Hand with a splinted, plastered leg; woke after six days and seven nights. Told Robert his wife acted on his command and that he went to the brothel to see Robert's daughter Barra. Wants to bring Jaime to justice; Robert refused, ordered Catelyn to free Tyrion, and handed back the silver Hand's badge, forbidding Ned to leave. Wants his daughters kept safe ("I fear this is only the beginning"). Haunted by a dream of an old fight at a tower in Dorne (seven of his friends against three Kingsguard knights, Lyanna dying in a bed of blood, a promise he made her); only he and the crannogman Howland Reed lived; he pulled the tower down and built eight cairns.
  • Alyn – Ned's man in King's Landing, now acting captain of his guard; sent Jory, Wyl and Heward's bodies north with the silent sisters; doubled the guard. Jory Cassel – dead, long-time guard captain; his father Martyn Cassel died in the old fight. Vayon Poole – Winterfell's steward, with Ned in King's Landing; tends him.
  • Sansa (elder daughter, betrothed to Joffrey) and Arya (younger daughter) – with him in the Red Keep; Sansa prays quietly; Arya has not spoken since the attack and is full of anger. Catelyn – Ned's wife, at the Eyrie holding Tyrion. Robb (fifteen, ruling Winterfell, weighing calling the banners), Bran (eight, crippled, attacked in the wolfswood but saved), Rickon (youngest, small), Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, at the Wall), Benjen (Ned's brother, missing); direwolves Summer (Bran's) and Grey Wind (Robb's). Lyanna – Ned's dead sister, once betrothed to Robert.
  • Old friends from Ned's past (dream): Howland Reed (crannogman, the other survivor), Martyn Cassel, Theo Wull, Ethan Glover (Brandon's squire), Ser Mark Ryswell, Lord Dustin; Ser Willem Darry – named as having fled to Dragonstone with the Targaryen queen and Viserys.
  • Winterfell household: Theon Greyjoy (Ned's smirking ward), Maester Luwin, Osha (captive wildling woman), Hallis Mollen (guard captain), Joseth (stableman), Hodor (simple giant), Old Nan, Septa Mordane (the girls' septa), Mikken (smith).
Royal court (House Lannister and the Baratheons)
  • King Robert Baratheon – drunk, irritable, tired of talk; wants peace with the Lannisters; struck Cersei for mocking him, then admitted he felt "sad and scared"; broods that Rhaegar, whom he killed, "still won" with Lyanna. Hunting in the kingswood tomorrow; insists Ned stay as Hand and refuses to discuss the Targaryen girl.
  • Queen Cersei Lannister – proud, cold, furious; blames the Starks; backhanded by Robert (will wear the bruise "as a badge of honor"). Ned watched her go still when he mentioned Barra.
  • Ser Jaime Lannister "the Kingslayer" – Cersei's twin and Tyrion's brother; fled the city after the ambush, rumored to have ridden to Casterly Rock; Robert says Jaime's men killed three of Ned's and Ned's five of his. Tregar – Jaime's captain, died of Ned's blow.
  • Ser Meryn Trant – Kingsguard knight, one of the queen's men in white armor; escorted Cersei out. Petyr Baelish "Littlefinger" – says he left to fetch the gold cloaks before the fighting. Grand Maester Pycelle – says Ned's leg will heal clean. Tywin Lannister (Cersei's father, Casterly Rock), Joffrey (12, heir), Varys (eunuch spymaster), Renly and Stannis (Robert's brothers), Ser Barristan Selmy (Kingsguard commander), Loras Tyrell (the Knight of Flowers), the Cleganes (Sandor "the Hound" and Gregor) – at court. Barra – Robert's baby bastard (mother a teen at Chataya's brothel). Rhaegar and Aerys – dead Targaryens (Robert killed Rhaegar; Jaime killed Aerys). Ser Arthur Dayne (Sword of the Morning, greatsword Dawn), Ser Oswell Whent, Ser Gerold Hightower (White Bull, Lord Commander) – Aerys's Kingsguard, killed at the tower in Ned's dream.
Catelyn's party and captives (at the Eyrie)
  • Tyrion Lannister "the Imp" – Lannister dwarf, held in a sky cell, beaten; demanded trial by combat; his champion is Bronn (sellsword), who volunteered. Catelyn Stark – holds Tyrion as prisoner, opposed Lysa's treatment. Marillion (singer), Ser Rodrik Cassel (Winterfell's master-at-arms, wounded), Ser Willis Wode (Harrenhal hedge knight).
House Tully / House Arryn / the Vale
  • Lysa Arryn (Lady of the Eyrie, Catelyn's sister), Robert Arryn (sickly boy lord), Ser Vardis Egen (captain of Jon Arryn's guard, her champion in the coming duel), Mord (cruel gaoler), Ser Brynden "the Blackfish" Tully (Catelyn's uncle, Knight of the Gate), Lord Nestor Royce (High Steward of the Vale) and heir Ser Albar Royce, Ser Lyn Corbray (vain young knight), Lord Hunter and Lady Waynwood (lords of the Vale), Ser Donnel Waynwood (young knight), Mya Stone (mule guide), Maester Colemon (Eyrie maester), Hoster Tully (Catelyn's father, Riverrun), Edmure (her brother); Jon Arryn (dead Hand).
The Night's Watch
  • Jon Snow (Ned's bastard, new brother), Samwell Tarly (fat gentle recruit), Yoren (black brother, recruiter), Jeor Mormont (Lord Commander), Maester Aemon, Ser Alliser Thorne (hostile master-at-arms), recruits Pyp, Grenn, Halder, Rast; Benjen (missing), Ser Waymar Royce (killed by the Others), Gared (executed deserter).
Beyond the Wall / legend
  • The Others (cold dead killers of legend, seen in the prologue), children of the forest (vanished folk of legend), Mance Rayder (wildling leader, mentioned), wildlings.
House Targaryen (exiles) / Dothraki
  • Daenerys Targaryen "Khaleesi" – exiled princess, Drogo's pregnant wife; at Vaes Dothrak she struck her brother Viserys across the face with a medallion belt after he abused her; keeps a green dragon egg.
  • Viserys Targaryen – her proud, cruel brother, the self-styled king ("the dragon"); mocked by the Dothraki; cut on the cheek, impatient for the army Drogo promised.
  • Khal Drogo – her husband, Dothraki warlord with forty thousand riders; at Vaes Dothrak. Ser Jorah Mormont – exiled knight advising her (son of Jeor Mormont); hates Ned Stark. Cohollo, Haggo, Qotho – Drogo's bloodriders; Aggo, Rakharo – her khas; Irri, Jhiqui, Doreah – her handmaids; Illyrio Mopatis – Pentoshi magister; dosh khaleen – crone-prophetesses of Vaes Dothrak.
